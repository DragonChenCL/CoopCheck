# Evidence-led content rebuild - November 5, 2026

## Scope and outcomes that can actually be claimed

This is an editorial and product-usability change, not an indexing win. 137 of 142 HTML files were changed. Six flock guides and three footprint guides have replacement decision-led bodies; six state pages have source-linked comparison matrices; About has a replacement methodology/ownership statement. All 100 city profiles gain an editable, source-aware local enquiry worksheet, with field-level uncertainty retained. Other changed pages receive focused additions or corrections, not an unsupported claim of complete fresh research.

## Measured difference

On the six flock guides (15 pairings), distinct exact five-word fragment overlap relative to the smaller set decreased from 46.40-49.47% to 13.26-14.36%. Main text only, retaining numbers, excluding script/style/SVG/noscript. Words decreased from 673-699 to 584-625. This is a reproducible text metric, not Google's classifier, a quality score or evidence of indexing causation. Full pairs and baseline commit are in `audits/content-rebuild-2026-11-05.json`.

The new decisions cover cleanout reach, four-to-six expansion, outside-versus-inside dimensions, equal-area fencing differences, shed conversions and service-access/workload. Examples are hypothetical and labelled; product listings are not passed off as field measurements.
