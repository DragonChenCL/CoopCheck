## Why this specific target
- Existing article: https://www.backyardchickens.com/articles/portland-oregon-chicken-ordinance.61358/
- The public BYC article was published in 2012, last updated February 2012, and still states "Three or less without permit" with historic code references.
- Current Portland §13.40.020(A)(1) amended by Ordinance 192002, effective January 10, 2025, allows up to four chickens/small fowl on any lot and up to six on lots >=10,000 square feet; up to four extra under 12 weeks and separate agriculture-zoning exceptions. §13.40.020(A)(3) restricts roosters outside qualifying agriculture.
- Current Portland §13.40.040(D) locates structures in livestock facilities at least three feet from side and rear property lines and ten feet from front, with additional separation rules on multi-unit lots.
- Primary sources: https://www.portland.gov/code/13/40/020 and https://www.portland.gov/code/13/40/040

I noticed that your Portland, Oregon ordinance article (https://www.backyardchickens.com/articles/portland-oregon-chicken-ordinance.61358/) still reports "Three or less without permit" and cites older city code sections. That information appears outdated compared with Portland's current published municipal code.

Portland City Code §13.40.020(A)(1), as amended effective January 10, 2025, permits up to four chickens or similar small domestic fowl on any lot, and up to six on lots of at least 10,000 square feet. Separate provisions govern young birds, agricultural-use lots, and roosters. The city's §13.40.040(D) also sets livestock facility structure setbacks.

Official references:
