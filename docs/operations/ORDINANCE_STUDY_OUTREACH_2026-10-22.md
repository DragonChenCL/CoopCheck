## What is actually evidenced
- 100 selected municipal profiles in 11 states. Convenience sample, NOT representative of all U.S. municipalities.
- 88 profiles meet the project's source-reviewed threshold; 12 remain pending and have blank legal claims in CSV.
- Of 88 source-reviewed profiles, 71 record specific source evidence for all four tracked fields.
- Recorded source checks across 88: flock limit 84; rooster rule 81; permit/building context 78; placement 83.
- Five examples freshly compared with government pages on Oct 22: Portland (4/6 depending lot size), San Diego (5/15/25 depending placement), Denver (8 mixed birds, $25 permit), Chandler (2024 ordinance revised 5 to 6), Seattle (typically 8 fowl; no roosters).
- No claim of nationally representative statistics or legal approval; this is a curated ordinance evidence inventory.
