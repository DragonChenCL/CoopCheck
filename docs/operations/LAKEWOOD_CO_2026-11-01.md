# Lakewood, Colorado — poultry law verification
**Checked:** 2026-11-01  
**Status:** HOLD / limited-scope only. Do not publish numerical hen limits, rooster permissions, or coop setbacks as current law until the operative Title 17 text is confirmed.

## What the official sources establish

1. The City's **City Codes & Laws** page explicitly says Title 17 (the Zoning Ordinance) is not available in Municode and directs readers to the City's Zoning page for the full ordinance. It also warns that the online Municipal Code can take time to reflect newly passed ordinances.  
   https://www.lakewoodco.gov/Local-Government/Departments/City-Clerks-Office/City-Codes-Laws

2. The City's **R-2026-09** resolution called the April 7, 2026 special municipal election on a referendum concerning **Ordinance O-2025-28**, which replaced Articles 1, 2, 4 and 5 of Title 17.  
   https://www.lakewoodco.gov/Local-Government/Departments/City-Clerks-Office/City-Council-Ordinances-Resolutions/2026-Resolutions/R-2026-09

3. The official April 7 sample ballot identifies Question 2 as the referendum on Ordinance O-2025-28.  
   https://www.lakewoodco.gov/files/assets/public/v/1/city-clerks-office/elections/sample-election-ballot-lakewood-april-7-2026-special-election-002.pdf

4. The City's 2026 ordinance list records **O-2026-04 as FAILED**. It was a proposed City Council repeal of O-2025-28, not the voter referendum result. Do not confuse the failed council ordinance with the separate Question 2 election outcome.  
   https://www.lakewoodco.gov/Local-Government/Departments/City-Clerks-Office/City-Council-Ordinances-Resolutions/2026-Ordinances

5. The City's current **Zoning Permits** page lists a Small Animal Permit application for small animals including poultry, pigeons, hens and dwarf goats. The **Zoning Review Forms** page separately describes a Small Animal Permit for chickens (hens), ducks, dwarf goats and apiaries. These pages establish that a permit pathway is currently advertised; they do not establish flock limits, rooster rules, or setbacks.  
   https://www.lakewoodco.gov/Local-Government/Departments/Sustainability-and-Community-Development/Zoning-Permits  
   https://www.lakewoodco.gov/Local-Government/Departments/Sustainability-and-Community-Development/Zoning-Review-Forms

6. The April 7 election result reported by local media showed Question 2 leading for repeal, but the source at hand is not the City's certified final canvass. It is not enough to declare which version of Article 4 is legally operative.  
   https://www.denvergazette.com/2026/04/07/lakewood-voters-reject-citys-density-zoning-changes/
