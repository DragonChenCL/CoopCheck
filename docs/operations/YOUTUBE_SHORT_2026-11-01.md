## Description
A coop can fit your backyard and still fail a setback rule. Some cities measure from the property line; others use a neighboring dwelling or a different reference point. The coop and run may also be treated differently. Before you buy or build, check the current rules for your address and confirm what the setback is measured from.

CoopCheck is a free planning aid—not a permit approval, parcel survey, or legal opinion. Local rules vary.
