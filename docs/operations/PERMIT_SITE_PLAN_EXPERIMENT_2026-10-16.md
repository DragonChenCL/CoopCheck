## Why this one page, source check 2026-10-16

Municipalities explicitly require site-plan preparation; keywords and demand volumes are hypotheses:
- Grand Rapids MI https://www.grandrapidsmi.gov/grow-and-thrive/development-center/permits/residential-building-permits/backyard-chicken-permit/ : measured site plan with property lines, buildings, fences, coop, chicken yard; supplemental plans/product specs; landlord permission if renting.
- Blaine MN https://www.blainemn.gov/2997/Chicken-Permit : coop, pen/run plan, distances to lot lines and neighboring homes, dimensions, HOA rules when relevant. Official page contains conflicting fee amounts ($65 vs $45 in different sections); worksheet avoids a fee claim.
- Marietta GA https://www.mariettaga.gov/1108/Chickens : accurately scaled property sketch with existing/proposed structures, coops and fences and their lot-line distances; separate coop building permit.
