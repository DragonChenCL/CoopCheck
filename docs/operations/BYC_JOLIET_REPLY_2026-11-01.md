## Suggested reply

One caution before you build an amendment around an old PDF: I found a Joliet Granicus document discussing Chapter 6, §6-10, but the document itself is labelled **“Proposed: Draft: November 17, 2016.”** I would not cite that as the final adopted text.

The City's official Municipal Codes & Ordinances page links to the current Municipal Code, and the City Clerk's Office says it maintains the official City Code and ordinance records. I'd ask the Clerk for the current consolidated Chapter 6, §6-10, plus the current Zoning Ordinance provision it cross-references (the old draft refers to §47-3.9(c)). That will tell you exactly what needs to change—and whether the animal-control and zoning sections both need an amendment.

For a workable proposal, I'd ask the City to define the rules in one place: maximum hens by lot type/size, rooster treatment, permit requirements, coop/run placement, sanitation and nuisance standards, and any transition rule for existing flocks. I would avoid quoting a hen count or setback until the Clerk confirms the operative text.
