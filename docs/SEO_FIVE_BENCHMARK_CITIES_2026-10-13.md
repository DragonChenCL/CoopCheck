# Five CoopCheck benchmark city guides — 2026-10-13

Five existing city URLs were upgraded with specific regulatory decision tables, hypothetical scenarios, parcel planning checklists, linked primary sources and explicit limits on legal conclusions.

- Portland: 4/6 small fowl depending on lot area; 10 sq ft per adult bird, 3/10-ft property-line setbacks, multi-unit provisions.
- Austin: City guidance supports residential chicken keeping and 30 ft between two-or-more-bird enclosures and adjacent residences/businesses. Numeric citywide hen cap, roosters and permits remain unverified in the cited overview.
- Arlington: up to 4 without added lot/distance criteria in official guidance; larger flocks have 50-ft dwelling/acreage tiers, and published bands overlap at exactly 10 birds.
- Denver: up to 8 hens/ducks total, $25 one-time FPA permit, 16 sq ft of permeable area per bird and location on rear 50% of zone lot.
- Seattle: up to 8 domestic fowl, no roosters, 10 ft from adjacent residential structures; larger-lot exceptions require qualifying use, not simply acreage.

All figures are explained as source-specific screening conditions, **not** a parcel-specific compliance decision. No fake customer cases or approvals are claimed.
