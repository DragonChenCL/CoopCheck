CONTACT & SUPPORT

We’re here to help.

For questions about CoopCheck tools, local-law corrections or a paid report, email our support inbox. Please do not send payment card details, passwords, or other sensitive personal information.

Contact CoopCheck

support@serunio.com

Include a short description of your issue and the relevant page URL. For a purchase concern, include the order reference if available but never your full card number.

Open email ↗

What to include

  • Rule corrections: city/state, the claim in question, and an official local-government source link.
  • Planning tool issues: the URL, browser and what you expected to happen.
  • Paid-report or refund requests: approximate purchase date, order reference if available, and the problem you encountered.
  • Privacy requests: explain what information or email correspondence your request concerns.

Important note

Support can help with CoopCheck features and research corrections, but cannot approve poultry keeping, confirm property boundaries, determine zoning, or provide legal or structural engineering advice. Ask your local authority for official decisions.
