    Need help deciding bird count first? Use our free coop size calculator, or browse a worked example for 6 hens before changing the dimensions here.

    CoopCheck uses a 4 sq ft indoor space per bird and 10 sq ft outdoor run per bird planning target for typical adult laying hens with run access. These are simplified, deliberately conservative space targets derived from university poultry guidance, not municipal legal minima. Actual needs depend on breed, climate, confinement, age, ventilation and local restrictions.

    As a starting point, the tool estimates approximately one 12-inch-square nest box per four birds and 9 inches of total roost length per bird. The diagram is a schematic: it does not guarantee these components physically fit for every custom shape or flock.

    Before turning a concept into a build

    • Confirm local flock-size, roosters, zoning and building-permit requirements. A source-reviewed city rule is not parcel approval.
    • Check measured property lines, setbacks, existing buildings, easements and HOA restrictions.
    • Work out secure roof, predator-resistant enclosure, ventilation without drafts, drainage and cleanout access.
    • Have a qualified builder review the detailed foundation, framing, doors, roof and attachment loads if you intend to build.

    Our existing map-based setback planner helps you sketch an approximate coop location, while our setback guide explains why city boundary distances differ. Neither substitutes for local officials or a survey.
