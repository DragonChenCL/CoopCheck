Privacy Policy

This policy explains the data involved when you visit CoopCheck, use its planning tools, contact support or purchase an optional report. Last updated: October 20, 2026.

1. Information you provide

When using a map-based tool, you may enter an address or draw approximate lot, house and coop shapes. You may also contact support@serunio.com, in which case we receive the message and information you choose to include. Purchase workflows use an order or project reference to associate report access.

2. Browser storage

The planner uses your browser’s local storage to save sketches, project identifiers and report-access records so they can be restored later on the same browser. The free 2D generator may also save configuration locally. Clearing your browser’s site data can remove locally stored work or access records. Export a copy of any work you need to keep.

3. Analytics and technical information

CoopCheck uses Google Analytics to understand visits and interactions, such as pages opened or tools used. This can involve cookies or similar identifiers, approximate location inferred from network information, device/browser details, and interaction events. Our hosting and security providers may process IP addresses and request logs for delivery, security and abuse prevention.

4. Mapping and third parties

Interactive maps and address search use Mapbox. Map requests and search queries may be sent to Mapbox, which handles them under its own policies. External reference links send you to third-party sites with their own privacy practices.

5. Payment and report access

Optional report payments are handled using an external checkout flow provided through our payment gateway and third-party payment processor. Payment and order information may be processed by those services; please review the checkout provider’s terms and privacy notice. CoopCheck stores limited report-access identifiers in your browser and communicates with the gateway to verify access. Do not email us card numbers.

6. Purposes, retention and requests

Information is used to operate the site, provide mapping and report access, respond to requests, measure performance and protect the service. Retention varies by the category of data and the providers involved; we do not promise a fixed period for all third-party logs. You can clear local browser data yourself. For access, deletion or other privacy inquiries regarding data under our control, email support@serunio.com. We may need reasonable details to identify the records at issue.

7. Changes

We may update this notice as services evolve. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new update date. Contact support with questions.
