Four hens: should you buy a 4 x 4 or a 4 x 6 coop?

Compare two four-hen layouts, subtract blocked floor, and test cleaning access before buying. Includes a printable measurement task and separate run figures.

A small flock exposes a common buying mistake: a room can meet a floor-area number and still be impossible to clean. Choose between two measured interiors, not two advertised bird counts.

A compact room versus room to move

For four adult laying hens with daily outdoor access, the site's 4-square-foot indoor planning target produces 16 sq ft. A clear 4 x 4 ft interior reaches that target exactly. A clear 4 x 6 ft interior supplies 24 sq ft: eight more square feet, before fixtures. Neither result decides ventilation, predator protection or municipal permission.

Subtract only floor the birds genuinely cannot use. A feeder suspended over accessible ground is not automatically a two-square-foot loss; a sealed cupboard on the floor is. Measure the obstruction rather than subtracting a standard allowance.

The reach test is more useful than another capacity label

Before ordering, ask for the cleanout opening's width and height and the distance from that opening to the farthest corner. Mark the proposed floor on the ground with tape. Standing where the opening will be, try reaching every corner with the actual cleaning tool you intend to use. Note any corner that needs a second door or a removable panel.

This is an owner-run fit test, not a claim that CoopCheck tested a particular coop. A photo of a wide open roof does not tell you whether the roof can be lifted once a fence or wall is beside it.

One nest is a starting ratio, not a guarantee of access

The calculator rounds four divided by four to 1 nest box and uses 3 ft of combined perch. Locate the box entrance and egg-collection hatch on the taped sketch. Check whether the hatch can open without blocking a passage or exposing the birds. Do not count a protruding nest box as extra unobstructed sleeping-house floor.

Write these measurements on the seller enquiry

Ask: “Is 4 x 4 the clear enclosed interior or the whole coop-and-run unit? What floor remains after fixed fixtures? Which panels open for cleaning, and what clearance do they require?” Record the reply alongside the model name and specification date. Allocate a separate 40 sq ft run under the outdoor reference.

For birds housed entirely indoors, do not use the 16-square-foot example. OSU's no-outdoor-access range gives 32–40 sq ft for four birds; management and bird-specific needs still require review.
