What is included in the free concept drawing?

  • Dimensioned rectangular layout for combined available coop + run space.
  • Distinct sheltered coop footprint and remaining outdoor run area, without counting the same footprint twice.
  • Illustrative nest box symbols, perch mark and area-target warnings.
  • Local browser export to vector SVG, PNG image or print-to-PDF.

The tool computes geometry on your device; no AI API or paid CAD service is needed.

What is NOT included in a construction-ready plan?

  • Foundations, spans, roof loads, bracing, wind/snow engineering or joist calculations.
  • Fastener schedule, material cut list, lumber shopping quantities and doors that have been physically clearance-checked.
  • Predator-proof mesh specification, drainage design or measured parcel survey.
  • A city permit or automatic zoning approval. Check both the local animal ordinance and the building department.

For actual building, obtain a detailed professionally checked plan tailored to the site. Do not construct solely from a proportional concept sketch.

How to get your printable layout in three steps

Same sheltered and run areas, very different whole-site envelopes

A free concept plan can test whether proposed shapes even fit the property, but it is not a cut list or structural building plan. Take a four-hen illustrative combination: 4 × 4 ft enclosed coop (16 sq ft) and 5 × 8 ft separate run (40 sq ft). Their usable areas total 56 sq ft; that is not the same as their outer bounding rectangle.

For side-by-side placement, width is 4 + 5 = 9 ft, length is the larger of 4 and 8 = 8 ft. For end-to-end placement, depth is 4 + 8 = 12 ft, width is the larger of 4 and 5 = 5 ft. Those results assume two rectangles touch. A real build can require gate clearances, keeper access, drainage, roof projection and legal setbacks, so the actual envelope may be larger.

A five-line measurement sheet to take outside

  1. Measure the available site dimensions from an appropriate, verified property reference; a phone map is not a boundary survey.
  2. Record the enclosed clear indoor footprint, and note nest projections and permanently blocked floor separately.
  3. Record the separate usable run rectangle and every permanent obstruction.
  4. Draw the gate swing, roof overhang, grading and safe cleanout route; do not mistake unused bounding-area cells for usable bird ground.
  5. Check local flock, structure and setback rules in the city source directory before asking a contractor to build.

Oregon State University Extension emphasizes predator protection, secure flooring, ventilation and the management difference between birds with and without outdoor access. Those considerations are outside these two rectangles. Open the downloadable 2D concept tool to try different orientations, then keep the source assumptions with the drawing.

Evidence boundary: A zero-gap bounding rectangle is purely geometric. It is not a safe, lawful or construction-ready plan. Figures are illustrative calculations, not an inspection, animal-welfare certification, local legal minimum, supplier quote or engineered build plan.
