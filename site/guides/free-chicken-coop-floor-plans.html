What is included in the free concept drawing?

  • Dimensioned rectangular layout for combined available coop + run space.
  • Distinct sheltered coop footprint and remaining outdoor run area, without counting the same footprint twice.
  • Illustrative nest box symbols, perch mark and area-target warnings.
  • Local browser export to vector SVG, PNG image or print-to-PDF.

The tool computes geometry on your device; no AI API or paid CAD service is needed.

What is NOT included in a construction-ready plan?

  • Foundations, spans, roof loads, bracing, wind/snow engineering or joist calculations.
  • Fastener schedule, material cut list, lumber shopping quantities and doors that have been physically clearance-checked.
  • Predator-proof mesh specification, drainage design or measured parcel survey.
  • A city permit or automatic zoning approval. Check both the local animal ordinance and the building department.

For actual building, obtain a detailed professionally checked plan tailored to the site. Do not construct solely from a proportional concept sketch.

How to get your printable layout in three steps

  1. Select a flock size and then measure the entire available coop-plus-run rectangle.
  2. Open the free chicken coop plan generator, choose a walk-in, raised or compact concept and verify both indoor and outdoor area targets.
  3. Download SVG/PNG or choose Print / Save PDF. The printout is a planning reference, not a permit drawing.

You can also start with a chicken coop size calculator or check property-line and neighboring-house setbacks before deciding the footprint.
