Chicken run fencing calculator: mesh area & perimeter

Calculate chicken run fence perimeter, wall mesh, optional roof mesh and apron area from your dimensions. Free geometry calculator; no invented material quantities.

Estimate simple rectangular fencing dimensions

Enter the length, width and mesh height of a separate rectangular chicken run. We calculate linear perimeter, wall mesh area, optional overhead mesh and optional outward ground-apron area. These are geometric surface measurements, not a structural cut list or a guaranteed predator-proof materials order.

Illustrative geometry only. Confirm build constraints separately.

Example: 8 × 12 ft run with 6-ft mesh walls

Perimeter = 2 × (8 + 12) = 40 linear ft. Wall mesh (no doors subtracted) = 40 × 6 = 240 sq ft. Optional mesh roof = 8 × 12 = 96 sq ft. A 1-ft-wide continuous apron adds 40 × 1 = 40 sq ft before corner overlaps, cutting waste and seams: total simple surface area 376 sq ft.

Actual mesh roll selection requires overlap, fastener details and specification review. A shared wall with a solid coop changes the geometry.

What this estimate does not include

It does not choose wire gauge, opening size, buried fence depth, roof support, post spacing, footing, doors, latches or labor. Oregon State University Extension recommends overhead covering and buried protective fencing to reduce predator access, but the right barrier depends on the site and local wildlife. Confirm any code rules for fences and animal structures too.

A mesh area number is not permission to skip engineering or supplier-specific material requirements.
