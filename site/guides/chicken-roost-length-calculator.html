Chicken roost length calculator: feet and perch bars

Calculate chicken roost length in feet and estimated number of bars from your flock and bar length. Includes 9-inch-per-hen arithmetic and placement cautions.

Calculate total roost length and bar count

Start with an illustrative 9 inches of combined usable roosting pole per standard chicken. This is within the 5–10 inch range in Oregon State University Extension’s backyard coop guide. Example: 8 hens and 4-ft roost bars

8 hens × 9 inches = 72 inches, or 6 ft combined. Two four-foot bars give 8 ft of nominal combined linear space, but they still must fit with adequate separation and clear access. Bar length alone does not measure safety, height or overcrowding.

For 12 standard hens, 12 × 9 inches = 9 ft; with four-foot bars, three bars add to 12 ft on this simplistic calculation.

Placement and breed limitations

OSU Extension describes rounded-edge 1–2-inch wood poles and about 10 inches between poles, with typical poles about 2 ft above the floor; other breeds need different heights. Penn State Extension cautions about heavy/meat breeds roosting too high. Avoid placing the roost so droppings fall into nest boxes or feeders.

These figures do not include framing load, wall fixings, bird-to-wall spacing, drop height, circulation or veterinary assessment.
