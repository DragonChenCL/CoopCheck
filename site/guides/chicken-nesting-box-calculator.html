Chicken nesting box calculator: how many per hen?

Calculate how many nesting boxes you need for 2–100 laying hens. One 12-inch nest for about four hens, plus layout and placement advice from Extension.

How many nesting boxes for your hens?

Oregon State University Extension suggests one nest box for about every four laying hens. Boxes can be shared, so a flock of six hens does not need six private boxes. Enter your adult hen count below and round up to the next whole box. A typical starting interior size per standard-hen nest is 12 × 12 × 12 inches.

Box placement matters more than extra count

Give birds a quieter laying area that is accessible for egg collection and cleaning. OSU Extension advises against placing boxes directly on the ground and says nests should be lower than the roosting poles, so birds are less likely to sleep in them. A sloped top discourages sleeping above nest boxes.

If you keep heavy breeds or special breeds, adjust physical box size and access instead of treating 12 inches as an exact build specification.
