A 6 x 8 coop: which measurement limits the flock?

A marketed overall footprint may include the outdoor run. Use a 48 sq ft interior with independent run and perch measurements to expose the limiting component.

First establish the measurement

Advertised exterior dimensions are not usable indoor dimensions. A measured clear 6 x 8 ft indoor floor is 48 sq ft; the number “twelve” is only one area calculation, not a full housing assessment.

Compare components without adding unlike areas

Consider a hypothetical room with 48 square feet of accessible floor, an 80-square-foot separate run and six feet of usable perch. Using the site's selected references produces three different checks.

Indoor floor48 / 412 hens

Perch6 ft x 12 / 9 in8 hens

The twelve-hen indoor result does not erase the other shortfalls. Even the lowest comparison count is not a safe-flock certification: ventilation, access, management and municipal rules remain outside these arithmetic checks.

What would change for a twelve-hen plan?

The same references call for a separate 120 sq ft run, nine feet of combined usable perch and three nests. Record the proposed changes on a drawing instead of buying birds on the indoor figure alone. A fixed cupboard would also reduce the 48-square-foot starting area and require recalculation.

Measure actual usable perch lengths after wall clearances and obstructions. Do not infer length from a photograph or count an occupied storage shelf as a roost. Supports and arrangements need their own design review.

Bring a useful question to the city

Describe the proposed animal use, flock, footprint, height, utilities and distances to the boundaries or neighboring buildings named in the local rule. Ask which approvals apply to that proposal. A generic “do small sheds need a permit?” answer might not address keeping birds or changing a structure's use.

The permit worksheet organizes those inputs; it is not a permit-ready engineered drawing.

Keep the conclusion narrow

Write “48 sq ft of measured usable interior; other components pending” when the run or perch evidence is missing. For indoor-only management, do not divide by four: the OSU source uses a different indoor range. Record the assumptions alongside any number sent to a builder or seller.
