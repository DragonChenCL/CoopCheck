How many chickens fit in a 6 × 8 ft coop?

A 6×8 ft coop has 48 sq ft of indoor floor space. At 4 sq ft per standard hen that suggests up to 12 birds as a planning target—before run, ventilation and local rules.

Start with the interior measurement

A measured 6 × 8 ft usable indoor floor equals 48 sq ft. Using the 4-sq-ft-per-standard-hen planning target, that is 12 hens by indoor area alone (floor(48 ÷ 4)). This does not indicate you can legally keep 12 birds or that the space is comfortable for every breed and climate.

What changes with a smaller flock?

At 6×8 ft this is no longer a tiny nesting box: a 48-sq-ft indoor footprint could hold 12 standard hens on the illustration rule, with 120 sq ft of run calculated separately. Taller walls, entry routes and detailed structure remain design decisions.

What the 6 × 8 ft measurement does not tell you

It does not account for a low ceiling, cramped door, unusable shelves, wet litter, blocked ventilation, breed differences or time spent confined. Verify the interior dimensions rather than an advertised exterior retail size. The run requires its own dimensions and protection.

For example, adding a 6 × 8 ft secure run supplies only 48 sq ft outdoors, compared with 120 sq ft as the illustration for 12 standard hens. The same size rectangle repeated outdoors is not automatically enough.

How to read these numbers: CoopCheck uses 4 sq ft of indoor floor and 10 sq ft of outdoor run per standard laying hen as illustrative, comparatively generous planning targets for birds with daily run access. Oregon State University Extension gives at least 3 sq ft of indoor space per bird when outdoor access is available; Penn State Extension provides different poultry minimums by breed and management. The 4/10 calculation is not a universal husbandry minimum, municipal code or permit clearance. Increase room for confinement, heavy breeds, climate and flock behavior. Measure usable interior area, not roof overhangs.
