What goes on a chicken coop site plan?

Some municipalities require a measured drawing with your property lines, existing buildings, fences, proposed coop, and covered chicken run. Other offices request coop plans or product sheets, neighbor consent, an animal-care plan, or a separate structure permit. There is no nationwide submission format.

Before drawing: check whether your office needs a sketch, a drawing to scale, an existing survey, or a certified plot plan. A satellite map outline and a backyard fence do not prove legal boundary locations. Do not assume your chicken-keeping permit substitutes for zoning or building approval.

Three examples from official municipal sources

Grand Rapids, Michigan

The city's backyard chicken permit instructions request a measured site plan showing property lines, existing structures, fences, the coop, and the fenced or covered yard. It also asks for coop/enclosure plans or pre-manufactured product information and, for renters, landlord permission. The city provides its own site-plan template. Use that city's required form if you are applying there.

Blaine, Minnesota

The official chicken permit page describes site-plan information including distance from the coop to property lines and nearby residences; applicants should check its current forms and HOA-related requirements. Those are Blaine-specific requirements, not generic US rules.

Marietta, Georgia

The city's chicken information page describes a drawing showing the property's buildings, enclosure and placement. Review the official ordinance and application for any scale and setback details before submission.

From checklist to a practical sketch

  1. Find your city in CoopCheck's source-linked directory; uncovered or partially reviewed legal fields require separate municipal confirmation.
  2. Use the free map sketcher to visualize a proposed lot, house and coop. Its coordinates are approximate and cannot establish a legal property line.
  3. Use the coop concept generator for a preliminary size/shape idea, and the setback guide for measurement questions.
  4. Re-create or supplement the sketch using the official city's required format, with a survey and separately drawn run if needed.

No nationwide chicken coop setback or permit exemption can be inferred from this page. Official municipal review controls whether an application is complete.
