Budget a coop and run using your actual prices

A chicken coop cost calculator should not pretend every US city has the same lumber or labor prices. This worksheet totals your supplier quotes for setup categories and explicitly identifies everything you have not priced. Empty categories are unknown, not free; enter 0 only for a deliberately zero-cost/reused item. The result is a partial or fully priced worksheet subtotal, not an estimated build quote.

What should go in a chicken coop construction budget?

Include the indoor coop and the outdoor run, not just the sleeping box. Look for materials with verified specifications, ventilation openings, secure barriers and weather-safe roofing. Then include site work, tools, delivery, applicable tax, labor and permits. If you already own tools or have reused lumber, record why a category is $0.

Because costs vary sharply by location and build scope, the site deliberately does not publish a made-up national materials bill.

Example: understanding an incomplete quote

If you enter $250 for framing and $120 for fencing but leave every other category blank, the tool shows a $370 priced subtotal and six unknown categories. It does not say the whole coop costs $370. Compare quotes only when the same scope and materials are included, and do not double-count contractor bids containing both supply and labor.

Before committing money, confirm your municipal flock allowance, zoning and building requirements.
