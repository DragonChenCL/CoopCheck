Six questions to answer before ordering a chicken coop

  • What is the exact clear sleeping-floor area? Request inside dimensions after nesting boxes, built-in fixtures and unusable spaces.
  • What is the separate enclosed run area? Ask whether it comes with the purchase, and whether that run is secure and usable.
  • What does the claimed bird capacity mean? “Roosting spots,” “suitable for,” and tested daily housing capacity are different statements.
  • What can you clean and access? Check nest access, litter management, openings, and the ability to inspect all areas without disturbing the birds.
  • Which hazards remain untested? Ventilation, thermal conditions, weather loads, predators and the needs of larger breeds require separate evaluation.
  • Can you keep the birds at your address? City and county rules, rooster restrictions, HOA requirements, setbacks and structure permits may differ. Start with CoopCheck's city directory and verify applicable official text.

How we assess a listing

We preserve the source location for each number, distinguish manufacturer claims from simple calculations, and label unknown measurements as unknown. We do not award an overall score when a listing lacks the information required to assess it. Our calculator uses one university-extension indoor benchmark only; it does not independently establish the safety of any coop or certify a legal bird count.

Merchants who wish to correct a documented specification can send the direct manufacturer's product page or technical sheet to support@serunio.com. Evidence-driven corrections are welcome regardless of any partnership.
