A 4 x 6 coop: how much of the 24 square feet is usable?

A marketed overall footprint may include the outdoor run. Audit a measured 24 sq ft indoor floor and compare obstruction losses before choosing a flock.

First establish the measurement

Advertised exterior dimensions are not usable indoor dimensions. Start this worksheet only after the seller or your own measurement confirms a clear enclosed 4 x 6 ft room.

A floor-loss ledger you can take to a seller

Twenty-four square feet divided by the site's four-square-foot daily-outdoor-access reference gives six hens by area alone. Floor loss changes that integer comparison: a fraction of a bird is rounded down, never up.

The table is not a welfare certificate or a municipal limit. It is a sensitivity check on one measurement. Count a cupboard or sealed bay as blocked; do not automatically deduct space underneath something birds can safely access.

Label the evidence behind each number

Beside “4 x 6,” write whether it came from an inside tape measurement, a seller's dimensioned drawing or an exterior listing. Record the unit and measurement date. If only exterior size is known, leave usable indoor area unknown instead of estimating wall thickness from a photograph.

List the fixtures that will be installed after delivery. An empty showroom measurement is not necessarily the final bird floor. Ask whether a removable tray, nest projection or attached wire section was included in the advertised dimensions.

Choose between four and six without calling either automatically safe

In an unobstructed 24-square-foot interior, four hens have six square feet each; six have four. The difference is room per bird, not a new legal allowance. Their outdoor references are 40 and 60 square feet respectively. Review actual confinement, breed, ventilation and access with the relevant adviser.

For an indoor-only arrangement, use the different source guidance rather than applying the six-bird estimate. An accessible cleanout opening and a workable perch layout still need independent checks.
