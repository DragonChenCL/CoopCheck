METHOD, OWNERSHIP AND LIMITS

What CoopCheck checks—and what it does not.

CoopCheck is a research and planning website. It organizes public city sources, seller-reported dimensions and explicit calculation assumptions. It is not a municipality, a surveying practice, an engineering service or a certification body.

Who is responsible for this material?

The project is maintained through the CoopCheck source repository. Editorial records, formula changes and source corrections can be inspected there. Contact support@serunio.com for a correction or purchase-support request. We do not claim an expert credential or first-hand product test that is not documented.

How the content is produced

The project uses structured source records, automated page generation and AI-assisted drafting and code work. Those processes are not evidence by themselves. Legal fields remain unresolved unless the record supports the specific claim; missing dimensions are not filled from product photos. Hypothetical worked examples are labelled, not presented as personal ownership experience.

An editorial update date describes the page revision. Each legal or product source keeps its own evidence date. A wording change does not silently mark an old source as checked again.

How corrections are handled

Send the page, disputed sentence and current primary source. Identify the effective date, jurisdiction and relevant section. Conflicting or incomplete evidence should remain visibly qualified while it is investigated; an old summary is not preferred merely because it is already published.

Payment and independence

The optional $4.99 printable report organizes user-entered geometry and a verification checklist; it does not purchase a permit or guarantee approval. Read the preview and exclusions. The currently documented three-model buying comparison is not an affiliate or sponsored placement.
