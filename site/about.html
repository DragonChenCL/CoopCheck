OUR MISSION

Build thoughtfully. Check the rules first.

CoopCheck helps people navigate backyard chicken-keeping requirements and turn a rough coop idea into a clearer first draft. We make research and planning tools accessible; we do not issue permits or make decisions for local authorities.

What we do

City rules with sources

Explore summaries of flock limits, rooster rules, permit considerations and coop location restrictions. We link to public authorities where available and distinguish researched statements from details still requiring verification.

Free planning tools

Try a chicken coop size calculator, basic 2D floor-plan generator, and a map-based location sketch. These are preliminary planning aids, not surveyed parcel boundaries or construction documents.

Optional printable report

The optional $4.99 site plan and setback report organizes user-entered map geometry and a verification checklist. You can preview the offer before deciding whether to buy.

Where the limits are

Local poultry rules can change. Citywide summaries may not reflect a specific address, zoning district, lot size, HOA, easement, building code or recent ordinance. Geometric checks are based on user-drawn approximations, not professional surveys. Always confirm requirements with your local planning or animal-control department before keeping birds or building.
