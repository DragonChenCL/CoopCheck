Why city-level rules matter

Backyard chicken questions are usually local: one city may limit flock size, another may require a permit, and another may measure coop separation from a neighboring dwelling instead of the property line. CoopCheck keeps the city rule and the map-based geometry check separate so the tool does not pretend to verify rules it cannot yet calculate.

All 22 California city profiles

20 profiles include some official-source research; 2 remain research pending and are not eligible for indexing. The notes below are starting points, not a statewide permission or address-level legal determination. Open the city page for current citations, exceptions and review scope.

  • Anaheim, CA

    Source reviewed

    Residential noncommercial animal keeping under Anaheim Municipal Code §18.38.030: combined poultry, birds, rabbits and rodents are limited to one animal per 1,800 sq ft of lot area. This is a combined-category density, not a separate hen allowance.

  • Bakersfield, CA

    Limited-scope source review

    Bakersfield CURRENT §17.10.020 Table 17.10-1 permits animal raising/breeding in R-S (and R-H) but NOT ordinary R-1 through R-6. §17.10.040(C)(1)(a) authorizes domestic poultry in R-S without giving a NUMERIC chicken cap in that subsection; NOT an unlimited-flock authorization or general agricultural cap.

  • Berkeley, CA

    Research pending — noindex

    City-specific rules have not been verified. Do not use this profile as approval to keep poultry or build a coop.

  • Elk Grove, CA

    Limited-scope source review

    RD-zone residential lots allow up to 6 chickens with a clean coop/cage; up to 12 only when a 40-ft buffer from the chicken area to every neighboring property line is maintained (§23.27, residential note 3).

  • Escondido, CA

    Limited-scope source review

    Escondido Table 33-95a: in R-1 up to 6 hens/domesticated fowl, in eligible R-A/R-E up to 25 domesticated fowl across species COMBINED; R-T/R-2–R-5 do not list chicken permission. §33-116 property split halves quantities.

  • Folsom, CA

    Limited-scope source review

    Folsom 2026 Ordinance 1362 §4 (§7.04.080(C)) permits at most SIX CHICKENS as pets in residential and other nonagricultural/non-A combining zones. In Agricultural Combining District (A), separate §17.40 allows agriculture/livestock farming; its 100-head use-permit trigger is NOT a general six-hen cap. Roosters prohibited in ALL zoning districts.

  • Fremont, CA

    Source reviewed

    Residential lots GREATER THAN 6,000 sq ft: at most 6 female chickens/rabbits/pigeons/doves COMBINED (two species max); city table states ZERO chickens for 6,000 sq ft or less; extra animals require Chapter 6.40 fancier permit.

  • Fresno, CA

    Limited-scope source review

    Fresno §15-2707(B)-(C): in ordinary residential districts, chickens/ducks/geese/turkeys and other farm fowl are not household pets on lots <=36,000 sq ft. Residential lots GREATER than 36,000 sq ft may keep up to 12 fowl per gross acre with other zoning conditions. No blanket all-residential prohibition.

  • Irvine, CA

    Research pending — noindex

    City-specific rules have not been verified. Do not use this profile as approval to keep poultry or build a coop.

  • Long Beach, CA

    Source reviewed

    Up to 20 fowl under the city's general quantity provision, but residential chicken placement distances and permits vary by flock size

  • Los Angeles, CA

    Limited-scope source review

    Los Angeles CURRENT R1 zoning §12.08(A)(3) authorizes NONCOMMERCIAL poultry as incidental to residence but gives NO numeric hen ceiling in that subsection. A1 §12.05(A)(7) has DISTINCT agricultural/commercial categories. This does NOT prove unlimited hens or uniform residential number; animal welfare/noise and specific parcel zoning still apply.

  • Modesto, CA

    Source reviewed

    Modesto §10-3.202(a)(3) authorizes MAX TWELVE combined domestic fowl (HENS ONLY), rabbits, hares and customary household pets per dwelling unit in R-1/R-2/R-3 zones. Not a universal 12-chicken permission in other zones.

  • Oakland, CA

    Limited-scope source review

    Community Garden / Extensive Agriculture animal keeping zoning depends on zone and animal control. The published Planning guide has NO universal numeric CHICKEN LIMIT; this missing value is not a legal right to unlimited birds.

  • Riverside, CA

    Limited-scope source review

    Current 2025 amendment permits up to 5 NONCOMMERCIAL poultry in qualifying RE and R-1 residential zoning; RR/RA-5/RC have separate 5/50 tiers. Old rural-only prohibition was stale; not 50 allowed on all urban lots.

  • Roseville, CA

    Source reviewed

    Up to 10 ducks, chickens, and pigeons in any combination on nonagricultural residential premises

  • Sacramento, CA

    Source reviewed

    Up to 3 chickens per residential parcel

  • San Diego, CA

    Source reviewed

    5, 15, or 25 depending on separation

  • San Francisco, CA

    Source reviewed

    Residential premises may keep no more than FOUR animals IN TOTAL across the species listed in San Francisco Health Code §37(a); chickens share that combined cap with named other birds/mammals, so this is not a separate four-hen allowance.

  • San Jose, CA

    Limited-scope source review

    San José Code §7.60.815 regulates the COMBINED count of chickens, rabbits and other small animals by distance from the enclosure to the nearest OTHER dwelling: <20 ft: 0; >20 to <40 ft: 6; >40 to <50 ft: 10; >50 ft: 20. Exactly 20, 40 or 50 ft is NOT resolved by the printed bands; seek city confirmation.

  • Santa Ana, CA

    Source reviewed

    Santa Ana City Code §5-6 allows up to FOUR fowl (chicken/duck/goose/turkey), rabbits or any COMBINATION on premises without special permit. More than four requires separate special animal permit and city Planning approval; zoning still applies.

  • Santa Rosa, CA

    Limited-scope source review

    Residential hen limit under Table 4-1: up to 3 on lots <=5,000 sq ft, 6 on 5,001–10,000, up to 12 on 10,001–43,560 (6 per 10,000 sq ft), and up to 30 on lots >1 acre (6 per 10,000 sq ft). Different rules apply to qualifying livestock-farming uses.

  • Stockton, CA

    Limited-scope source review

    Only in eligible RL/RM/RH residential zones: Stockton §16.80.060(E) Table 3-14 permits up to 4 hens/ducks per 5,000 sq ft on qualifying parcels (minimum generally 5,000 sq ft, certain existing-lot exceptions). Separate zoning tiers for agricultural/commercial uses.
