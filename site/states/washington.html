Why city-level rules matter

Backyard chicken questions are usually local: one city may limit flock size, another may require a permit, and another may measure coop separation from a neighboring dwelling instead of the property line. CoopCheck keeps the city rule and the map-based geometry check separate so the tool does not pretend to verify rules it cannot yet calculate.

All 12 Washington city profiles

11 profiles include some official-source research; 1 remain research pending and are not eligible for indexing. The notes below are starting points, not a statewide permission or address-level legal determination. Open the city page for current citations, exceptions and review scope.

  • Bellevue, WA

    Limited-scope source review

    Bellevue codified §20.20.130(B) counts SIX FOWL as household pets; over six become small domestic animals with additional lot-size rules. Official site says Ordinance 6930 PENDING codification; not a parcel-wide approval.

  • Bellingham, WA

    Limited-scope source review

    Bellingham Code §7.12.060(A) allows securely confined domestic fowl including chickens subject to other municipal ordinances; that specific provision DOES NOT state a numeric flock cap. This does not establish an unlimited citywide allowance.

  • Everett, WA

    Limited-scope source review

    Everett's approved small-livestock facility regulation §2(4)(c) allows up to 6 chickens on lots up to 10,000 sq ft, 8 on 10,000–15,000, 10 on 15,000–35,000, plus 1 per additional 5,000 above 35,000; combined small-livestock cap 12 per 10,000 sq ft and zoning/permit conditions apply. At exactly 10,000 or 15,000 sq ft published tiers overlap; ask city.

  • Kent, WA

    Source reviewed

    Up to 3 domesticated fowl on lots of at least 5,000 sq ft, plus 1 per additional 1,000 sq ft

  • Olympia, WA

    Limited-scope source review

    ≤¼ acre: up to 5 ducks or female chickens; >¼ to <1 acre: up to 9; ≥1 to <2 acres: 10 plus one per additional 1,000 sq ft (see exceptions)

  • Redmond, WA

    Source reviewed

    Current Redmond Municipal Code §7.04.157(B), amended 2025: Urban Recreation/RA-5 10 hens; NR lots >=18,000 sq ft 10, 12,000–17,999 8, 7,000–11,999 7, 5,500–6,999 6, 4,000–5,999 4, <4,000 0. IMPORTANT: current published NR bands overlap at 5,500–5,999 sq ft; ask Planning for that boundary. Older city webpage's R-1/R-6 labels may be stale.

  • Renton, WA

    Research pending — noindex

    City-specific rules have not been verified. Do not use this profile as approval to keep poultry or build a coop.

  • Seattle, WA

    Source reviewed

    Up to 8 domestic fowl on a standard lot

  • Spokane, WA

    Source reviewed

    1 chicken/fowl per 1,000 sq ft of lot area

  • Tacoma, WA

    Source reviewed

    6 adult poultry per parcel; may reach 10 with written consent of qualifying nearby residence owners; +1 bird per 5,000 sq ft lot above 10,000 sq ft

  • Vancouver, WA

    Limited-scope source review

    Vancouver VMC §20.895.050(B)(5) permits chickens/ducks/geese/rabbits for personal hobby uses with NO MINIMUM LOT SIZE; this is NOT an unlimited numeric flock allowance; roosters prohibited.

  • Yakima, WA

    Source reviewed

    Up to 4 hen chickens in eligible residential locations
