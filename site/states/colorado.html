Take the correct questions to the authority

Open the matching city profile and copy its local enquiry worksheet. Fill in the address, zoning, measured parcel area, birds, coop dimensions and proposed utilities. Ask the authority to identify the controlling provisions and the official application channel. Postal addresses, nearby city rules and a planning sketch are not substitutes.
