Why city-level rules matter

Backyard chicken questions are usually local: one city may limit flock size, another may require a permit, and another may measure coop separation from a neighboring dwelling instead of the property line. CoopCheck keeps the city rule and the map-based geometry check separate so the tool does not pretend to verify rules it cannot yet calculate.

  • Avondale, AZ

    Limited-scope source review

    For qualifying Avondale DETACHED single-family houses on LOTS ≤0.5 ACRE, Arizona §9-462.12(A),(D)-(E) protects the keeping of UP TO SIX DOMESTIC CHICKENS against local categorical prohibitions. Avondale §28-33(h) also subjects farm/backyard fowl to Chapter 3; this statewide floor is NOT the citywide cap on larger/rural lots or approval of every enclosure.

  • Buckeye, AZ

    Source reviewed

    Buckeye CURRENT Development Code (effective 2025-11-20): single-family DETACHED residence up to SIX domestic chickens under backyard-fowl exception. A secondary ADU adds no extra chicken allowance. Larger-scale farm animals have AG/R1-43/R1-18 zoning criteria; six is not a universal agricultural cap.

  • Casa Grande, AZ

    Source reviewed

    A resident of a detached single-family residence may keep up to 6 domestic chickens under city Code §6.06.010; agricultural zoning exceptions may apply.

  • Chandler, AZ

    Source reviewed

    Up to 6 hens on eligible single-family residential lots (2024 Ordinance 5113; not the obsolete five-hen city handout)

  • Flagstaff, AZ

    Limited-scope source review

    Domestic chicken (fowl) caps under §6-03-001-0003(A) Table A: 6 on ≤19,999 sq ft lots; 10 at 20,000–29,999; 15 at 30,000–39,999; 20 at 40,000–79,999; 25 on ≥80,000 sq ft. Lot use/zoning and offspring-age exceptions apply.

  • Gilbert, AZ

    Limited-scope source review

    Gilbert CURRENT Land Development Code §5.2.2(A)(5)(a), revised under adopted Ordinance 2911 effective September 2024: a qualifying detached-home lot ≤ONE-HALF ACRE allows up to SIX backyard CHICKENS/FOWL; larger lots allow 12 additional fowl per additional 10,000 sq ft above half-acre, subject to zoning/lot thresholds and state preemption. The separate OLD Code Compliance FAQ referring to 25 FOWL+RODENTS on 20,000sqft lots reflects a different/older mixed-animal rule and must NOT be used to promise 25 chickens on small lots.
