Backyard chicken laws in Santa Ana, CA

Up to FOUR fowl/rabbits in any COMBINATION before special keeping permit required. No separate four-chicken PLUS four-rabbit allotment; separate zoning requirements.

Flock size

Primary clause recorded

Santa Ana City Code §5-6 allows up to FOUR fowl (chicken/duck/goose/turkey), rabbits or any COMBINATION on premises without special permit. More than four requires separate special animal permit and city Planning approval; zoning still applies.

Santa Ana Code §5-6(a) · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

List every animal category counted by the cited rule, not just hens. Ask how the combined total and parcel eligibility apply to your household.

Roosters

Primary clause recorded

Roosters prohibited on residential-used premises or zoning districts that permit residential uses, §5-6.5.

Santa Ana Code §5-6.5 · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Confirm the rule for the proposed birds and zone. A hen allowance does not independently authorize roosters.

Permits and approvals

Primary clause recorded

A special permit is required to keep more than FOUR combined fowl/rabbits under §5-6(a), subject to Planning approval. At or below four this specific excess-animals special permit does not apply; check accessory building/zoning permit independently.

Santa Ana Code §5-6(a)-(b) · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Separate permission to keep birds from building, zoning and utility approvals. Obtain the applicable procedure for this particular proposal.

Coop placement

Primary clause recorded

§5-18(b) prohibits fowl/rabbits within 30 ft of another person's FAMILY residence or human-occupancy building, not 30 ft from a parcel boundary. For >4 animals requiring a permit, §5-6(b)(1) similarly applies 30-ft distance to others' homes and additional space/cleanliness requirements.

Santa Ana Code §§5-18(b), 5-6(b)(1) · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Label both boundary and building distances separately. Ask which rule applies to the coop, the run or the whole enclosure and how each distance is measured.
