Backyard chicken laws in Portland, OR

Portland allows up to four small domestic fowl on any lot and up to six on lots of 10,000 square feet or more. Standard backyard chicken facilities need no livestock permit, but structures must meet property-line setbacks.

Common questions

How many chickens can I keep in Portland?

Up to four small domestic fowl are allowed on any lot and up to six on lots of 10,000 square feet or more.

Do I need a chicken permit in Portland?

No standard livestock permit is required, but Title 13 and other building or zoning rules still apply.

What are Portland's chicken coop setbacks?

Livestock facility structures must be at least 3 feet from side and rear property lines and 10 feet from the front property line.
