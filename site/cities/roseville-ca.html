Backyard chicken laws in Roseville, CA

Mixed species count together, and the run has a boundary setback

Roseville's reviewed city guidance groups ducks, chickens and pigeons under a ten-bird combined allowance. Six chickens and four ducks use all ten under that category rather than allowing ten birds of each species. Roosters are not categorically excluded by this allowance, but animal-noise rules still apply.

The city requires a 20-foot separation from property lines for chicken enclosures, including fenced runs. For an illustrative 45-foot-wide lot, the two opposing 20-foot bands leave only five feet of width before other restrictions; fitting an animal shelter could be impractical. The city's discretionary special permit for nonconforming locations is not automatic permission.
