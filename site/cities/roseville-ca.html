Chicken rules at a glance

Official sources ↘
Flock size

Up to 10 ducks, chickens, and pigeons in any combination on nonagricultural residential premises

Roosters

Roosters are included in the domestic-fowl allowance, subject to the animal-noise ordinance

Permits and approvals

Animal special permit may be granted for nonconforming enclosure location, subject to discretionary review

Coop placement

Outdoor enclosure must be at least 20 ft from any property line; fenced chicken run also counts as enclosure
