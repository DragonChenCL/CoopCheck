Flock size

Bellevue codified §20.20.130(B) counts SIX FOWL as household pets

Read the full rule and exceptions

Bellevue codified §20.20.130(B) counts SIX FOWL as household pets; over six become small domestic animals with additional lot-size rules. Official site says Ordinance 6930 PENDING codification; not a parcel-wide approval.

Roosters

A 2013 Bellevue City Council meeting record did not establish an outright rooster ban at that time

Read the full rule and exceptions

A 2013 Bellevue City Council meeting record did not establish an outright rooster ban at that time; do not assume that remains current without checking subsequent amendments.

Permits and approvals

Verify with Bellevue Development Services if six or more domestic fowl would be treated as a small domestic animal use and whether a permit is needed.

Coop placement

Bellevue household pet coops must stand >=15 ft from PROPERTY LINE

Read the full rule and exceptions

Bellevue household pet coops must stand >=15 ft from PROPERTY LINE; small-domestic animal structures (over six fowl) >=25 ft from property line. Fenced free-roaming exception does NOT waive coop structure setback; Ord6930 PENDING codification.
