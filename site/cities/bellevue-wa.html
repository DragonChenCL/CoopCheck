Bellevue: six fowl is a category threshold, not a site approval

Bellevue Land Use Code §20.20.130 lists six fowl in the ordinary household-pet category, with a 15-foot property-line buffer for structures housing those pets. Larger small-domestic-animal uses are governed by another row, including additional lot-area tests and a 25-foot structure distance. On a hypothetical six-hen lot, the ordinary household-pet row is the correct starting category, but that still does not clear every structure, critical-area, easement or building restriction.

The code allows certain fenced free-roaming arrangements, yet the separate wording on the location of the structure housing pets remains important. The published section also warns that Ordinance 6930 is pending codification. Confirm the operative amendment and lot zoning before trusting a drawing as a lawful placement. The section cited here does not establish a blanket rooster rule or a general chicken-keeping permit waiver.
