Scope and limitations

Current Indianapolis-Marion County §743-306(W) personal livestock clause (not commercial farming): combined adult chickens, quail, pigeons and ducks <=12 with narrow personal-livestock license rules; up to one rooster subject to overnight enclosure. Check district-based structure setback, zoning and HOA.

Questions to check before building

How many chickens can I keep here?

Indianapolis §743-306(W) permits up to 12 ADULT chickens/quail/pigeons/ducks COMBINED for qualifying personal accessory husbandry at single-/two-family residence without livestock license; more require approval.

Are roosters allowed?

At most ONE rooster per lot and it must stay in a fully enclosed coop from dusk to dawn (§743-306(W)(5)).

Do I need a permit?

No personal livestock license needed for standard eligible up-to-12 combined adult category-one birds; above that a personal livestock license may be required (§743-306(W)(3)-(4)). Other building/zoning permits separate.

Where can the coop be placed?

No coops, roosts, pens or outside exercise areas in front yard; comply with actual zoning district setback and fencing/confinement requirements (§743-306(W)(7)); the code section gives NO universal numerical coop setback.

Before buying birds or constructing a coop

Confirm that your address is inside the correct city jurisdiction; look up your parcel zoning; confirm flock-size and rooster restrictions, any poultry permit, neighboring-dwelling or property-line separation, accessory-structure/building permits, and HOA or deed restrictions.
