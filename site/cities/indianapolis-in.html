Questions to check before building

How many chickens can I keep here?

Up to 12 adult chickens, quail, pigeons and ducks collectively as category-one personal livestock without livestock license, subject to other animal and zoning conditions

Are roosters allowed?

Rooster-specific noise and livestock restrictions require further city-code review

Do I need a permit?

Personal livestock license can permit higher quantities or smaller lots; code allows up to 12 category-one birds without one under its personal-livestock standards

Where can the coop be placed?

Coop zoning and enclosure setbacks must be checked under Marion County's zoning ordinance and personal livestock rules; no universal setback concluded from §743-306

Before buying birds or constructing a coop

Confirm that your address is inside the correct city jurisdiction; look up your parcel zoning; confirm flock-size and rooster restrictions, any poultry permit, neighboring-dwelling or property-line separation, accessory-structure/building permits, and HOA or deed restrictions.
