Backyard chicken laws in Indianapolis, IN

Eligible owner/resident of single-/two-family home may keep up to 12 combined adult chickens, quail, pigeons and ducks without personal livestock license; adult means 8+ months and counts combined, not by species.

Flock size

Primary clause recorded

Indianapolis §743-306(W) permits up to 12 ADULT chickens/quail/pigeons/ducks COMBINED for qualifying personal accessory husbandry at single-/two-family residence without livestock license; more require approval.

Indianapolis §743-306(W)(1)-(4), Table 743-306-3 · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

List every animal category counted by the cited rule, not just hens. Ask how the combined total and parcel eligibility apply to your household.

Roosters

Primary clause recorded

At most ONE rooster per lot and it must stay in a fully enclosed coop from dusk to dawn (§743-306(W)(5)).

Indianapolis §743-306(W)(5) · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Confirm the rule for the proposed birds and zone. A hen allowance does not independently authorize roosters.

Permits and approvals

Primary clause recorded

No personal livestock license needed for standard eligible up-to-12 combined adult category-one birds; above that a personal livestock license may be required (§743-306(W)(3)-(4)). Other building/zoning permits separate.

Indianapolis §743-306(W)(3)-(4), Chapter 838 · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Request the keeping/licensing procedure and renewal conditions, then ask separately about the proposed structure, utilities and building approvals.

Coop placement

Primary clause recorded

No coops, roosts, pens or outside exercise areas in front yard; comply with actual zoning district setback and fencing/confinement requirements (§743-306(W)(7)); the code section gives NO universal numerical coop setback.

Indianapolis §743-306(W)(7) · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Mark the legal boundary and the regulated part of the enclosure. Confirm zoning setbacks, easements and any additional structure requirements separately.
