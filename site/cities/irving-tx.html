Scope and limitations

Current Irving Code §6-62(a)-(d) distinguishes <=1-acre maximum 25 chickens/conditional one rooster (>=6 hens), >1-acre rooster-to-hen ratio, 25-ft setback to other people's occupied buildings and enclosure specifications. §6-62(c)(1) requires compliance with separate building/zoning requirements; its special exhibition permit is NOT a generic ordinary residential chicken license or an exemption.

Questions to check before building

How many chickens can I keep here?

Up to 25 chickens on parcels <=1 acre; larger-parcel rooster-to-hen ratio rules apply

Are roosters allowed?

No more than 1 rooster per 6 hens on <=1 acre, and ratio of 1 rooster per 6 hens on larger lots; additional code exceptions

Do I need a permit?

Irving Code §6-62(c)(1) requires chicken enclosures to meet all applicable CITY building and zoning requirements. §6-62 does not provide a general building-permit waiver or resolve whether an ordinary chicken-keeping permit is required for a given property. Special permitted animal exhibitions under §6-62(d) are a different case.

Where can the coop be placed?

Chicken housing at least 25 ft from an occupied building on another person's property; roofed roosting area and outdoor run min 10 sq ft per chicken

Before buying birds or constructing a coop

Confirm that your address is inside the correct city jurisdiction; look up your parcel zoning; confirm flock-size and rooster restrictions, any poultry permit, neighboring-dwelling or property-line separation, accessory-structure/building permits, and HOA or deed restrictions. City rules can change.
