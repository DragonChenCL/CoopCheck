Can you keep chickens in Phoenix?

Yes, backyard chickens are allowed under city rules, but parcel-specific zoning and distance requirements still matter. The exact answer for a specific home can still depend on zoning, lot size, accessory-structure rules, neighboring buildings and private HOA or CC&R restrictions.
