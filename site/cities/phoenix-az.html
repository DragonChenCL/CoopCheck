Flock size

Primary clause recorded

Roosters

Primary clause recorded

Phoenix current §8-7(c), preserved in 2024 adopted poultry amendment G-7300, permits male poultry ONLY if incapable of vocal noise disturbing residents; not permission for crowing roosters.

§8-7(c), 2024 adopted ordinance G-7300 City Council packet p114 · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Confirm the applicable noise and nuisance conditions; a conditional statement is not an unconditional rooster allowance.

Permits and approvals

Primary clause recorded

City says NEW detached residential structures LARGER THAN 200 SQ FT require a BUILDING PERMIT; electrics/plumbing require their own approvals. Whether a coop is classified as a detached accessory building or qualifies for a small-structure exception must be confirmed with Phoenix PDD; chicken KEEPING permit status is not established.

Phoenix Planning & Development Residential Projects 2026: detached structure >200 sqft, electrical and plumbing permit triggers · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Provide the coop footprint, height and utilities. Ask how the city classifies this structure and which conditions remain even if a building permit is exempt.

Coop placement

Primary clause recorded

Rear or side yard; enclosure at least 20 ft from a neighboring property line unless adjoining owners/occupants consent in writing; no front-yard poultry

Can you keep chickens in Phoenix?

Yes. Phoenix City Code § 8-7, as amended by Ordinance G-7300, allows backyard poultry subject to flock-size, enclosure-location and nuisance rules; parcel-specific zoning and private HOA/CC&R restrictions can still matter. The exact answer for a specific home can still depend on zoning, lot size, accessory-structure rules, neighboring buildings and private HOA or CC&R restrictions.

Common questions

Can I have chickens in Phoenix, AZ?

Yes. Phoenix City Code § 8-7, as amended by Ordinance G-7300, allows backyard poultry subject to flock-size, enclosure-location and nuisance rules; parcel-specific zoning and private HOA/CC&R restrictions can still matter.

How many chickens can I keep in Phoenix?

Up to 6 poultry on the first ½ acre; more may be allowed with written consent from all adjoining owners/occupants.

Are roosters allowed in Phoenix?

Male poultry are allowed only if incapable of making vocal noise that disturbs the peace, comfort or health of nearby residents.

What are the chicken coop placement rules in Phoenix?

Rear or side yard; enclosure at least 20 ft from a neighboring property line unless adjoining owners/occupants consent in writing; no front-yard poultry.
