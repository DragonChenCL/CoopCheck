Can you keep chickens in Phoenix?

Yes. Phoenix City Code § 8-7, as amended by Ordinance G-7300, allows backyard poultry subject to flock-size, enclosure-location and nuisance rules; parcel-specific zoning and private HOA/CC&R restrictions can still matter. The exact answer for a specific home can still depend on zoning, lot size, accessory-structure rules, neighboring buildings and private HOA or CC&R restrictions.

Phoenix chicken coop rules at a glance

Maximum / quantity: Up to 6 poultry on the first ½ acre. On a lot of ½ acre or less, more than 6 may be kept only with written consent from every lawful owner and occupant of adjoining property. For larger lots, the ordinance adds 20 poultry per additional ½ acre and removes the numerical limit above 2½ acres.

Roosters / male poultry: Allowed only if incapable of making vocal noise that disturbs the peace, comfort or health of nearby residents.

Permit note: City says NEW detached residential structures LARGER THAN 200 SQ FT require a BUILDING PERMIT; electrics/plumbing require their own approvals. Whether a coop is classified as a detached accessory building or qualifies for a small-structure exception must be confirmed with Phoenix PDD; chicken KEEPING permit status is not established..

Placement: Rear or side yard; enclosure at least 20 ft from a neighboring property line unless adjoining owners/occupants consent in writing; no front-yard poultry.
