Permits and approvals

Primary clause recorded

Fort Worth Animal Care's CURRENT rules require rooster/fowl kept in pen or coop AT ALL TIMES, cleaning DAILY and enclosure MORE THAN FIFTY ft from a regulated human-use structure (neighbor residence, restaurants, schools etc.). These are keeping CONDITIONS; the page does NOT establish a generic livestock or building permit exemption.

Can you keep chickens in Fort Worth?

Yes, subject to lot-size limits and a 50-foot separation from regulated structures. The exact answer for a specific home can still depend on zoning, lot size, accessory-structure rules, neighboring buildings and private HOA or CC&R restrictions.
