Flock size

Primary clause recorded

A resident of a detached single-family residence may keep up to 6 domestic chickens under city Code §6.06.010; agricultural zoning exceptions may apply.

§6.06.010(B) · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Bring the measured parcel area and zoning designation. Ask which row or threshold applies; do not substitute the postal city name for jurisdiction.

Roosters

Primary clause recorded

Male fowl, including roosters, are prohibited in the backyard-fowl allowance.

§6.06.010(B)(1) · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Confirm the rule for the proposed birds and zone. A hen allowance does not independently authorize roosters.

Permits and approvals

Primary clause recorded

Casa Grande city code FAQ: qualifying detached SMALL RESIDENTIAL accessory buildings ≤200 SQ FT, height-limited and WITHOUT UTILITIES can avoid building permit, BUT CITY PLANNING APPROVAL for size/height/LOT-LINE SETBACKS is still REQUIRED. A specific chicken coop must qualify for similar-use exemption; §6.06 poultry rights and licensing separately unresolved.

Casa Grande City Planning & Development FAQ: small accessory structures exempt construction permit, require Planning sign-off · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Provide the coop footprint, height and utilities. Ask how the city classifies this structure and which conditions remain even if a building permit is exempt.

Coop placement

Primary clause recorded

Rear or side yard enclosure at least 20 ft from neighboring properties; maximum 200 sq ft and 8 ft height (lower than fence line on some lots).

§6.06.010(B)(2) · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Mark the legal boundary and the regulated part of the enclosure. Confirm zoning setbacks, easements and any additional structure requirements separately.
