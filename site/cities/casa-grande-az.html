Questions to check before building

How many chickens can I keep here?

A resident of a detached single-family residence may keep up to 6 domestic chickens under city Code §6.06.010; agricultural zoning exceptions may apply.

Are roosters allowed?

Male fowl, including roosters, are prohibited in the backyard-fowl allowance.

Do I need a permit?

Follow Chapter 6.06 and applicable zoning/structure requirements; no universal chicken permit is stated in §6.06.010.

Where can the coop be placed?

Rear or side yard enclosure at least 20 ft from neighboring properties; maximum 200 sq ft and 8 ft height (lower than fence line on some lots).

Before buying birds or constructing a coop

Confirm that your address is inside the correct city jurisdiction; look up your parcel zoning; confirm flock-size and rooster restrictions, any poultry permit, neighboring-dwelling or property-line separation, accessory-structure/building permits, and HOA or deed restrictions. City rules can change.
