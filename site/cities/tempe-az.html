Can you keep chickens in Tempe?

Yes. Tempe allows domestic chickens on residential lots with single-family dwellings, subject to its animal-quantity rules. The exact answer for a specific home can still depend on zoning, lot size, accessory-structure rules, neighboring buildings and private HOA or CC&R restrictions.

Tempe chicken coop rules at a glance

Maximum / quantity: Up to 6 domestic chickens per lot.

Roosters: Prohibited except in the AG district.

Permit note: A use permit may be available to exceed the standard quantity.

Placement: On lots under 1 acre, chicken enclosures may not exceed the fence/wall height.
