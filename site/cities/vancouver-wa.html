Can you keep chickens in Vancouver?

Yes. Vancouver's official code-compliance FAQ says chickens are allowed within city limits. The exact answer for a specific home can still depend on zoning, lot size, accessory-structure rules, neighboring buildings and private HOA or CC&R restrictions.
