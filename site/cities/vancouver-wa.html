Flock size

Primary clause recorded

Vancouver VMC §20.895.050(B)(5) permits chickens/ducks/geese/rabbits for personal hobby uses with NO MINIMUM LOT SIZE; this is NOT an unlimited numeric flock allowance; roosters prohibited.

Permits and approvals

Primary clause recorded

Vancouver domestic poultry personal/hobby keeping is a listed permitted use across zoning districts subject to VMC 8.20/8.24 and off-site impacts; this does NOT settle chicken-keeping licenses or construction/building permits.

VMC §20.895.050(A),(B)(5),(E)-(F) · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Request the keeping/licensing procedure and renewal conditions, then ask separately about the proposed structure, utilities and building approvals.

Coop placement

Primary clause recorded

Vancouver §20.895.050(B)(5) classifies chicken keeping as domestic poultry requiring proper containment and nuisance compliance under VMC8.20/8.24 and OFF-SITE IMPACTS §20.935. Livestock shelter rear-yard/house-setback standard in (B)(6) is specifically for LIVESTOCK and cannot automatically set a chicken-coop numerical setback. Separate accessory building zone rules need parcel review.

Vancouver VMC §20.895.050(B)(5)-(6), (E) and VMC Chapter20.935 offsite impacts · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Mark the legal boundary and the regulated part of the enclosure. Confirm zoning setbacks, easements and any additional structure requirements separately.

Can you keep chickens in Vancouver?

Yes. Vancouver's official code-compliance FAQ says chickens are allowed within city limits. The exact answer for a specific home can still depend on zoning, lot size, accessory-structure rules, neighboring buildings and private HOA or CC&R restrictions.
