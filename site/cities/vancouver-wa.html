Read full rule and exceptions

Vancouver §20.895.050(B)(5) classifies chicken keeping as domestic poultry requiring proper containment and nuisance compliance under VMC8.20/8.24 and OFF-SITE IMPACTS §20.935. Livestock shelter rear-yard/house-setback standard in (B)(6) is specifically for LIVESTOCK and does NOT automatically set a chicken-coop numerical setback. Separate accessory building zone rules need parcel review.

Can you keep chickens in Vancouver?

Yes. Vancouver's official code-compliance FAQ says chickens are allowed within city limits. The exact answer for a specific home can still depend on zoning, lot size, accessory-structure rules, neighboring buildings and private HOA or CC&R restrictions.
