Backyard chicken laws in Buckeye, AZ

Qualifying detached single-family residence may keep up to SIX DOMESTIC CHICKENS, not all fowl species, in backyard. ADU does not grant another quota; AG/R1-43/R1-18 livestock provisions separate.

Flock size

Primary clause recorded

Buckeye CURRENT Development Code (effective 2025-11-20): single-family DETACHED residence up to SIX domestic chickens under backyard-fowl exception. A secondary ADU adds no extra chicken allowance. Larger-scale farm animals have AG/R1-43/R1-18 zoning criteria; six is not a universal agricultural cap.

Buckeye Development Code §3.2 Domesticated Farm Animals (B)(1)-(2), adopted Ord.28-25 effective 2025-11-20 · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

List every animal category counted by the cited rule, not just hens. Ask how the combined total and parcel eligibility apply to your household.

Roosters

Primary clause recorded

Buckeye 2025 Development Code §3.2(B)(5) explicitly PROHIBITS male backyard chickens including ROOSTERS. Farm/agricultural uses, other species and legal exceptions must be checked separately.

Buckeye Development Code §3.2 Domesticated Farm Animals (B)(5), Ord.28-25 · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Confirm the rule for the proposed birds and zone. A hen allowance does not independently authorize roosters.

Permits and approvals

Primary clause recorded

Buckeye's CURRENT §3.2(B) expressly authorizes qualifying detached-house backyard chickens SUBJECT to conditions and zoning; unrelated domesticated farm animals are zone-limited (AG/R1-43/R1-18 and half-acre in rural residential). This is LAND USE permission, not a statement that coop construction or any separate permit is waived.

Buckeye Development Code §3.1.1(A), §3.2(B), §3.2 backyard fowl / Ord.28-25 · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Separate permission to keep birds from building, zoning and utility approvals. Obtain the applicable procedure for this particular proposal.

Coop placement

Primary clause recorded

Buckeye §3.2(B)(3)-(4): covered chicken enclosure SIDE OR REAR YARD, at least 20 ft from a NEIGHBORING PROPERTY LINE (not neighbor dwelling), max 200 sq ft and 8 ft tall, no taller than required perimeter fence. Required fence height and manure-cleaning conditions apply; not a building permit exemption.

Buckeye Development Code §3.2(B)(3)-(4), Ord.28-25 · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Label both boundary and building distances separately. Ask which rule applies to the coop, the run or the whole enclosure and how each distance is measured.
