Questions to check before building

How many chickens can I keep here?

The City of Los Angeles does not have a single citywide backyard hen limit confirmed by the reviewed zoning provision. Agricultural and other zoning districts differ; verify property zoning.

Are roosters allowed?

City Code §53.71 generally limits a property to at most ONE rooster, with expressly stated permitted business, educational, exhibition and filming exceptions; nuisance rules still apply.

Do I need a permit?

Poultry permissions depend on zoning and accessory use; any commercial or educational exception requires proper licensing.

Where can the coop be placed?

No universal residential coop setback established by these provisions; consult applicable zoning, animal and accessory-structure rules.

Before buying birds or constructing a coop

Confirm that your address is inside the correct city jurisdiction; look up your parcel zoning; confirm flock-size and rooster restrictions, any poultry permit, neighboring-dwelling or property-line separation, accessory-structure/building permits, and HOA or deed restrictions. City rules can change.
