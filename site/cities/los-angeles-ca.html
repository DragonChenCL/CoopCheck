Flock size

Read the full rule and exceptions

Los Angeles CURRENT R1 zoning §12.08(A)(3) authorizes NONCOMMERCIAL poultry as incidental to residence but gives NO numeric hen ceiling in that subsection. A1 §12.05(A)(7) has DISTINCT agricultural/commercial categories. This does NOT prove unlimited hens or uniform residential number; animal welfare/noise and specific parcel zoning still apply.

Roosters

This field has conditions that need a closer look.

Read the full rule and exceptions

City Code §53.71 generally limits a property to at most ONE rooster, with expressly stated permitted business, educational, exhibition and filming exceptions; nuisance rules still apply.

Permits and approvals

Los Angeles R1 zoning §12.08(A)(3) allows NONCOMMERCIAL domestic poultry in conjunction with residential use.

Read the full rule and exceptions

Los Angeles R1 zoning §12.08(A)(3) allows NONCOMMERCIAL domestic poultry in conjunction with residential use. A1 §12.05(A)(7)(a) has narrow 5-acre COMMERCIAL chicken conditions. This is zoning USE authorization only: commercial licence or coop BUILDING permit still may be needed, depending on parcel and project.

Coop placement

No universal residential coop setback established by these provisions; consult applicable zoning, animal and accessory-structure rules.
