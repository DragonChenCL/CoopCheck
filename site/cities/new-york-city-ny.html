Backyard chicken laws in New York City, NY

The City confirms HENS are allowed but ROOSTERS and other birds/fowl are not. The city FAQ and referenced Health Code section DO NOT state one universally applicable numeric backyard-hen quota. This is a checked narrow negative finding, NOT unlimited permission; noise, housing, sanitation, zoning and private restrictions remain.

Permits and approvals

Primary clause recorded

NYC allows backyard HENS and prohibits ROOSTERS. Commercial live-poultry market permits are a DIFFERENT business use, not automatic backyard licence requirements; individual coop structural/zoning approvals remain undetermined.

NYC Urban Agriculture FAQ and separate commercial Health Code §161.19 · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Request the keeping/licensing procedure and renewal conditions, then ask separately about the proposed structure, utilities and building approvals.

Coop placement

Primary clause recorded

NYC Health Code §161.19(c) imposes at least 25 FT between buildings and coops of LIVE POULTRY MARKETS, which may not operate on a residential dwelling's premises. It is NOT a general 25ft backyard hen coop setback. Regular home-hen coops need separate property/building/zoning review; no uniform citywide residential line-offset proven.

NYC Health Code Article161 §161.19(b)-(c), 25ft live poultry MARKET coop setback only · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Label both boundary and building distances separately. Ask which rule applies to the coop, the run or the whole enclosure and how each distance is measured.
