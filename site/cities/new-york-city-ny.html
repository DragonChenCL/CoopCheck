Read the full rule and exceptions

NYC allows backyard HENS and prohibits ROOSTERS. Commercial live-poultry market permits are a DIFFERENT business use, not automatic backyard licence requirements; individual coop structural/zoning approvals remain undetermined.

Coop placement

This field has conditions that need a closer look.

Read the full rule and exceptions

NYC Health Code §161.19(c) imposes at least 25 FT between buildings and coops of LIVE POULTRY MARKETS, which may not operate on a residential dwelling's premises. It is NOT a general 25ft backyard hen coop setback. Regular home-hen coops need separate property/building/zoning review; no uniform citywide residential line-offset proven.
