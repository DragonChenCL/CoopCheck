Backyard chicken laws in Kent, WA

At least 5000 sq ft lot permits up to three fowl, plus one for each extra 1000 sq ft of lot area; fowl are separate from ordinary pet category.

A 7,000-square-foot lot changes the fowl count

Kent's official FAQ describes up to three domesticated fowl on a qualifying lot of 5,000 square feet, plus one for each additional 1,000 square feet. A qualifying 7,000-square-foot parcel therefore suggests up to five fowl under this arithmetic, subject to other conditions. The city's FAQ does not publish a categorical rooster ban, but noise and nuisance rules still matter.

The city also lists ten feet between a chicken coop and the property line. Its general shed guidance discusses building permits at 200 square feet or larger, but applying that threshold to a specific chicken house requires a building-classification check. Property easements may impose further siting restrictions.
