Flock size

Read the full rule and exceptions

Chicken permit Tier I 1–6 HENS, Tier II 7–15, Tier III 16–30. Normal residential coop SIX fowl only; >6 requires explicit MACC APPROVAL and possibly extra conditions. Tier III NOT automatic 30 birds.

Roosters

Permits and approvals

A fowl permit is required before purchasing birds; neighbor consent, training, and zoning approval are part of the application

Coop placement

This field has conditions that need a closer look.

Read the full rule and exceptions

Residential coop/run must be ≥20 ft from a HABITABLE BUILDING on an ADJACENT property (not lot line), entirely REAR of principal dwelling, screened from neighboring residences, zoning compliant.
