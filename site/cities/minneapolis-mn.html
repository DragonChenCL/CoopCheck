Permit tiers do not automatically authorize thirty backyard hens

Minneapolis describes permit tiers for one to six hens, seven to fifteen and sixteen to thirty, but the normal residential six-bird standard remains important. Applicants in higher categories may need additional authorization from Animal Care and Control; choosing the highest fee tier does not itself permit thirty hens at an ordinary home.

The permit application involves neighbor consent, education, zoning approval and inspection before acquiring birds. The coop and run must be behind the principal dwelling and at least twenty feet from a habitable building on an adjacent property, not twenty feet from the shared boundary. A separate rooster permit category is not automatic rooster permission.
