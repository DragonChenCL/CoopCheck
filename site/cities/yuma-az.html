Questions to check before building

How many chickens can I keep here?

Yuma §130-075: eligible detached single-family lots <=1/2 acre SIX fowl/rodents COMBINED; extra 1/2-acre tiers allow 20 more; >2.5 acres no numeric ceiling in this section. Zoning and Arizona state preemption separately apply.

Are roosters allowed?

Yuma §130-076: male poultry only if INCAPABLE of vocal noises that disturb residents; neither unconditional rooster permission nor blanket rooster ban.

Do I need a permit?

Yuma R-1-40 zone specifically permits poultry raising as principal use under §154-05.04(C)(7); SR uses addressed separately, subject to current animal Chapter130 and Arizona state preemption. This is ZONING use eligibility, NOT a universal animal-keeping or building permit determination.

Where can the coop be placed?

Yuma §130-071: enclosure at least 20 ft from NEIGHBORING PROPERTY on lots <=1/2 acre; 30 ft on larger lots. §130-073 enclosure <=200 sq ft, height at/below fence on <1 acre or <=8 ft on >1 acre. Different dwelling, zoning and state rules separately.
