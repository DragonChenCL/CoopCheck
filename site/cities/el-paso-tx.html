Questions to check before building

How many chickens can I keep here?

El Paso §7.24.020(B)(1): fewer than SIX fowl/rabbits as HOUSEHOLD PETS qualify for the specific poultry permit EXCEPTION. SIX OR MORE normally requires poultry permit outside permitted ranch-farm zones. The city does NOT set a universal max of six in this clause; permitted total decided by §7.24.050 veterinary officer and zoning.

Are roosters allowed?

El Paso §7.24.020(B)(1) explicitly sets NO MORE THAN THREE ROOSTERS where keeping fowl is otherwise permitted. This does NOT override noise/nuisance limits, zoning or required permit for six-plus birds.

Do I need a permit?

El Paso fowl/rabbit permit REQUIRED for SIX OR MORE outside farm-ranch/stable-permitted zoning; <6 maintained as household PETS exempt from this PARTICULAR permit. City animal officer determines permit's total-animal limit and nuisance/setback conditions; does not waive coop construction zoning.

Where can the coop be placed?

El Paso §7.24.030(B): for FOWL SUBJECT TO PERMIT, secure coop ≥30 ft from third-party residence, hotel, school, church etc (NOT lot line). Can be 20 ft with PUBLIC ALLEY plus ≥6-ft solid wall; city veterinary officer may approve a waiver if health/nuisance safe. This is NOT automatically a 30-ft rule for <6 household pets.
