Can you keep chickens in Arlington?

Yes. Arlington permits small backyard flocks and adds distance and lot-size rules as flock size increases. The exact answer for a specific home can still depend on zoning, lot size, accessory-structure rules, neighboring buildings and private HOA or CC&R restrictions.

Arlington chicken coop rules at a glance

Maximum / quantity: Up to 4 chickens without distance or lot-size restrictions; larger flocks have extra rules.

Roosters: Prohibited except agricultural zoning.

Permit note: Check accessory-structure permit rules for the coop itself.

Placement: For 5+ chickens, city rules add 50-ft dwelling-distance and lot-size thresholds.
