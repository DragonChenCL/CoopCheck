Questions to check before building

How many chickens can I keep here?

The City of Avondale's zoning ordinance conditionally permits farm animals/backyard fowl in certain residential districts under §28-33(h); no general municipal hen cap was confirmed by this zoning provision. Eligible homes may also have state-law protections under A.R.S. §9-462.12.

Are roosters allowed?

City chapter 3 requirements and any state-law exception must be checked; no reliable universal rooster ruling established by §28-33(h).

Do I need a permit?

Some zoning districts list keeping farm animals as permitted with conditions; check City Code Chapter 3 Article II before acquiring birds.

Where can the coop be placed?

The reviewed zoning use table does not establish a universal chicken-coop setback; Arizona law may permit city requirements up to 20 ft from neighboring property for qualifying lots.

Before buying birds or constructing a coop

Confirm that your address is inside the correct city jurisdiction; look up your parcel zoning; confirm flock-size and rooster restrictions, any poultry permit, neighboring-dwelling or property-line separation, accessory-structure/building permits, and HOA or deed restrictions.
