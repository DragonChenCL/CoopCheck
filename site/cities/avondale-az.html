Flock size

Primary clause recorded

For qualifying Avondale DETACHED single-family houses on LOTS ≤0.5 ACRE, Arizona §9-462.12(A),(D)-(E) protects the keeping of UP TO SIX DOMESTIC CHICKENS against local categorical prohibitions. Avondale §28-33(h) also subjects farm/backyard fowl to Chapter 3; this statewide floor is NOT the citywide cap on larger/rural lots or approval of every enclosure.

Arizona Revised Statutes §9-462.12(A),(D)-(E), statewide 2024 law; Avondale Zoning §28-33(h) · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Bring the measured parcel area and zoning designation. Ask which row or threshold applies; do not substitute the postal city name for jurisdiction.

Roosters

Primary clause recorded

Avondale 2025-amended zoning DEFINES BACKYARD FOWL as domestic backyard chickens EXCLUDING MALE ROOSTERS. Arizona §9-462.12(A)(1) allows towns to restrict roosters. This verifies the narrow definition, NOT that all agricultural/farm-zoned rooster keeping is categorically prohibited without reading Chapter 3.

Avondale Zoning Code Article1 §28-5 definitions Backyard fowl (2025 amendment) and Arizona §9-462.12(A)(1) · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Confirm the rule for the proposed birds and zone. A hen allowance does not independently authorize roosters.

Coop placement

Primary clause recorded

Arizona §9-462.12(B) REQUIRES cities including Avondale to enact rules keeping backyard fowl enclosures on RESIDENTIAL-COMMUNITY lots UNDER ONE ACRE SHORTER THAN PROPERTY FENCE. §(A)(2) only ALLOWS, not forces, city to adopt 20ft neighboring-property buffer, ≤200sf/8ft cap; no citywide numerical Avondale coop setback proven. §(C) preserves enclosures predating Sept 14, 2024 from later ordinances.

Arizona A.R.S. §9-462.12(B),(C),(D) mandatory under-fence-line rule for municipal residential communities · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Label both boundary and building distances separately. Ask which rule applies to the coop, the run or the whole enclosure and how each distance is measured.
