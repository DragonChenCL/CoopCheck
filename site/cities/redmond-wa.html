Questions to check before building

How many chickens can I keep here?

Current Redmond Municipal Code §7.04.157(B), amended 2025: Urban Recreation/RA-5 10 hens; NR lots >=18,000 sq ft 10, 12,000–17,999 8, 7,000–11,999 7, 5,500–6,999 6, 4,000–5,999 4, <4,000 0. IMPORTANT: current published NR bands overlap at 5,500–5,999 sq ft; ask Planning for that boundary. Older city webpage's R-1/R-6 labels may be stale.

Are roosters allowed?

Roosters prohibited

Do I need a permit?

Approval of chicken husbandry registration required before housing hens under current Redmond Code §7.04.157(C). Official registration form says no fee, but any accessory-structure permissions and HOA restrictions remain independent.

Where can the coop be placed?

Current Redmond §7.04.157(D): shelter normally >=15 ft from any PROPERTY line, potentially >=5 ft only next to specified nonresidential use, qualifying >=10-ft easement or public corridor; no front yard, screened, shelter <=200 sq ft / <=8 ft tall. Check site-specific zoning/building rules.

Before buying birds or constructing a coop

Confirm that your address is inside the correct city jurisdiction; look up your parcel zoning; confirm flock-size and rooster restrictions, any poultry permit, neighboring-dwelling or property-line separation, accessory-structure/building permits, and HOA or deed restrictions.
