Backyard chicken laws in Redmond, WA

2025 amended zone table is NR square-foot-tiered, unlike older city Chicken Husbandry page. NR bands overlap 5,500–5,999 sf (4 vs 6 hens); city must resolve. UR/RA-5 up to 10; other NR tiers as code. Not automatic property approval.

Flock size

Primary clause recorded

Current Redmond Municipal Code §7.04.157(B), amended 2025: Urban Recreation/RA-5 10 hens; NR lots >=18,000 sq ft 10, 12,000–17,999 8, 7,000–11,999 7, 5,500–6,999 6, 4,000–5,999 4, <4,000 0. IMPORTANT: current published NR bands overlap at 5,500–5,999 sq ft; ask Planning for that boundary. Older city webpage's R-1/R-6 labels may be stale.

Redmond Code §7.04.157(B)(1)-(3), Ord.3219 of 2025 / 3267 as of 2026-08-03 · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Bring the measured parcel area and zoning designation. Ask which row or threshold applies; do not substitute the postal city name for jurisdiction.

Roosters

Primary clause recorded

Roosters prohibited

City of Redmond Chicken Husbandry: Raising Chickens in Redmond · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Confirm the rule for the proposed birds and zone. A hen allowance does not independently authorize roosters.

Permits and approvals

Primary clause recorded

Approval of chicken husbandry registration required before housing hens under current Redmond Code §7.04.157(C). Official registration form says no fee, but any accessory-structure permissions and HOA restrictions remain independent.

Redmond Code §7.04.157(C), approved husbandry registration · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Request the keeping/licensing procedure and renewal conditions, then ask separately about the proposed structure, utilities and building approvals.

Coop placement

Primary clause recorded

Current Redmond §7.04.157(D): shelter normally >=15 ft from any PROPERTY line, potentially >=5 ft only next to specified nonresidential use, qualifying >=10-ft easement or public corridor; no front yard, screened, shelter <=200 sq ft / <=8 ft tall. Check site-specific zoning/building rules.

RMC §7.04.157(D)(1)-(6), 2025 Ord.3219; current through Ordinance 3267 2026 · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Label both boundary and building distances separately. Ask which rule applies to the coop, the run or the whole enclosure and how each distance is measured.
