A four-hen count can pass while the coop location fails

Yakima's planning guidance describes up to four hens on eligible residential premises and excludes roosters. For a four-hen coop beside a garage, measure separately to the residential building, property lines and the other nearby structures. The cited rules require ten feet from a residence or property line, five feet from other structures and backyard confinement.

A coop can satisfy a generic space calculator yet be too close to a fence or a building. Some detached accessory structures have different permit thresholds, but that does not override chicken-specific placement regulations. Verify the residential zoning, site survey and construction category.
