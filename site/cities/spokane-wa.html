Can you keep chickens in Spokane?

Yes. Spokane allows small domestic animals including chickens in zones that permit dwelling units, subject to lot-area ratios and enclosure rules. The exact answer for a specific home can still depend on zoning, lot size, accessory-structure rules, neighboring buildings and private HOA or CC&R restrictions.

A 5,000-square-foot lot gives an animal-count starting point

Spokane's cited small-domestic-animal provision uses a combined allowance of one qualifying animal or fowl for each 1,000 square feet of parcel area. A 5,000-square-foot lot therefore suggests five under that calculation if the property otherwise qualifies and no other covered animals use the allowance. It is not an automatic promise to keep five hens on every parcel.

Roosters are restricted to the RA district. The published exception from a WSU animal-keeping certificate for chickens is narrower than a general building-permit or zoning waiver. Structures still follow residential accessory-building setbacks, which should be drawn from the correct zone's actual rules.
