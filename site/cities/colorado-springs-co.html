Can you keep chickens in Colorado Springs?

Yes. Colorado Springs allows a limited number of chickens, while coop structures must also comply with zoning and building rules. The exact answer for a specific home can still depend on zoning, lot size, accessory-structure rules, neighboring buildings and private HOA or CC&R restrictions.

Colorado Springs chicken coop rules at a glance

Maximum / quantity: Up to 10 rabbits or chickens aged 6 months or older.

Roosters: Prohibited.

Permit note: Coops must comply with applicable zoning/building rules for accessory structures.

Placement: At least 4 sq ft of shelter space per chicken; accessory-structure setbacks vary by zoning/size.
