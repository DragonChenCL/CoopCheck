Permits and approvals

Primary clause recorded

Colorado Springs government guidance says chicken coops must meet zoning/building rules; accessory structures SMALLER THAN 200 sq ft generally need no Pikes Peak regional BUILDING permit but zoning, site setbacks, overlays and chicken-keeping permit questions remain separate.

Colorado Springs city Accessory Structures guide; Rabbits and Chickens city guidance · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Request the keeping/licensing procedure and renewal conditions, then ask separately about the proposed structure, utilities and building approvals.

Can you keep chickens in Colorado Springs?

Yes. Colorado Springs allows a limited number of chickens, while coop structures must also comply with zoning and building rules. The exact answer for a specific home can still depend on zoning, lot size, accessory-structure rules, neighboring buildings and private HOA or CC&R restrictions.
