Questions to check before building

How many chickens can I keep here?

Oro Valley ADOPTED 2024 (O)24-08, Zoning §23.6.A.7 Table 23.6.1: detached single-family parcels ≤41,999 sq ft allow up to SIX chickens; 42,000–143,999 up to TEN; ≥144,000 up to TWENTY-FOUR subject to zone eligibility. CONDITIONAL USE PERMIT to exceed applicable limits on eligible very large parcels. Arizona statewide protection for ≤0.5acre also applies; this is not a universal parcel permit.

Are roosters allowed?

Oro Valley ADOPTED (O)24-08 §23.6.A.7(c)(ii): MALE FOWL INCLUDING ROOSTERS PROHIBITED under the town's small-animal-husbandry rules. Zoning and nuisance/animal welfare requirements still apply.

Do I need a permit?

Oro Valley ADOPTED (O)24-08 §23.6.A.7(a)-(b) recognizes NONCOMMERCIAL chicken keeping as an ACCESSORY use at eligible DETACHED single-family dwellings. Table23.6.1 note requires CONDITIONAL USE PERMIT to increase animal quotas on lots exceeding 144,000 sq ft. No blanket zoning, coop-building or independent animal license waiver is established.

Where can the coop be placed?

Oro Valley enacted §23.6.A.7: chicken enclosure REAR YARD ONLY. For ≤0.5-acre eligible lot, enclosure ≥20 ft from NEIGHBORING PROPERTY (not dwelling), possible §23.5.C.2(h) relief; larger lots ordinarily MAIN BUILDING setbacks. On lots UNDER 21,780 sq ft coop area ≤200 sq ft / height ≤8 ft; fully contained/screened and manure removed twice weekly.
