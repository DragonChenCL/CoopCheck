Oro Valley ADOPTED 2024 (O)24-08, Zoning §23.6.A.7 Table 23.6.1: detached single-family parcels ≤41,999 sq ft allow up to SIX chickens; 42,000–143,999 up to TEN; ≥144,000 up to TWENTY-FOUR subject to zone eligibility. Oro Valley ADOPTED (O)24-08 §23.6.A.7(a)-(b) recognizes NONCOMMERCIAL chicken keeping as an ACCESSORY use at eligible DETACHED single-family dwellings. Table23.6.1 note requires CONDITIONAL USE PERMIT to increase animal quotas on lots exceeding 144,000 sq ft. No blanket zoning, coop-building or independent animal license waiver is established.

Coop placement

Oro Valley enacted §23.6.A.7: chicken enclosure REAR YARD ONLY.

Read the full rule and exceptions

Oro Valley enacted §23.6.A.7: chicken enclosure REAR YARD ONLY. For ≤0.5-acre eligible lot, enclosure ≥20 ft from NEIGHBORING PROPERTY (not dwelling), possible §23.5.C.2(h) relief; larger lots ordinarily MAIN BUILDING setbacks. On lots UNDER 21,780 sq ft coop area ≤200 sq ft / height ≤8 ft; fully contained/screened and manure removed twice weekly.
