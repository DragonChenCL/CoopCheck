Scope and limitations

The town's Nov 2024 executive report confirms an adopted September 2024 zoning-code amendment allowing small animal husbandry on qualifying detached single-family parcels; later 2025 markup document is a DRAFT and must not be presented as operative city law. Lot-size bands, roosters, permits and enclosure setbacks need an effective-code check.

Questions to check before building

How many chickens can I keep here?

Chickens are allowed as noncommercial small animal husbandry on qualifying detached single-family residential property; the currently operative town-specific maximum for each lot-size tier has not been fully established in this review. Arizona A.R.S. §9-462.12 provides a six-fowl protection for qualifying detached-home properties of one-half acre or less.

Are roosters allowed?

Rooster-specific restrictions require the operative Town Zoning Code §23.6.A.7 and nuisance provisions; do not infer blanket permission.

Do I need a permit?

Review town zoning §23.6.A.7, accessory-use conditions and any applicable structure permits; no universally applicable permit conclusion established.

Where can the coop be placed?

Rear-yard and enclosure provisions appear in town small-animal-husbandry standards, but the exact applicable setback must be confirmed from the currently enacted version for the property's zoning and lot size.

Before buying birds or constructing a coop

Confirm that your address is inside the correct city jurisdiction; look up your parcel zoning; confirm flock-size and rooster restrictions, any poultry permit, neighboring-dwelling or property-line separation, accessory-structure/building permits, and HOA or deed restrictions. City rules can change.
