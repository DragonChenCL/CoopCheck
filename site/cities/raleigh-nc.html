Questions to check before building

How many chickens can I keep here?

Residential districts do not allow raising farm animals under Raleigh's published home-garden guidance; chickens in qualifying community gardens/nonresidential sites have separate limits

Are roosters allowed?

Raleigh prohibits animal raising in RESIDENTIAL community gardens; in eligible NONRESIDENTIAL COMMUNITY GARDENS chicken keeping max 1 per 1000sqft, max10, and NO ROOSTERS. Scope is community gardens, not all home backyards.

Do I need a permit?

A community garden requires a Tier One planning review before permit issuance. §6.6.1(B)(2)(h) bars residential-district community-garden animals. Does NOT establish permit exemption or zoning approval for an ordinary backyard coop.

Where can the coop be placed?

Eligible NONRESIDENTIAL community-garden coops must stand at least 25 ft from ANY PROPERTY LINE. This does NOT establish setbacks/permission for all residential backyard enclosures.

Confirm that your address is inside the correct city jurisdiction; look up your parcel zoning; confirm flock-size and rooster restrictions, any poultry permit, neighboring-dwelling or property-line separation, accessory-structure/building permits, and HOA or deed restrictions.
