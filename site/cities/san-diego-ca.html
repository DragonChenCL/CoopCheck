What this means for a backyard coop

San Diego uses a tiered system: up to five chickens outside required setbacks, up to 15 with greater property-line separation, and up to 25 when the coop and enclosure are at least 50 feet from a residence.

San Diego chicken coop setback requirements: 5 vs 15 vs 25 hens

San Diego Municipal Code §42.0709(e)(2) sets three different placement conditions. Do not confuse a distance to a property line with a distance to a residential building.

  • Up to 5 hens: The coop must be outside the zoning-required setbacks. City guidance says a typical single-family lot uses 5 ft from side property lines and 13 ft from the rear line; confirm your particular zone instead of treating these sample figures as universal.
  • Up to 15 hens: The coop must be outside all required zoning setbacks or at least 15 ft from each property line, whichever is greater.
  • Up to 25 hens: The coop and the outdoor enclosure must each be at least 50 ft from any structure used for residential purposes. This is a residential-building separation test, not a blanket 50-ft property-line setback.

In each case, confirm your zoning, parcel boundaries, nearby residences, and any other city or building requirements before construction. Common questions

Are backyard chickens allowed in San Diego?

Yes, on qualifying single-family residential properties, community gardens and retail farms.

Are roosters allowed in San Diego?

No. Roosters are prohibited.

How many chickens can I keep in San Diego?

The allowed number depends on coop placement and separation: generally 5, 15 or 25 under different distance rules.

What are the setbacks for 5, 15, or 25 chickens in San Diego?

For 5 hens, outside the zoning-required setbacks; for 15, at least 15 ft from property lines or the required zoning setback, whichever is greater; for 25, the coop and outdoor enclosure must be at least 50 ft from any residential-use structure. See San Diego Municipal Code §42.0709(e)(2) and confirm your parcel-specific zoning and nearby buildings.

San Diego worked case: the threshold changes with location, not coop size alone

The city's official chickens guidance describes three conditional tiers: up to five when the coop is outside required zone setbacks; up to fifteen if the relevant coop distances are at least fifteen feet from all property lines and outside any greater required zoning setbacks; and up to twenty-five under a separate fifty-foot-from-residences condition. Roosters are prohibited. These are tiers for eligible uses, not permission to substitute one distance for another. City of San Diego chicken guidance.
