San Diego chicken coop setbacks: 5, 15 or 25 chickens?

San Diego uses three different coop-placement thresholds. Up to 5 chickens require the coop outside all zoning setbacks; up to 15 need at least 15 ft from property lines or the zoning setback, whichever is greater; up to 25 require the coop and enclosure at least 50 ft from any residential building.

How far from the property line can a San Diego chicken coop be?

The official city guidance and Municipal Code §42.0709 set out three alternatives. These are different placement conditions, not one universal distance:

Up to 5 chickensCoop outside all required zoning setbacks

The city says a typical single-family case places the coop in the rear yard, 5 ft from side lines and 13 ft from the rear line. Your property's zoning may differ.

Up to 15 chickensAt least 15 ft from every property line

Or the zoning-required setback, whichever is greater.

Up to 25 chickensCoop and enclosure at least 50 ft from any residential building

This is a residential-building separation condition, not a 50-ft property-line setback.

RoostersNot permitted

Separate coop, run, zoning and applicable building requirements still apply.

Which setback applies to your San Diego backyard?

First confirm whether you are inside San Diego city limits and check the zoning setback for your parcel. If the 5-chicken tier does not fit your planned flock, compare the 15-chicken property-line rule. The 25-chicken tier instead requires measuring distance from the coop and enclosure to any residential building, which must be verified separately.

Verify before acting. Zoning, lot size, accessory-structure rules, neighboring structures and HOA/CC&R restrictions can change the answer for a specific property.

Common questions

Are backyard chickens allowed in San Diego?

Yes, on qualifying single-family residential properties, community gardens and retail farms.

Are roosters allowed in San Diego?

No. Roosters are prohibited.

How many chickens can I keep in San Diego?

The allowed number depends on coop placement and separation: generally 5, 15 or 25 under different distance rules.

What are the chicken coop setback requirements in San Diego?

Up to 5 chickens: the coop must be outside zoning-required setbacks (typically 5 ft at the side and 13 ft at the rear for many single-family lots). Up to 15: at least 15 ft from every property line or the zoning setback, whichever is greater. Up to 25: both coop and enclosure must be at least 50 ft from any residential building. Confirm your parcel, zone and neighboring structures with the city.
