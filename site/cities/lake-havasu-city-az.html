Backyard chicken laws in Lake Havasu City, AZ

Up to six domestic backyard fowl on qualified detached single-family residences; zone table A(2) allows only residents of homes existing on September 13, 2024 in marked districts; not a blanket citywide permission.

Flock size

Primary clause recorded

Lake Havasu City §14.03.03(E)(9) states up to six domestic chickens on qualifying single-family detached residences; eligibility also depends on zoning table §14.03.02, including a September 13, 2024 existing-residence condition in zones marked A(2). Not a citywide permit for all parcels.

§14.03.03(E)(9)(a); §14.03.02 zoning table A(2) · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Bring the measured parcel area and zoning designation. Ask which row or threshold applies; do not substitute the postal city name for jurisdiction.

Roosters

Primary clause recorded

No male fowl, including roosters, within the qualifying backyard-fowl use (§14.03.03(E)(9)(b)).

§14.03.03(E)(9)(b) · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Confirm the rule for the proposed birds and zone. A hen allowance does not independently authorize roosters.

Permits and approvals

Primary clause recorded

Lake Havasu §14.03.02 backyard-fowl use table marks A(2) zones limited to qualifying detached homes EXISTING ON 2024-09-13; §14.03.03(E)(9)(c) explicitly requires CITY BUILDING PERMIT for perimeter fence above six ft in buildable area (up to eight ft). This is a fence-specific approval, NOT proof every coop needs or avoids a keeping/building permit.

Lake Havasu Development Code §14.03.02 A(2) grandfathering; §14.03.03(E)(9)(c), current 2026 · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Provide the coop footprint, height and utilities. Ask how the city classifies this structure and which conditions remain even if a building permit is exempt.

Coop placement

Primary clause recorded

On qualifying properties, fowl enclosure must be in rear or side yard at least 20 ft from neighboring lot line, roofed, no more than 200 sq ft; maximum height/opacity/fence rules also apply (§14.03.03(E)(9)(c)).

§14.03.03(E)(9)(c) · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Mark the legal boundary and the regulated part of the enclosure. Confirm zoning setbacks, easements and any additional structure requirements separately.
