Permits and approvals

Primary clause recorded

Goodyear exempts qualifying SINGLE-STORY detached accessory sheds/playhouses/SIMILAR structures of ≤200 SQ FT WITH NO ELECTRIC, PLUMBING, OR MECHANICAL SYSTEMS from BUILDING PERMIT; CITY ZONING still governs location. A chicken coop's classification/animal-keeping approval must be confirmed; do not assume universal coop permit exemption.

Goodyear Homeowner Permit FAQs Storage Sheds, Playhouses and Similar Structures · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Provide the coop footprint, height and utilities. Ask how the city classifies this structure and which conditions remain even if a building permit is exempt.

Coop placement

Primary clause recorded

Side or backyard enclosure at least 20 ft from neighboring property; enclosure max 200 sq ft and no taller than 8 ft or required fence line

Goodyear official FAQ, Where Can I Keep Them? · recorded 2026-10-13
