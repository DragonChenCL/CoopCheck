Backyard chicken laws in Riverside, CA

Flock size

Primary clause recorded

Current 2025 amendment permits up to 5 NONCOMMERCIAL poultry in qualifying RE and R-1 residential zoning; RR/RA-5/RC have separate 5/50 tiers. Old rural-only prohibition was stale; not 50 allowed on all urban lots.

Riverside §19.455.030(B)(1), (C)-(E), Ord7701 (2025) · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Bring the measured parcel area and zoning designation. Ask which row or threshold applies; do not substitute the postal city name for jurisdiction.

Roosters

Primary clause recorded

CROWING poultry prohibited in RE/R-1 zones. Qualified RR, RA-5 and RC parcels may keep up to SEVEN crowing roosters with land-area restrictions and acoustical nighttime enclosure 100 ft from a neighboring RESIDENCE.

Riverside §19.455.030(B)(1)(c),(C)(1)(f),(D)-(E) · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Confirm the rule for the proposed birds and zone. A hen allowance does not independently authorize roosters.

Permits and approvals

Primary clause recorded

Qualified RE/R-1 up to five ordinary poultry allowed as zoning use. Exceeding larger RR and RA-5/RC tier counts needs a MINOR CONDITIONAL USE or discretionary permit; independent coop building permissions still apply. NOT a citywide no-permit assertion.

Riverside §19.455.020,§19.455.030(B)-(E),§19.440 · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Separate permission to keep birds from building, zoning and utility approvals. Obtain the applicable procedure for this particular proposal.

Coop placement

Primary clause recorded

New RE/R-1 hobby coop must be ≥10 ft from a NEIGHBORING RESIDENCE, NOT a property-line offset, plus accessory building code. RR/RA-5/RC poultry tiers need 50/100 ft from residences; rooster overnight acoustic shelter 100 ft under separate rules.

Riverside §19.455.030(B)(1)(b),(C)-(E), §19.440 · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Label both boundary and building distances separately. Ask which rule applies to the coop, the run or the whole enclosure and how each distance is measured.
