Questions to check before building

How many chickens can I keep here?

On residential lots greater than 6,000 sq ft, up to 6 FEMALE chickens/rabbits/pigeons/doves combined without an animal-fancier permit, maximum two eligible species per lot. Lots smaller than 6,000 sq ft cannot keep chickens; confirm treatment of exactly 6,000 sq ft.

Are roosters allowed?

Roosters prohibited on residentially zoned property under §6.10.030.

Do I need a permit?

An animal fancier permit under Chapter 6.40 is needed for numbers beyond the ordinary permitted aggregate.

Where can the coop be placed?

Must comply with Fremont Municipal Code §6.10.130 minimum 10 sq ft permeable land per chicken, and setback tiers based on chicken count and lot size; verify exact measurements.

Before buying birds or constructing a coop

Confirm that your address is inside the correct city jurisdiction; look up your parcel zoning; confirm flock-size and rooster restrictions, any poultry permit, neighboring-dwelling or property-line separation, accessory-structure/building permits, and HOA or deed restrictions.
