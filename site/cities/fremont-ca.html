Questions to check before building

How many chickens can I keep here?

Residential lots GREATER THAN 6,000 sq ft: at most 6 female chickens/rabbits/pigeons/doves COMBINED (two species max); city table states ZERO chickens for 6,000 sq ft or less; extra animals require Chapter 6.40 fancier permit.

Are roosters allowed?

Fremont §6.10.030: roosters PROHIBITED on residential-zoned parcels; no claim about unrelated agricultural/other zones.

Do I need a permit?

Animal-fancier permit under Chapter 6.40 needed beyond ordinary combined six female chicken/rabbit/pigeon/dove allowance; no universal building-permit waiver.

Where can the coop be placed?

Chicken enclosure needs >=10 sq ft permeable land per bird. Table PROPERTY-line offsets: 1–5 chickens on 6,001–9,999 sq ft lots 10 ft, 6–10 chickens on 10,000–21,779 35 ft, 11–15 on >=21,780 50 ft (larger flocks need separate §6.40 permit). No front-yard coops. Section preamble also references residences: verify actual measurement.
