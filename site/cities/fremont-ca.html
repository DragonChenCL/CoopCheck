Backyard chicken laws in Fremont, CA

Eligible residential lots >6,000sf permit six combined FEMALE chickens/rabbits/pigeons/doves and at most two species; precisely <=6,000sf table 0 chickens.

Flock size

Primary clause recorded

Residential lots GREATER THAN 6,000 sq ft: at most 6 female chickens/rabbits/pigeons/doves COMBINED (two species max); city table states ZERO chickens for 6,000 sq ft or less; extra animals require Chapter 6.40 fancier permit.

§6.10.040, §6.10.130(b) table · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

List every animal category counted by the cited rule, not just hens. Ask how the combined total and parcel eligibility apply to your household.

Roosters

Primary clause recorded

Fremont §6.10.030: roosters PROHIBITED on residential-zoned parcels; no claim about unrelated agricultural/other zones.

§6.10.030 · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Confirm the rule for the proposed birds and zone. A hen allowance does not independently authorize roosters.

Permits and approvals

Primary clause recorded

Animal-fancier permit under Chapter 6.40 needed beyond ordinary combined six female chicken/rabbit/pigeon/dove allowance; no universal building-permit waiver.

§6.10.040; Chapter 6.40 · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Separate permission to keep birds from building, zoning and utility approvals. Obtain the applicable procedure for this particular proposal.

Coop placement

Primary clause recorded

Chicken enclosure needs >=10 sq ft permeable land per bird. Table PROPERTY-line offsets: 1–5 chickens on 6,001–9,999 sq ft lots 10 ft, 6–10 chickens on 10,000–21,779 35 ft, 11–15 on >=21,780 50 ft (larger flocks need separate §6.40 permit). No front-yard coops. Section preamble also references residences: verify actual measurement.

§6.10.130(a)-(b), §6.10.070(f) · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Ask for a chicken-specific or independently applicable structure rule. Do not borrow a livestock distance or use an unconfirmed buffer as a clearance.
