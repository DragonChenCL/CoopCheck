Official sources ↘
Flock size

Medford Municipal Code §5.555 allows poultry for personal, noncommercial use but does not establish a universal flock-number limit in this section.

Roosters

Roosters are expressly prohibited.

Permits and approvals

Medford §5.555(1)(e) explicitly requires poultry pens/runs/coops to satisfy all applicable building and fire codes.

Read the full rule and exceptions

Medford §5.555(1)(e) explicitly requires poultry pens/runs/coops to satisfy all applicable building and fire codes. The city Building Safety permit guide says sheds/outbuildings MAY need a building permit; this does NOT itself prove a separate chicken-keeping license or universal no-permit exception. Ask Planning/Building Safety about your specific coop.

Coop placement

Poultry enclosures at least 4 ft from abutting parcel property lines; none in required front-yard setback.

What was reviewed — and what is not confirmed?

These field notes reflect the linked sources, but not every rule or exception has been confirmed against the currently effective code. See the evidence audit.

Current Medford §5.555 (through Ordinance 2026-56, Aug. 19 2026) permits personal-use fowl but prohibits roosters and requires four feet to neighboring property lines and no front-yard-setback pens. §5.555(1)(e) explicitly invokes applicable building/fire codes. City Building Safety says outbuildings may require permits; a separate poultry-keeping permit is NOT confirmed, and building-permit status is property/structure-dependent.
