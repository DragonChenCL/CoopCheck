Questions to check before building

How many chickens can I keep here?

Gilbert CURRENT Land Development Code §5.2.2(A)(5)(a), revised under adopted Ordinance 2911 effective September 2024: a qualifying detached-home lot ≤ONE-HALF ACRE allows up to SIX backyard CHICKENS/FOWL; larger lots allow 12 additional fowl per additional 10,000 sq ft above half-acre, subject to zoning/lot thresholds and state preemption. The separate OLD Code Compliance FAQ referring to 25 FOWL+RODENTS on 20,000sqft lots reflects a different/older mixed-animal rule and must NOT be used to promise 25 chickens on small lots.

Are roosters allowed?

Gilbert LDC §5.2.2(A)(5)(b): ONE rooster older than FOUR MONTHS per 20,000 sqft lot area, subject to Chapter 6 NOISY ANIMAL limits. Roosters are not categorically barred citywide nor allowed automatically on small parcels. Verify commercial raising exceptions separately.

Do I need a permit?

Gilbert CURRENT LDC §5.2.2(A)(5) expressly allows qualifying residential FOWL subject to lot-size caps, coop siting and Municipal Code Chapter 6 animal/nuisance requirements. This establishes ZONING CONDITIONAL USE STANDARDS, NOT a determination that every chicken coop is exempt from construction approval or a municipal keeping license.

Where can the coop be placed?

Gilbert LDC §5.2.2(A)(5): poultry enclosures must be in SIDE OR REAR YARD, at least TWENTY FEET from ANY PROPERTY LINE, no more than 200 SQ FT and 8ft high; on lots <1 acre enclosure must also be LOWER THAN fence line. AZ §9-462.12 preemption affects eligible existing enclosures and lot rights.

Before buying birds or constructing a coop

Confirm that your address is inside the correct city jurisdiction; look up your parcel zoning; confirm flock-size and rooster restrictions, any poultry permit, neighboring-dwelling or property-line separation, accessory-structure/building permits, and HOA or deed restrictions.
