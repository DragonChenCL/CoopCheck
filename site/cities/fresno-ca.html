Flock size

This field has conditions that need a closer look.

Read the full rule and exceptions

Fresno §15-2707(B)-(C): in ordinary residential districts, chickens/ducks/geese/turkeys and other farm fowl are not household pets on lots <=36,000 sq ft. Residential lots GREATER than 36,000 sq ft may keep up to 12 fowl per gross acre with other zoning conditions. No blanket all-residential prohibition.

Roosters

Roosters prohibited anywhere inside Fresno city under §15-2707(G), except within an accredited zoological park.

Read the full rule and exceptions

Roosters prohibited anywhere inside Fresno city under §15-2707(G), except within an accredited zoological park. Not a generic 'unverified' rooster rule.

Coop placement

This field has conditions that need a closer look.

Read the full rule and exceptions

On eligible >36,000-sq-ft residential lots, §15-2707(C)(4) requires animal housing/pens >=40 ft from PROPERTY lines AND >=50 ft from neighboring residences. Small residential parcels do not receive a poultry-keeping entitlement from this section.

What was reviewed — and what is not confirmed?

These field notes reflect the linked sources, but not every rule or exception has been confirmed against the currently effective code. See the evidence audit.

City of Fresno current Municipal Code §15-2707(A)-(G), animal keeping. Residential sites <=36,000 sqft prohibit farm fowl as household pets, >36,000 sqft permits 12 fowl/gross acre, city roosters prohibited (accredited zoo exception), and 40-ft lot-line/50-ft neighboring-residence separation applies to non-household animals. Actual parcel zoning, grandfathered use, annexation overlay, construction approvals require local review.
