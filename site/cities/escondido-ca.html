Flock size

Primary clause recorded

Escondido Table 33-95a: in R-1 up to 6 hens/domesticated fowl, in eligible R-A/R-E up to 25 domesticated fowl across species COMBINED; R-T/R-2–R-5 do not list chicken permission. §33-116 property split halves quantities.

§33-95 Table 33-95a, notes *, §33-116 · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

List every animal category counted by the cited rule, not just hens. Ask how the combined total and parcel eligibility apply to your household.

Roosters

Primary clause recorded

Escondido rooster rights ZONING-SPECIFIC: R-A 1–2 by lot size, R-E one only with CONDITIONAL USE PERMIT; no R-1 rooster authorization.

§33-95 Table 33-95a Roosters row and notes ** · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Record bird age as well as sex. Ask which age-based restriction applies; do not turn an age threshold into a blanket all-age statement.

Permits and approvals

Primary clause recorded

Escondido R-E one rooster requires CONDITIONAL USE PERMIT (CUP); commercial egg/poultry production in R-A has a separate CUP under §33-94; neither establishes a building-permit exemption for ordinary hen coops.

§33-95 Table 33-95a; §33-94 commercial use table · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Separate permission to keep birds from building, zoning and utility approvals. Obtain the applicable procedure for this particular proposal.

Coop placement

Primary clause recorded

Escondido animal coops/enclosures are accessory structures subject to §§33-144(b),33-145 and parcel-specific attached/detached standards under §33-102; NO universal property-line chicken-coop distance established.

§33-95 accessory enclosure table; §33-102(a)-(b) · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Mark the legal boundary and the regulated part of the enclosure. Confirm zoning setbacks, easements and any additional structure requirements separately.
