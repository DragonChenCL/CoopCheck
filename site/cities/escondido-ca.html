Questions to check before building

How many chickens can I keep here?

Escondido Table 33-95a: in R-1 up to 6 hens/domesticated fowl, in eligible R-A/R-E up to 25 domesticated fowl across species COMBINED; R-T/R-2–R-5 do not list chicken permission. §33-116 property split halves quantities.

Are roosters allowed?

Escondido rooster rights ZONING-SPECIFIC: R-A 1–2 by lot size, R-E one only with CONDITIONAL USE PERMIT; no R-1 rooster authorization.

Do I need a permit?

Escondido R-E one rooster requires CONDITIONAL USE PERMIT (CUP); commercial egg/poultry production in R-A has a separate CUP under §33-94; neither establishes a building-permit exemption for ordinary hen coops.

Where can the coop be placed?

Escondido animal coops/enclosures are accessory structures subject to §§33-144(b),33-145 and parcel-specific attached/detached standards under §33-102; NO universal property-line chicken-coop distance established.
