A one-time hen permit does not authorize free-range placement

Longmont's standard nonagricultural allowance is four hens, and the reviewed city form requires a one-time chicken-hen permit. Roosters do not qualify for the routine residential program apart from narrow educational or scientific exceptions. A four-hen drawing should begin with the existing rear-yard structures, not just the count and suggested indoor area.

The city's application adds enclosure and separation rules for side and rear lot lines and nearby structures, plus specific exceptions. It also restricts free-ranging without relevant abutting-owner permission. An owner's written permission for an exception is not a substitute for the city permit; confirm the current form and actual parcel conditions before building.
