Permits and approvals

Primary clause recorded

Oakland requires zoning-specific authorization for animal raising within Community Garden or Extensive Agriculture uses: some zones allow the activity outright, others require a Conditional Use Permit (CUP), while other animal/health regulations apply. This DOES NOT imply that all ordinary backyard hens universally need a CUP.

City of Oakland Urban Agriculture > Community Gardens / Extensive Agriculture / Apply for Permits; zoning by activity category · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Separate permission to keep birds from building, zoning and utility approvals. Obtain the applicable procedure for this particular proposal.

Coop placement

Primary clause recorded

City Planning requires proposed enclosure site plan, new structures and any electrical/plumbing alterations in permit applications where activity requires review. It does NOT provide a numeric citywide coop setback; parcel zoning and building department must check.

Oakland City Planning Urban Agriculture site plan and structural permit scope · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Mark the legal boundary and the regulated part of the enclosure. Confirm zoning setbacks, easements and any additional structure requirements separately.
