Questions to check before building

How many chickens can I keep here?

Community Garden / Extensive Agriculture animal keeping zoning depends on zone and animal control. The published Planning guide has NO universal numeric CHICKEN LIMIT; this missing value is not a legal right to unlimited birds.

Are roosters allowed?

Not established from the cited city urban-agriculture zoning overview

Do I need a permit?

Oakland requires zoning-specific authorization for animal raising within Community Garden or Extensive Agriculture uses: some zones allow the activity outright, others require a Conditional Use Permit (CUP), while other animal/health regulations apply. This DOES NOT imply that all ordinary backyard hens universally need a CUP.

Where can the coop be placed?

City Planning requires proposed enclosure site plan, new structures and any electrical/plumbing alterations in permit applications where activity requires review. It does NOT provide a numeric citywide coop setback; parcel zoning and building department must check.

Before buying birds or constructing a coop

Confirm that your address is inside the correct city jurisdiction; look up your parcel zoning; confirm flock-size and rooster restrictions, any poultry permit, neighboring-dwelling or property-line separation, accessory-structure/building permits, and HOA or deed restrictions. City rules can change.
