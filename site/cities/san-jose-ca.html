Flock size

This field has conditions that need a closer look.

Read the full rule and exceptions

San José Code §7.60.815 regulates the COMBINED count of chickens, rabbits and other small animals by distance from the enclosure to the nearest OTHER dwelling: <20 ft: 0; >20 to <40 ft: 6; >40 to <50 ft: 10; >50 ft: 20. Exactly 20, 40 or 50 ft is NOT resolved by the printed bands; seek city confirmation.

Roosters

Roosters OLDER than four months are prohibited by San José §7.60.820; younger birds remain subject to other ordinances and nuisances.

Permits and approvals

San José §7.60.700 requires animal permit for MORE than six combined small animals, subject to site inspection and a two-year permit

Read the full rule and exceptions

San José §7.60.700 requires animal permit for MORE than six combined small animals, subject to site inspection and a two-year permit; the city can require a permit for fewer upon written notice of an animal-code violation. Building/zoning and HOA restrictions are independent.

Coop placement

§7.60.810 bars permit-covered small animals from the zoning SIDE setback.

Read the full rule and exceptions

§7.60.810 bars permit-covered small animals from the zoning SIDE setback. §7.60.815 limits animal count according to the coop-to-NEAREST OTHER DWELLING distance, not lot lines; exact 20/40/50-ft endpoints absent from published intervals and require city clarification.

What was reviewed — and what is not confirmed?

These field notes reflect the linked sources, but not every rule or exception has been confirmed against the currently effective code. See the evidence audit.

City Code §§7.10.205,7.60.700,710,810,815,820: combined small-animal ceiling by nearest other dwelling (not property line), rooster ban over age four months, required two-year permit for >6, and side-setback restriction for permitted groups. §7.60.815's literal exactly-20/40/50-ft gaps are unresolved; city clarification needed.
