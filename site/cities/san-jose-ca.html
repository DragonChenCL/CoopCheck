Backyard chicken laws in San Jose, CA

Chickens and similar species are collectively small animals. Maximum depends on enclosure-to-NEAREST OTHER dwelling: <20 ft: 0, >20 to <40: 6, >40 to <50: 10, >50: 20. Exactly 20/40/50 ft not expressly addressed by the literal intervals. §7.60.700 separately requires a permit for >6.

Flock size

Primary clause recorded

San José Code §7.60.815 regulates the COMBINED count of chickens, rabbits and other small animals by distance from the enclosure to the nearest OTHER dwelling: <20 ft: 0; >20 to <40 ft: 6; >40 to <50 ft: 10; >50 ft: 20. Exactly 20, 40 or 50 ft is NOT resolved by the printed bands; seek city confirmation.

San Jose Municipal Code §§7.10.205, 7.60.815; table Small Animals · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

List every animal category counted by the cited rule, not just hens. Ask how the combined total and parcel eligibility apply to your household.

Roosters

Primary clause recorded

Roosters OLDER than four months are prohibited by San José §7.60.820; younger birds remain subject to other ordinances and nuisances.

San Jose Municipal Code §7.60.820 · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Record bird age as well as sex. Ask which age-based restriction applies; do not turn an age threshold into a blanket all-age statement.

Permits and approvals

Primary clause recorded

San José §7.60.700 requires animal permit for MORE than six combined small animals, subject to site inspection and a two-year permit; the city can require a permit for fewer upon written notice of an animal-code violation. Building/zoning and HOA restrictions are independent.

San Jose Municipal Code §§7.60.700(A)-(B), 7.60.710(A)-(C), 7.60.715 · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Provide the coop footprint, height and utilities. Ask how the city classifies this structure and which conditions remain even if a building permit is exempt.

Coop placement

Primary clause recorded

§7.60.810 bars permit-covered small animals from the zoning SIDE setback. §7.60.815 limits animal count according to the coop-to-NEAREST OTHER DWELLING distance, not lot lines; exact 20/40/50-ft endpoints absent from published intervals and require city clarification.

San Jose Municipal Code §§7.60.810, 7.60.815 · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Label both boundary and building distances separately. Ask which rule applies to the coop, the run or the whole enclosure and how each distance is measured.
