Limited-scope policy summary · 2026-10-13

Backyard chicken laws in Plano, TX

General §4-81 limits adult animals at one-lawful-dwelling property to ten, with separate conditions for other dwelling types; §4-185 does NOT grant unlimited backyard hens or a universal chicken-specific cap. Only hens under §4-185, agricultural uses treated separately.

Flock size

Primary clause recorded

Plano §4-185 permits backyard HENS at property with one lawful dwelling but does not declare an unconditional flock-specific cap. General adult-animal limits §4-81 (usually 10 at a qualifying one-dwelling property) and zoning apply; not an unlimited-hen approval.

Plano Code §§4-81(a), 4-185(a)-(b) · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Bring the measured parcel area and zoning designation. Ask which row or threshold applies; do not substitute the postal city name for jurisdiction.

Roosters

Primary clause recorded

Roosters and non-hen fowl prohibited for ordinary residential backyard keeping under §4-185(a); legitimate farms/ranches are separately regulated.

Plano City Code §4-185(a) · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Confirm the rule for the proposed birds and zone. A hen allowance does not independently authorize roosters.

Permits and approvals

Primary clause recorded

Plano §4-185 requires a paid annual backyard-hen permit BEFORE keeping hens, with coop/site plan, approved training, written notarized property-owner permission for tenants and possible inspection; HOA/building approvals separate.

Plano City Code §4-185(c)-(e),(i) · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Request the keeping/licensing procedure and renewal conditions, then ask separately about the proposed structure, utilities and building approvals.

Coop placement

Primary clause recorded

Plano §4-29 requires coop/animal enclosure >=20 ft from adjacent buildings for fewer than 5 hens, >=50 ft when 5+ hens (excluding keeper-owned buildings), plus >=4 sq ft coop and >=8 sq ft outdoor exercise area per hen; six-foot fenced protection, accessory-structure standards apply.

Plano City Code §§4-29(a)(1)-(5), 4-185(f)(1) · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Mark the legal boundary and the regulated part of the enclosure. Confirm zoning setbacks, easements and any additional structure requirements separately.
