Flock size

Plano §4-185 permits backyard HENS at property with one lawful dwelling but does not declare an unconditional flock-specific cap.

Read the full rule and exceptions

Plano §4-185 permits backyard HENS at property with one lawful dwelling but does not declare an unconditional flock-specific cap. General adult-animal limits §4-81 (usually 10 at a qualifying one-dwelling property) and zoning apply; not an unlimited-hen approval.

Roosters

Roosters and non-hen fowl prohibited for ordinary residential backyard keeping under §4-185(a); legitimate farms/ranches are separately regulated.

Permits and approvals

This field has conditions that need a closer look.

Read the full rule and exceptions

Plano §4-185 requires a paid annual backyard-hen permit BEFORE keeping hens, with coop/site plan, approved training, written notarized property-owner permission for tenants and possible inspection; HOA/building approvals separate.

Coop placement

This field has conditions that need a closer look.

Read the full rule and exceptions

Plano §4-29 requires coop/animal enclosure >=20 ft from adjacent buildings for fewer than 5 hens, >=50 ft when 5+ hens (excluding keeper-owned buildings), plus >=4 sq ft coop and >=8 sq ft outdoor exercise area per hen; six-foot fenced protection, accessory-structure standards apply.
