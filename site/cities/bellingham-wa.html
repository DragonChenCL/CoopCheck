Backyard chicken laws in Bellingham, WA

Flock size

Primary clause recorded

Bellingham Code §7.12.060(A) allows securely confined domestic fowl including chickens subject to other municipal ordinances; that specific provision DOES NOT state a numeric flock cap. This does not establish an unlimited citywide allowance.

Bellingham Code §7.12.060(A), current through Ordinance 2026-08-022 · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Ask which zoning, nuisance and site constraints limit this proposal. A missing universal number is not permission for an unlimited flock.

Permits and approvals

Primary clause recorded

Domestic fowl allowed only securely enclosed, sanitary and ≥50ft from an occupied home/working building. This positive city animal-keeping condition is NOT proof of no separate keeping license or coop construction approval.

Bellingham §7.12.060(A)-(C), amended 2026-08-022 · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Request the keeping/licensing procedure and renewal conditions, then ask separately about the proposed structure, utilities and building approvals.

Coop placement

Primary clause recorded

Animals must be securely enclosed and kept at least 50 feet from any dwelling or other building where people regularly work or stay, under §7.12.060(A). This is a building-distance rule, not a property-line setback.

BMC §7.12.060(A), current through 2026-08-022 · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Label both boundary and building distances separately. Ask which rule applies to the coop, the run or the whole enclosure and how each distance is measured.
