Flock size

Bellingham Code §7.12.060(A) allows securely confined domestic fowl including chickens subject to other municipal ordinances

Read the full rule and exceptions

Bellingham Code §7.12.060(A) allows securely confined domestic fowl including chickens subject to other municipal ordinances; that specific provision DOES NOT state a numeric flock cap. This does not establish an unlimited citywide allowance.

Roosters

Section 7.12.060 does not conclusively establish current rooster restrictions; check applicable noise/nuisance and zoning rules.

Permits and approvals

Domestic fowl allowed only securely enclosed, sanitary and ≥50ft from an occupied home/working building.

Read the full rule and exceptions

Domestic fowl allowed only securely enclosed, sanitary and ≥50ft from an occupied home/working building. This positive city animal-keeping condition is NOT proof of no separate keeping license or coop construction approval.

Coop placement

This field has conditions that need a closer look.

Read the full rule and exceptions

Animals must be securely enclosed and kept at least 50 feet from any dwelling or other building where people regularly work or stay, under §7.12.060(A). This is a building-distance rule, not a property-line setback.
