City Building requires permits for most sheds/garages ≥200 SQ FT or >10ft HIGH; smaller coops may qualify for exemptions (handout discusses ≤200sqft and ≤15ft for CERTAIN structures), but must meet separate zoning/placement rules and other utility permits.
