Common questions

How many chickens can I keep in Seattle?

Seattle allows up to eight domestic fowl on a standard lot.

Are roosters allowed in Seattle?

No. Seattle does not permit roosters under its domestic fowl rules.

How far should a Seattle chicken coop be from neighboring homes?

City guidance says structures housing domestic fowl must be at least 10 feet from residential structures on adjacent lots.

Which Seattle chicken rules apply to your yard?

Seattle generally allows eight domestic fowl. Its longer urban-agriculture guidance also addresses larger qualifying lots and the distance between bird structures and adjacent residential buildings.

Examples: what the numbers mean

  • Eight hens on a standard lot

    This meets the ordinary fowl-count limit, but is not automatic approval of every enclosure location.

  • Nine hens on a normal small lot

    Nine exceeds the ordinary limit; do not assume the larger-lot exception applies.

  • Coop 6 ft from fence but 12 ft from neighboring residence

    The cited animal-structure distance refers to the adjacent residence, not the fence; separately check all applicable yard/building rules.

Before you plan a coop

  1. Verify address jurisdiction and whether any special urban-farm/community-garden use is actually authorized.
  2. Locate adjacent residential buildings and record the shortest distance from the proposed bird structure.
  3. Confirm current zoning, structural rules and any larger-lot exception directly with Seattle SDCI.
