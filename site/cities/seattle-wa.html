Backyard chicken laws in Seattle, WA

Seattle allows up to eight domestic fowl on a standard lot, with more possible on qualifying larger lots. Roosters are not permitted and coop placement depends partly on neighboring residences.

Common questions

How many chickens can I keep in Seattle?

Seattle allows up to eight domestic fowl on a standard lot.

Are roosters allowed in Seattle?

No. Seattle does not permit roosters under its domestic fowl rules.

How far should a Seattle chicken coop be from neighboring homes?

City guidance says structures housing domestic fowl must be at least 10 feet from residential structures on adjacent lots.
