Official sources ↘
Flock size

≤¼ acre: up to 5 ducks or female chickens; >¼ to <1 acre: up to 9; ≥1 to <2 acres: 10 plus one per additional 1,000 sq ft (see exceptions)

Roosters

Roosters, geese, peacocks and similar loud fowl prohibited (OMC §18.04.060(KK)(2)(b))

Permits and approvals

The agricultural-use section does not establish a universal coop/building permit exemption; confirm zoning and structural permits per parcel

Coop placement

Agricultural animal enclosures ≥10 ft from every property line AND outside applicable setback areas (OMC §18.04.060(KK)(4))
