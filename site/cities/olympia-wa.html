Backyard chicken laws in Olympia, WA

Up to five ducks or female chickens at ≤¼ acre; nine at quarter-to-acre; ten plus 1 per 1,000sqft above one acre until two acres. Overlap at exactly one acre requires clarification.

Check the lot-size category before choosing a flock

Olympia's revised animal-keeping rule distinguishes parcels of one-quarter acre or less, with up to five female chickens or ducks, from parcels larger than a quarter-acre and under an acre, with up to nine. The larger-lot categories have their own formulas and exceptions; a simple one-bird-per-acre multiplier would therefore misrepresent the code. Roosters and certain loud fowl are excluded.

Enclosures must remain at least ten feet from every property line and also outside applicable zoning setback areas. Meeting the ten-foot measurement alone may be insufficient. The reviewed animal provision does not resolve whether an accessory building permit is required for a specific coop.
