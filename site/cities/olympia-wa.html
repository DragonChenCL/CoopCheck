Scope and limitations

OMC §18.04.060(KK), added by Ordinance 7457 in January 2026. The listed 10-ft enclosure distance is a minimum and does not override zone-specific setback areas. Exactly one-acre lot wording overlaps adjacent quantity tiers; lots ≥2 acres use USDA Animal Unit rules. Also confirm birds of other types, parcel zoning, structure permits, HOA restrictions and any more recent updates. No automated compliance clearance.

Questions to check before building

How many chickens can I keep here?

≤¼ acre: up to 5 ducks or female chickens; >¼ to <1 acre: up to 9; ≥1 to <2 acres: 10 plus one per additional 1,000 sq ft (see exceptions)

Are roosters allowed?

Roosters, geese, peacocks and similar loud fowl prohibited (OMC §18.04.060(KK)(2)(b))

Do I need a permit?

The agricultural-use section does not establish a universal coop/building permit exemption; confirm zoning and structural permits per parcel

Where can the coop be placed?

Agricultural animal enclosures ≥10 ft from every property line AND outside applicable setback areas (OMC §18.04.060(KK)(4))

Before buying birds or constructing a coop

Confirm that your address is inside the correct city jurisdiction; look up your parcel zoning; confirm flock-size and rooster restrictions, any poultry permit, neighboring-dwelling or property-line separation, accessory-structure/building permits, and HOA or deed restrictions. City rules can change.
