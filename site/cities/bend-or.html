Bend: worked example for a 5,000-square-foot residential lot

Bend Development Code §3.6.300(I)(1)(c) allows no more than four rabbits and/or chickens combined on qualifying residential lots of at least 5,000 square feet; the chicken allowance specifies hens only, no roosters. For example, two hens plus two rabbits use all four of those animal slots; four hens plus a rabbit would exceed the stated count. A parcel below 5,000 square feet cannot rely on this provision as proof of eligibility.

The same section imposes sanitation, rodent-resistant feed-storage and animal-fencing duties. Its 25-foot-from-residences and 15-foot-from-lot-line setback language specifically concerns structures housing large farm animals. Do not copy that rule onto a hen coop without checking the independently applicable accessory-structure provisions. A zoning confirmation and any building/permit questions remain necessary before you use a generated plan.
