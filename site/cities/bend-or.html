Questions to check before building

How many chickens can I keep here?

On eligible residential parcels of 5,000 sq ft or more, no more than FOUR chickens (hens) and/or rabbits combined under Bend Development Code §3.6.300(I); this does not establish allowance on smaller parcels.

Are roosters allowed?

Roosters are not allowed within the cited §3.6.300(I) residential farm-animal accessory use; confirm parcel zoning.

Do I need a permit?

Specific chicken-keeping permit and any separate building/accessory-structure approval are NOT established by the cited section; ask Bend Planning before getting birds or building.

Where can the coop be placed?

No universal chicken-coop lot-line or neighboring-dwelling setback confirmed. The prior chapter's 15/25-ft rules expressly discuss structures for LARGE farm animals and must not be copied to hen coops.

Before buying birds or constructing a coop

Confirm that your address is inside the correct city jurisdiction; look up your parcel zoning; confirm flock-size and rooster restrictions, any poultry permit, neighboring-dwelling or property-line separation, accessory-structure/building permits, and HOA or deed restrictions. City rules can change.
