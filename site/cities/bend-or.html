Flock size

This field has conditions that need a closer look.

Read the full rule and exceptions

On eligible residential parcels of 5,000 sq ft or more, no more than FOUR chickens (hens) and/or rabbits combined under Bend Development Code §3.6.300(I); this does not establish allowance on smaller parcels.

Roosters

Roosters are not allowed within the cited §3.6.300(I) residential farm-animal accessory use; confirm parcel zoning.

Coop placement

No universal chicken-coop lot-line or neighboring-dwelling setback confirmed.

Read the full rule and exceptions

No universal chicken-coop lot-line or neighboring-dwelling setback confirmed. The prior chapter's 15/25-ft rules expressly discuss structures for LARGE farm animals and must not be copied to hen coops.
