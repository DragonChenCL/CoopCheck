Scope and limitations

Current Flagstaff animal keeping Chapter 6-03, amended Ord. 2024-26, sets lot-size flock tiers, a prohibition on male poultry older than four months, conditional livestock permits, and distinct neighboring-lot/dwelling offsets. Do not mistake an ER/RR animal-permit exception for a building approval. Parcel zoning, HOA and applicable Arizona state preemption remain separate questions.

Questions to check before building

How many chickens can I keep here?

Domestic chicken (fowl) caps under §6-03-001-0003(A) Table A: 6 on ≤19,999 sq ft lots; 10 at 20,000–29,999; 15 at 30,000–39,999; 20 at 40,000–79,999; 25 on ≥80,000 sq ft. Lot use/zoning and offspring-age exceptions apply.

Are roosters allowed?

Male poultry over four months old are prohibited under §6-03-001-0003(C)(4). Younger chicks have a narrow age exception, not permanent permission for adult roosters.

Do I need a permit?

Backyard livestock keeping permit required outside Estate Residential (ER) and Rural Residential (RR) zones under §6-03-001-0004(A); coop and fence work can separately trigger minor-improvement permits under §6-03-001-0004(E).

Where can the coop be placed?

Outside ER/RR, livestock containment belongs in the rear yard. Small-livestock shelter: ≥10 ft from neighboring residential lot line (0 ft if abutting alley), ≥20 ft from nearest neighboring dwelling; on lots <1 acre fowl shelter ≤8 ft tall and below fence line (Chapter 6-03 Table C).

Before buying birds or constructing a coop

Confirm that your address is inside the correct city jurisdiction; look up your parcel zoning; confirm flock-size and rooster restrictions, any poultry permit, neighboring-dwelling or property-line separation, accessory-structure/building permits, and HOA or deed restrictions. City rules can change.
