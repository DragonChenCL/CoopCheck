Flock size

Primary clause recorded

Domestic chicken (fowl) caps under §6-03-001-0003(A) Table A: 6 on ≤19,999 sq ft lots; 10 at 20,000–29,999; 15 at 30,000–39,999; 20 at 40,000–79,999; 25 on ≥80,000 sq ft. Lot use/zoning and offspring-age exceptions apply.

§6-03-001-0003(A)(1), Table 6-03-001-0003.A · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Bring the measured parcel area and zoning designation. Ask which row or threshold applies; do not substitute the postal city name for jurisdiction.

Roosters

Primary clause recorded

Male poultry over four months old are prohibited under §6-03-001-0003(C)(4). Younger chicks have a narrow age exception, not permanent permission for adult roosters.

§6-03-001-0003(C)(4) · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Record bird age as well as sex. Ask which age-based restriction applies; do not turn an age threshold into a blanket all-age statement.

Permits and approvals

Primary clause recorded

Backyard livestock keeping permit required outside Estate Residential (ER) and Rural Residential (RR) zones under §6-03-001-0004(A); coop and fence work can separately trigger minor-improvement permits under §6-03-001-0004(E).

§6-03-001-0004(A)(1),(E)(1) · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Separate permission to keep birds from building, zoning and utility approvals. Obtain the applicable procedure for this particular proposal.

Coop placement

Primary clause recorded

Outside ER/RR, livestock containment belongs in the rear yard. Small-livestock shelter: ≥10 ft from neighboring residential lot line (0 ft if abutting alley), ≥20 ft from nearest neighboring dwelling; on lots <1 acre fowl shelter ≤8 ft tall and below fence line (Chapter 6-03 Table C).

§6-03-001-0003(A)(3),(C)(6), Table 6-03-001-0003.C · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Label both boundary and building distances separately. Ask which rule applies to the coop, the run or the whole enclosure and how each distance is measured.
