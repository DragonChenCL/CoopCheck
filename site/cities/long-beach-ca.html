Questions to check before building

How many chickens can I keep here?

Up to 20 fowl under the city's general quantity provision, but residential chicken placement distances and permits vary by flock size

Are roosters allowed?

Crowing fowl prohibited

Do I need a permit?

A residential chicken permit is required for 5 or more live chickens and is renewed annually

Where can the coop be placed?

Residential chickens must be at least 10 ft from adjacent dwellings for 1–4, 35 ft for 5–10, and 50 ft for 11–20; minimum 10 sq ft permeable land per bird
