The fifth chicken changes both permits and dwelling distance

Long Beach provides a useful comparison between four and five chickens. The cited municipal code requires a separation of ten feet from an adjacent person's dwelling for one to four live chickens; for five to ten it rises to 35 feet, and five or more chickens require an animal-care permit. Eleven to twenty birds use a 50-foot dwelling-distance tier. These distances are to buildings, not simply to boundary fences.

The city separately calls for at least ten square feet of permeable outdoor containment per bird. Five chickens therefore imply at least fifty square feet of permeable enclosure. The general twenty-fowl maximum does not override the distance bands, zoning and additional permit requirements.
