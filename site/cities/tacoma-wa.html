Backyard chicken laws in Tacoma, WA

Six adult poultry + six pigeons per parcel normally; up to ten adults with filed written consent of all qualifying nearby residence owners within 50 ft, and +1 poultry per additional 5,000 sq ft beyond 10,000 sq ft; inspect exact lot.

Extra birds require more than a larger coop

Tacoma's ordinary adult-poultry allowance is six per parcel. A higher count toward ten requires specified written consent from qualifying nearby residence owners, not just more coop floor area. A homeowner planning eight hens should establish whether the additional-bird approval exists before buying materials.

Animal enclosures also have to remain behind the main building's front wall, outside required side-yard setbacks and at least twelve feet from certain dwelling or lodging buildings. Structures exceeding 200 square feet trigger the city's building-permit rule. That is not a promise that smaller structures ignore all zoning requirements.
