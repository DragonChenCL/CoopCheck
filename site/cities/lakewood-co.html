Questions to check before building

How many chickens can I keep here?

Up to 6 female chickens/ducks on lots under 6,000 sq ft; larger lots have enclosure-area-based provisions

Are roosters allowed?

Roosters prohibited on lots under 12,500 sq ft; larger lots subject to different zoning regulations

Do I need a permit?

Lakewood currently offers a Small Animal Permit application for poultry/hens where the zoning district allows them. Check whether your specific lot needs this permit; construction and zoning restrictions are independently applicable.

Where can the coop be placed?

Coop not in front yard, subject to zone-specific accessory-structure setbacks; 4 sq ft living space per bird and predator-resistant enclosure

Before buying birds or constructing a coop

Confirm that your address is inside the correct city jurisdiction; look up your parcel zoning; confirm flock-size and rooster restrictions, any poultry permit, neighboring-dwelling or property-line separation, accessory-structure/building permits, and HOA or deed restrictions. City rules can change.
