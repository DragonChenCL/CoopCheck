Flock size

Not fully confirmed

Current Lakewood Article 4 distinguishes Accessory (A) poultry and Limited (L) hens by zoning. Section 17.4.3.1(D)(2)(a) allows up to six hens under the Limited pathway, if the zone permits it. Coop placement

Not fully confirmed

Accessory (A) poultry under operative Article 4 §17.4.3.1(D)(1)(b) requires the coop behind the principal building's front and at least 15 feet from side/rear property lines. Limited (L) use §17.4.3.1(D)(2)(a) instead points to district-specific accessory-structure setbacks in Article 5/Table 17.5.1. Do not transfer the 15-foot rule across use categories; manual property review is still required.

Lakewood: six hens, the enclosure typo and the permit distinction

On November 9, 2026, a Lakewood Planner of the Day confirmed in writing that the currently linked Article 4 is operative and has not been amended following the April referendum. The source-version question is resolved; parcel zoning is not.

For Limited (L) uses under Article 4 §17.4.3.1(D)(2)(a), the code permits up to six hens (no roosters) when the zone allows the use, with at least six square feet of enclosure living space per bird. On November 10, City Planning confirmed that the public PDF's printed 32-square-foot maximum is a typo and the intended number is 36 square feet. The official PDF still prints 32; staff's written correction is not an enacted text amendment. Six hens at six square feet each illustrates 36 square feet, not property approval.

Planning also confirmed a Small Animal Permit is required for Limited (L) use, not Accessory (A) use in the zone district. Accessory poultry under §17.4.3.1(D)(1)(b) has separate requirements: one bird per four square feet of gross coop floor, a four-foot fence, and a coop at least 15 feet from side and rear lot lines. Limited use follows district-specific accessory-structure setbacks in Article 5/Table 17.5.1 instead. Do not transfer a setback or permit rule between the categories.

Confirm the property's jurisdiction, zoning use-table row, enclosure layout, and independent building, HOA or other approvals with Lakewood before constructing. The CoopCheck planner stays manual and cannot clear a parcel.
