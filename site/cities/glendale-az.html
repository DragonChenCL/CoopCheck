Permits and approvals

Primary clause recorded

Glendale requires RESIDENTIAL ZONING CLEARANCE before applicable coop/accessory BUILDING permits. City FAQ requires chicken enclosure side/rear yard, ≥20 ft to NEIGHBOR PROPERTY, ≥10 ft from buildings, ≤200 sqft/8ft. Accessory structures >120 sqft need building permits under FAQ. This does not establish a routine chicken-keeping permit waiver.

Glendale Planning and Zoning FAQ, backyard chickens, Residential Zoning Clearance, Accessory Buildings · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Provide the coop footprint, height and utilities. Ask how the city classifies this structure and which conditions remain even if a building permit is exempt.

Coop placement

Primary clause recorded

Rear or side yard, at least 20 ft from neighboring property; 10 ft from primary and accessory structures; enclosure no more than 200 sq ft and 8 ft high
