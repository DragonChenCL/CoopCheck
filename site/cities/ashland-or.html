Backyard chicken laws in Ashland, OR

Under 5,000 sq ft: up to five hens; above 5,000 sq ft: one hen per 1,000 sq ft up to 20. The FAQ does NOT expressly resolve exactly 5,000 sq ft or zone-specific exceptions.

Permits and approvals

Primary clause recorded

Ashland's City Community Development FAQ explicitly requires chicken coop/run construction to comply with the city's accessory building rules (AMC §18.2.5.040). This does NOT establish a general chicken-keeping license, nor automatically waive or mandate a construction permit for every coop; confirm parcel zoning and proposed structure with City Planning.

Ashland Community Development FAQ, Are chickens allowed?; AMC §18.2.5.040 Accessory Buildings and Structures · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Request the keeping/licensing procedure and renewal conditions, then ask separately about the proposed structure, utilities and building approvals.

Coop placement

Primary clause recorded

Chickens at least 10 ft from adjoining property and at least 20 ft from neighboring dwellings; coop/run design per AMC 18.2.5.040

Ashland Community Development FAQ, Are chickens allowed? (AMC 18.2.5.040 referenced) · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Label both boundary and building distances separately. Ask which rule applies to the coop, the run or the whole enclosure and how each distance is measured.
