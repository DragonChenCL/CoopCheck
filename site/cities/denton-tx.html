Permits and approvals

Primary clause recorded

Denton City Development Services current Accessory Structures permit guidance: detached accessory outbuildings TYPICALLY REQUIRE building permits, but one-story detached sheds/playhouses/SIMILAR USES of <=200 SQ FT without power, water, or HVAC can qualify for building permit EXEMPTION. ZONING setbacks and code compliance still apply even where exempt. A particular chicken coop may or may not qualify as a similar use; city confirmation required. This does NOT settle whether a separate ordinary CHICKEN-KEEPING permit is needed.

Denton Building Safety Accessory Structures (Residential), permit exemptions and zoning note; current 2026 website · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Provide the coop footprint, height and utilities. Ask how the city classifies this structure and which conditions remain even if a building permit is exempt.
