Flock size

Primary clause recorded

Up to 6 domestic fowl in the backyard of a qualifying detached single-family residence under Sierra Vista §90.11(A); not a citywide exemption for other housing types.

2026 S-24 §90.11(A), amended Ord. 2024-009 · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Ask whether the stated count applies to this address and intended birds, including age, species and any zoning exception.

Roosters

Primary clause recorded

Roosters prohibited in the residential domestic-fowl allowance under §90.11(A)(1).

2026 S-24 §90.11(A)(1) · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Confirm the rule for the proposed birds and zone. A hen allowance does not independently authorize roosters.

Permits and approvals

Not fully confirmed

§90.11 does not establish a general livestock-keeping or construction permit exemption; confirm zoning and separate coop/structure approval with Sierra Vista.

No field-level primary citation established

What to confirm for this proposal

Ask the authority for the operative keeping-permit and separate building-approval provision. The recorded evidence does not resolve this field.

Coop placement

Primary clause recorded

A secure rear- or side-yard enclosure at least 20 ft from a neighboring property, ≤200 sq ft, ≤8 ft tall and below the property's fence line, under §90.11(A)(2). The 20-ft reference is to neighboring property, not specifically to a neighboring dwelling.

2026 S-24 §90.11(A)(2) · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Label both boundary and building distances separately. Ask which rule applies to the coop, the run or the whole enclosure and how each distance is measured.
