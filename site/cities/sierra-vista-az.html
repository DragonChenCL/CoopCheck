Scope and limitations

Sierra Vista 2026 S-24 §90.11, amended Ordinance 2024-009, replaced the old 8-fowl/lot-size formula. Three fields are verified directly: up to 6 fowl for detached homes, no roosters, secure side/rear enclosure with 20-ft neighboring-property separation, 200-sq-ft/8-ft maximum and fence-height condition. Building/keeping permits remain uncertain, and parcel zoning, HOA and Arizona statewide rules need review.

Questions to check before building

How many chickens can I keep here?

Up to 6 domestic fowl in the backyard of a qualifying detached single-family residence under Sierra Vista §90.11(A); not a citywide exemption for other housing types.

Are roosters allowed?

Roosters prohibited in the residential domestic-fowl allowance under §90.11(A)(1).

Do I need a permit?

§90.11 does not establish a general livestock-keeping or construction permit exemption; confirm zoning and separate coop/structure approval with Sierra Vista.

Where can the coop be placed?

A secure rear- or side-yard enclosure at least 20 ft from a neighboring property, ≤200 sq ft, ≤8 ft tall and below the property's fence line, under §90.11(A)(2). The 20-ft reference is to neighboring property, not specifically to a neighboring dwelling.
