What this means for a backyard coop

Chandler now allows up to six hens on eligible single-family residential lots under adopted 2024 Ordinance 5113. Roosters remain prohibited. Coops belong in rear or side yards at least 5 feet from property lines, with other zoning requirements still applying.

Verify before acting. Zoning, lot size, accessory-structure rules, neighboring structures and HOA/CC&R restrictions can change the answer for a specific property.
Important 2024 law update: Chandler's older 2023 chicken webpage and even a 2026 homeowner PDF still state a five-chicken cap, but adopted Ordinance 5113 amended §14-33(A) to six. See the adopted amendment exhibit, signed city ordinance, and January 2025 official permit chart. The city's prior coop location guidance remains, but confirm zoning-specific building setbacks and permit triggers separately.

Common questions

How many chickens can I keep in Chandler?

Under Chandler Ordinance 5113, up to six chickens are allowed on qualifying single-family residential lots. Verify lot zoning and any private restrictions.

Are roosters allowed in Chandler?

No. Roosters are prohibited.

How far must a Chandler chicken coop be from the property line?

The outer edge of the coop may not be closer than 5 feet from any property line.
