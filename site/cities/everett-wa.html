Flock size

This field has conditions that need a closer look.

Read the full rule and exceptions

Everett's approved small-livestock facility regulation §2(4)(c) allows up to 6 chickens on lots up to 10,000 sq ft, 8 on 10,000–15,000, 10 on 15,000–35,000, plus 1 per additional 5,000 above 35,000; combined small-livestock cap 12 per 10,000 sq ft and zoning/permit conditions apply. At exactly 10,000 or 15,000 sq ft published tiers overlap; ask city.

Roosters

This field has conditions that need a closer look.

Read the full rule and exceptions

Everett Animal Services' CURRENT facility-license page expressly treats ANY number of roosters as requiring a small-livestock facility license (annual; listed fee $50). This does NOT establish that roosters are permitted on every lot or in every zoning district; confirm with Animal Services/Planning. Do not misread the proposed 2023 rooster ban as enacted code.

Permits and approvals

Small livestock license annually for 6+ domestic fowl or any number of roosters; permit inspection required

Coop placement

Coops and enclosures are not permitted in front yards or street-facing corner-lot sides

Read the full rule and exceptions

Coops and enclosures are not permitted in front yards or street-facing corner-lot sides; enclosed pens >=5 ft from adjoining PROPERTY lines under Everett facilities standards §3(3)-(5), plus any greater setbacks under EMC Title 19. Housing conditions and possible construction permits separate.

What was reviewed — and what is not confirmed?

These field notes reflect the linked sources, but not every rule or exception has been confirmed against the currently effective code. See the evidence audit.

Current Everett City Facility Licenses states any rooster requires small-livestock facility licensing and that 6+ domesticated fowl also trigger it. The operative EMC §6.04.070 through Sept. 2026 does NOT contain the proposed 2023 categorical rooster ban; its earlier clause is Reserved. This supports only a conditional license-trigger statement, not permission to keep a rooster on any residential parcel. Lot-size chicken limits and coop placement remain scoped to Jan 2024 approved facility regulations and zoning caveats; precise 10,000/15,000 sf tier endpoints overlap.
