Animal licensing and a dwelling-distance test both apply

Madison's official chicken-keeping guidance allows up to eight hens on eligible parcels and does not allow roosters. Keeping chickens requires a municipal chicken license and Wisconsin livestock-premises registration, which should not be confused with one another. Confirm the city's renewal and timing rules before birds arrive.

The covered or fenced chicken enclosure must be at least twenty-five feet from a residential structure on an adjoining lot. Measure the distance to that building, not a made-up property-line inset. Even when the flock count fits, separate zoning, private covenants and construction conditions can affect a particular rear-yard plan.
