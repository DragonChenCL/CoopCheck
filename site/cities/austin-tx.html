Can you keep chickens in Austin?

Yes. Austin says keeping livestock such as chickens is allowed in most residential areas, with enclosure-distance restrictions. The exact answer for a specific home can still depend on zoning, lot size, accessory-structure rules, neighboring buildings and private HOA or CC&R restrictions.

Which Austin chicken rules apply to your yard?

Austin's public city guide confirms many homes can keep chickens and describes where enclosures for two or more birds should sit. It does not provide one universal hen cap, rooster decision or permit clearance.

Examples: what the numbers mean

  • Three chickens, coop 18 ft from the neighbor's house

    This is short of the published 30-ft guideline for two or more chickens. Review a different placement.

  • Three chickens, coop 35 ft from the neighboring home

    This screens past that single distance threshold, but it is not proof that flock size, zoning or permits are allowed.

  • Coop 30 ft from the fence but 15 ft from a business

    Austin's source references the adjacent residence or business. Measuring only to a fence may produce the wrong answer.

Before you plan a coop

  1. Confirm the property is inside Austin city limits; check zoning, subdivision restrictions and any HOA conditions.
  2. Locate nearby residential and business structures and draw the coop-to-structure distance.
  3. Ask the city to verify the actual flock cap, rooster rule, permitting and building requirements before buying birds.
