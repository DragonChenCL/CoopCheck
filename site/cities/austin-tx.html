Can you keep chickens in Austin?

Yes. Austin says keeping livestock such as chickens is allowed in most residential areas, with enclosure-distance restrictions. The exact answer for a specific home can still depend on zoning, lot size, accessory-structure rules, neighboring buildings and private HOA or CC&R restrictions.
