Dallas: check the rooster rule before using an old setback

Dallas is an example of why an older ordinance excerpt is not a safe ready-to-build answer. The City of Dallas Animal Services page says the Texas Agriculture Code change made keeping roosters lawful within Dallas in September 2023. The city council later took up another repeal of the local rooster prohibition in August 2026. Neither event, by itself, establishes a blanket maximum for backyard hens, a permit exemption, or an approved coop location for your address.

Do not copy the 20-foot enclosure distance from an old rooster-enforcement provision into a plan for hens. Before choosing a coop footprint, check the operative local code and parcel zoning, ask Animal Services which nuisance and containment rules now apply, and separately confirm accessory-building approvals. A hypothetical yard with four hens cannot be deemed compliant from the rooster announcement alone.
