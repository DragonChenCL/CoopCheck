Scope and limitations

SOURCE UPDATE (October 13, 2026): Dallas Council approved item 26-2375A as Ordinance No. 33485 on August 12. City-ordinance legal notice published August 15 states that the repeal of the former rooster ban takes effect on publication. The enrolled full text and post-amendment codified section 7-7.3 remain unavailable for independent comparison. Older exception-only 20-ft enclosure setback cannot be represented as current law; no parcel zoning or keeping permit is cleared. Manual planning required.

Questions to check before building

Are roosters allowed?

Dallas City Council passed Ordinance No. 33485 on August 12, 2026. The public legal notice dated August 15 states that it repeals the former rooster-keeping prohibition and takes effect on publication. This does NOT establish unconditional rooster permission on every parcel; zoning, nuisance, health and other current rules remain to be checked.

Where can the coop be placed?

Dallas former §7-7.3 exception-rooster enclosure 20-ft property-line rule MAY be superseded by the August 2026 rooster-ban repeal. A currently operative poultry enclosure setback is NOT confirmed. This is NOT a universal hen-coop setback. Obtain the amended text and check lot zoning before building.
