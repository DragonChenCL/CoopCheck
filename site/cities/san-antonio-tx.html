Scope and limitations

San Antonio ACS government guidance confirms eight domestic fowl per residence including at most one rooster without an excess-fowl permit. CURRENT municipal Chapter 5 table of contents identifies §5-107 as animal limits/excess permit and §5-109 as pet shop license. ACS informational page still incorrectly points to §5-109 for fowl totals; cite the governing §5-107 instead. The 24-sq-ft / 6-sq-ft-per-bird enclosure and any increase beyond eight still need parcel/building/zoning confirmation.

Questions to check before building

How many chickens can I keep here?

Up to 8 domestic fowl per residence, including at most 1 rooster, without an excess-animal permit

Are roosters allowed?

One rooster may be kept within the standard 8-fowl limit; larger flocks have additional permit conditions

Do I need a permit?

Excess-animal permit is required to exceed the standard 8-fowl residential limit

Where can the coop be placed?

Standard fowl must be confined to a coop and run of at least 24 sq ft or 6 sq ft per bird, whichever is greater; additional separation rules apply to excess-animal permits

Before buying birds or constructing a coop

Confirm that your address is inside the correct city jurisdiction; look up your parcel zoning; confirm flock-size and rooster restrictions, any poultry permit, neighboring-dwelling or property-line separation, accessory-structure/building permits, and HOA or deed restrictions. City rules can change.
