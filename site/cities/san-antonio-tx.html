Backyard chicken laws in San Antonio, TX

Official city ACS page states up to 8 domestic fowl, with one rooster, plus a separate two-head livestock tier. The website mistakenly names §5-109; current Code index is §5-107.

Flock size

Primary clause recorded

Up to 8 domestic fowl per residence, including at most 1 rooster, without an excess-animal permit

City ACS Permits & Fees > Did You Know?; current City Code contents §5-107 · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Ask whether the stated count applies to this address and intended birds, including age, species and any zoning exception.

Roosters

Primary clause recorded

One rooster may be kept within the standard 8-fowl limit; larger flocks have additional permit conditions

City ACS Permits & Fees > Did You Know?; current Code §5-107 animal limits · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Record bird age as well as sex. Ask which age-based restriction applies; do not turn an age threshold into a blanket all-age statement.

Permits and approvals

Primary clause recorded

Excess-animal permit is required to exceed the standard 8-fowl residential limit

City ACS Permits & Fees > Residential Animal Permits and Livestock Permits · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Separate permission to keep birds from building, zoning and utility approvals. Obtain the applicable procedure for this particular proposal.

Coop placement

Primary clause recorded

Standard coop/run minimum 24 sq ft TOTAL or 6 sq ft PER FOWL, whichever greater. For EXCESS ANIMAL permit >8 birds, at least 50ft from third-party occupied DWELLING/BUSINESS, not lot line; NOT a universal 50ft requirement for ordinary eight or fewer birds.

San Antonio Ch5 §5-107 ordinary fowl/excess permit · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Label both boundary and building distances separately. Ask which rule applies to the coop, the run or the whole enclosure and how each distance is measured.

What was reviewed — and what is not confirmed?

These field notes reflect the linked sources, but not every rule or exception has been confirmed against the currently effective code. See the evidence audit.

San Antonio ACS government guidance confirms eight domestic fowl per residence including at most one rooster without an excess-fowl permit. CURRENT municipal Chapter 5 table of contents identifies §5-107 as animal limits/excess permit and §5-109 as pet shop license. ACS informational page still incorrectly points to §5-109 for fowl totals; cite the governing §5-107 instead. The 24-sq-ft / 6-sq-ft-per-bird enclosure and any increase beyond eight still need parcel/building/zoning confirmation. 2026-10-13: §5-107 placement verified only conditional 24sf/6sf per bird rule and excess animal permit 50ft THIRD-PARTY OCCUPIED building; §5-109 is PET SHOP, not flock-limit authority.
