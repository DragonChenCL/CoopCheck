Questions to check before building

How many chickens can I keep here?

Chicken quantity is based on animal units and lot size: 1 unit per chicken, up to 2 animal units per 1,000 sq ft with tiered maximums

Are roosters allowed?

Male fowl prohibited under the urban-agriculture use standards, subject to specific code exceptions

Do I need a permit?

Verify lot zoning and any required approvals under Tucson Unified Development Code §6.6.5

Where can the coop be placed?

Housing and setbacks depend on the use; Tucson animal code §4-57 generally references a 50-ft neighboring-dwelling separation with zoning exceptions

Before buying birds or constructing a coop

Confirm that your address is inside the correct city jurisdiction; look up your parcel zoning; confirm flock-size and rooster restrictions, any poultry permit, neighboring-dwelling or property-line separation, accessory-structure/building permits, and HOA or deed restrictions. City rules can change.
