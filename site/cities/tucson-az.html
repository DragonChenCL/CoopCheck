Flock size

Primary clause recorded

Chicken quantity is based on animal units and lot size: 1 unit per chicken, up to 2 animal units per 1,000 sq ft with tiered maximums

Tucson UDC §6.6.5(E)(3)(b)-(f), current 2026 text · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

List every animal category counted by the cited rule, not just hens. Ask how the combined total and parcel eligibility apply to your household.

Roosters

Primary clause recorded

Male fowl prohibited under the urban-agriculture use standards, subject to specific code exceptions

Tucson UDC §6.6.5(E)(1)(c) · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Confirm the rule for the proposed birds and zone. A hen allowance does not independently authorize roosters.

Permits and approvals

Primary clause recorded

Tucson requires Residential Addition/Alteration permit APPLICATION and site-plan ZONING REVIEW for residential sheds and SIMILAR structures regardless of size. Detached ≤200 sqft without utilities may avoid structural plans/inspections; with utilities or >200 sqft needs building plans and inspections. A particular chicken coop must be classified by PDS; do not claim exemption from zoning permit.

Tucson PDS Permit Exemptions and 2025 Residential Shed Permitting Guide · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Provide the coop footprint, height and utilities. Ask how the city classifies this structure and which conditions remain even if a building permit is exempt.

Coop placement

Primary clause recorded

Under current UDC §6.6.5(E)(4), coop/shelter stays behind the main building and at least 20 ft from the dwelling on an abutting lot. Shelters <=6 ft tall AND <=16 sq ft have special side/rear line exemption; larger ones follow accessory-structure zoning setbacks. This is not a universal 50-ft rule.

Tucson UDC §6.6.5(E)(4)(a)-(d) · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Ask for a chicken-specific or independently applicable structure rule. Do not borrow a livestock distance or use an unconfirmed buffer as a clearance.
