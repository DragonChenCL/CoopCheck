Questions to check before building

How many chickens can I keep here?

Folsom's Agricultural Combining District (A) permits small livestock farming, with a use permit required for raising more than 100 poultry/animals; this is NOT a general residential allowance.

Are roosters allowed?

Folsom's 2026 Ordinance 1362 §4 amends §7.04.080(C) and expressly states ROOSTERS SHALL NOT BE KEPT IN ANY ZONING DISTRICT. This citywide prohibition applies regardless of separate A combining district poultry-use rules. The amended section also permits up to six chickens as pets in qualifying nonag zones; do not confuse this with §17.40 A-district livestock limits.

Do I need a permit?

A use permit is required for more than 100 head of poultry/animals in an applicable A combining district; other districts require separate review.

Where can the coop be placed?

Within the A combining district, barns/chicken houses and similar accessories must be at least 50 ft from front property line, 20 ft from side line and 30 ft from a dwelling (§17.40.040).
