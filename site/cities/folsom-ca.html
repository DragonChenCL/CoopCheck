Flock size

Primary clause recorded

Folsom 2026 Ordinance 1362 §4 (§7.04.080(C)) permits at most SIX CHICKENS as pets in residential and other nonagricultural/non-A combining zones. In Agricultural Combining District (A), separate §17.40 allows agriculture/livestock farming; its 100-head use-permit trigger is NOT a general six-hen cap. Roosters prohibited in ALL zoning districts.

Folsom 2026 Ordinance 1362 §4 / §7.04.080(C), plus existing A district §17.40.020 · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Bring the measured parcel area and zoning designation. Ask which row or threshold applies; do not substitute the postal city name for jurisdiction.

Roosters

Primary clause recorded

Folsom's 2026 Ordinance 1362 §4 amends §7.04.080(C) and expressly states ROOSTERS SHALL NOT BE KEPT IN ANY ZONING DISTRICT. This citywide prohibition applies regardless of separate A combining district poultry-use rules. The amended section also permits up to six chickens as pets in qualifying nonag zones; do not confuse this with §17.40 A-district livestock limits.

Folsom Ordinance 1362 §4 amending City Code §7.04.080(C), enacted 2026; official council attachment · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Confirm the rule for the proposed birds and zone. A hen allowance does not independently authorize roosters.

Permits and approvals

Primary clause recorded

Folsom 2026 §7.04.080(C) permits up to six PET chickens in eligible nonagricultural zoning, but does NOT itself waive accessory-building approvals. In the Agricultural Combining District (A), §17.40.020/.030 requires a use permit to raise MORE THAN 100 poultry/livestock. Keeping and building licenses depend on zoning and property.

§17.40.020(3); §17.40.030 · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Request the keeping/licensing procedure and renewal conditions, then ask separately about the proposed structure, utilities and building approvals.

Coop placement

Primary clause recorded

Folsom 2026 §7.04.080(C): in NONAGRICULTURAL zones, permitted pet chickens must be kept ≥5 ft from ANY PROPERTY LINE AND ≥20 ft from a NEIGHBORING DWELLING. In Agricultural Combining District (A), separate §17.40.040 provides agricultural chicken-house siting distances (50ft front line, 20ft side line, 30ft dwelling). Do not substitute one zone's distance for another.

Folsom 2026 Ordinance 1362 §4 / §7.04.080(C); A-district §17.40.040 separately · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Label both boundary and building distances separately. Ask which rule applies to the coop, the run or the whole enclosure and how each distance is measured.
