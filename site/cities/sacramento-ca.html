Can you keep chickens in Sacramento?

Yes, but Sacramento requires licensing and a coop permit for residential chickens. The exact answer for a specific home can still depend on zoning, lot size, accessory-structure rules, neighboring buildings and private HOA or CC&R restrictions.

The actual yearly licensing calculation for three hens

Sacramento's current city page lists a maximum of three chickens per residential parcel and requires annual poultry licenses plus a coop permit. Using the published amounts, three $20 chicken licenses and one $47 coop permit add up to $107 in annual city poultry charges. Confirm current fees before applying because older downloadable documents have shown previous prices.

A qualifying coop belongs in the rear yard, between fifteen and forty-two square feet, and at least twenty feet from the nearest neighboring dwelling rather than a blanket twenty feet from every property line. The city also requires a roof, walls, nests and appropriate upkeep. A generic size calculation alone cannot verify this.
