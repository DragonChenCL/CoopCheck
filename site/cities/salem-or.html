Can you keep chickens in Salem?

Yes. Salem allows backyard poultry for personal use subject to quantity, location and nuisance rules. The exact answer for a specific home can still depend on zoning, lot size, accessory-structure rules, neighboring buildings and private HOA or CC&R restrictions.
