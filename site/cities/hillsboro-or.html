Roosters

Primary clause recorded

Hillsboro, OREGON city Code Compliance guidance says resident chickens are HENS ONLY, with a valid Animal Permit. This is not Hillsboro Kansas or Wisconsin.

Hillsboro City Code Compliance > Animal Regulations: Dogs, Cats, Chickens, and Livestock (2026 city guidance) · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Confirm the rule for the proposed birds and zone. A hen allowance does not independently authorize roosters.

Permits and approvals

Primary clause recorded

A valid City of Hillsboro (Oregon) Animal Permit is REQUIRED to keep hens within city limits; site, applicant ownership and planning review apply. Coop construction approvals and HOA rules are separate.

Hillsboro City Code Compliance > Animal Regulations; city Property Development > Animal & Livestock Permits · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Separate permission to keep birds from building, zoning and utility approvals. Obtain the applicable procedure for this particular proposal.

Coop placement

Primary clause recorded

Hillsboro city Code Compliance cites HMC 12.40.104 requiring ACCESSORY BUILDINGS to maintain separation from LOT LINES; the actual setback depends on residential zone and needs Planning verification. This applies separately from the City Animal Permit required to keep hens; no citywide numeric coop setback established.

Hillsboro City Code Compliance Accessory Structures HMC12.40.104 and animal permit conditions · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Label both boundary and building distances separately. Ask which rule applies to the coop, the run or the whole enclosure and how each distance is measured.
