Questions to check before building

How many chickens can I keep here?

Chickens are permitted under the city's animal permit process; this source does not establish a universal flock maximum

Are roosters allowed?

Hillsboro, OREGON city Code Compliance guidance says resident chickens are HENS ONLY, with a valid Animal Permit. This is not Hillsboro Kansas or Wisconsin.

Do I need a permit?

A valid City of Hillsboro (Oregon) Animal Permit is REQUIRED to keep hens within city limits; site, applicant ownership and planning review apply. Coop construction approvals and HOA rules are separate.

Where can the coop be placed?

Hillsboro city Code Compliance cites HMC 12.40.104 requiring ACCESSORY BUILDINGS to maintain separation from LOT LINES; the actual setback depends on residential zone and needs Planning verification. This applies separately from the City Animal Permit required to keep hens; no citywide numeric coop setback established.

Before buying birds or constructing a coop

Confirm that your address is inside the correct city jurisdiction; look up your parcel zoning; confirm flock-size and rooster restrictions, any poultry permit, neighboring-dwelling or property-line separation, accessory-structure/building permits, and HOA or deed restrictions.
