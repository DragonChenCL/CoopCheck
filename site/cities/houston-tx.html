Backyard chicken laws in Houston, TX

Ordinary §6-35 cap up to 30 of one enumerated species and 40 combined fowl/rabbits/guinea pigs on an enclosure of 65 x 125 feet or smaller; larger enclosure ratio adjustments. This does not supersede 100-ft neighboring-building rule or special medically justified seven-hen permit.

Flock size

Primary clause recorded

Houston Code §6-35 sets up to 30 of one listed fowl/animal type and up to 40 combined on a 65×125-ft or smaller enclosure, with larger-enclosure ratio adjustment; the 100-ft nearby-building restriction and §6-39 special exception still apply. Not a blanket residential entitlement.

Houston City Code §§6-35, 6-39 (2014 revision) · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

List every animal category counted by the cited rule, not just hens. Ask how the combined total and parcel eligibility apply to your household.

Roosters

Primary clause recorded

Roosters/male chickens generally prohibited under Houston §6-38, with narrow government, institutional research and education exceptions.

Houston City Code §6-38(a),(f) · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Confirm the rule for the proposed birds and zone. A hen allowance does not independently authorize roosters.

Permits and approvals

Primary clause recorded

Houston §6-39 creates a SPECIAL revocable permit for up to 7 laying hens only with a physician's certification of serious health need for unfertilized eggs, a 100-ft distance and city inspection; this is not a standard backyard chicken permit.

Houston City Code §6-39(a)-(b), specialized medical-need hen permit · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Provide the coop footprint, height and utilities. Ask how the city classifies this structure and which conditions remain even if a building permit is exempt.

Coop placement

Primary clause recorded

Houston §6-31 generally requires fowl enclosures >=100 ft from another person's residence/habitation, church, school or hospital, measured from the enclosure to that building. This is NOT a property-line setback; exceptions and building code apply.

Houston City Code §6-31(a)-(b) · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Label both boundary and building distances separately. Ask which rule applies to the coop, the run or the whole enclosure and how each distance is measured.
