Flock size

This field has conditions that need a closer look.

Read the full rule and exceptions

Only in eligible RL/RM/RH residential zones: Stockton §16.80.060(E) Table 3-14 permits up to 4 hens/ducks per 5,000 sq ft on qualifying parcels (minimum generally 5,000 sq ft, certain existing-lot exceptions). Separate zoning tiers for agricultural/commercial uses.

Roosters

Roosters/cockerels prohibited inside Stockton city limits under §16.80.060(C),(F)(2), subject to narrow exceptions.

Permits and approvals

Stockton allows conditional hobby hens/ducks in RL/RM/RH zoning on eligible ≥5000sqft residential lots (4 per 5000sqft).

Read the full rule and exceptions

Stockton allows conditional hobby hens/ducks in RL/RM/RH zoning on eligible ≥5000sqft residential lots (4 per 5000sqft). Coop subject to accessory STRUCTURE §16.36.035, NOT automatic construction/animal-keeping permit exemption.

Coop placement

Stockton §16.80.060(F): chicken/duck enclosure on side/rear yard, at least 5 ft from property line AND 20 ft from neighboring dwelling

Read the full rule and exceptions

Stockton §16.80.060(F): chicken/duck enclosure on side/rear yard, at least 5 ft from property line AND 20 ft from neighboring dwelling; accessory structure rules and screening apply.
