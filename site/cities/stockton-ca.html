Backyard chicken laws in Stockton, CA

Residential hobby poultry only in RL/RM/RH districts at 4 fowl per 5,000 sq ft with 5,000-sq-ft lot minimum (existing legal small-lot exceptions), NOT citywide cap. Agricultural/commercial tier 150 per acre with zoning restrictions.

Flock size

Primary clause recorded

Only in eligible RL/RM/RH residential zones: Stockton §16.80.060(E) Table 3-14 permits up to 4 hens/ducks per 5,000 sq ft on qualifying parcels (minimum generally 5,000 sq ft, certain existing-lot exceptions). Separate zoning tiers for agricultural/commercial uses.

Stockton Development Code §16.80.060(E)-(F), Table 3-14, 2025 amendment · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Bring the measured parcel area and zoning designation. Ask which row or threshold applies; do not substitute the postal city name for jurisdiction.

Roosters

Primary clause recorded

Roosters/cockerels prohibited inside Stockton city limits under §16.80.060(C),(F)(2), subject to narrow exceptions.

Stockton Development Code §16.80.060(C),(F)(2) · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Confirm the rule for the proposed birds and zone. A hen allowance does not independently authorize roosters.

Permits and approvals

Primary clause recorded

Stockton allows conditional hobby hens/ducks in RL/RM/RH zoning on eligible ≥5000sqft residential lots (4 per 5000sqft). Coop subject to accessory STRUCTURE §16.36.035, NOT automatic construction/animal-keeping permit exemption.

Stockton §16.80.060(E)-(F), Table3-14, Ord2025-06-03-1601 · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Provide the coop footprint, height and utilities. Ask how the city classifies this structure and which conditions remain even if a building permit is exempt.

Coop placement

Primary clause recorded

Stockton §16.80.060(F): chicken/duck enclosure on side/rear yard, at least 5 ft from property line AND 20 ft from neighboring dwelling; accessory structure rules and screening apply.

Stockton Development Code §16.80.060(E) Table 3-14, (F)(1)(d)-(e) · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Label both boundary and building distances separately. Ask which rule applies to the coop, the run or the whole enclosure and how each distance is measured.
