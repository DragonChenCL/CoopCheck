Backyard chicken laws in Mesa, AZ

Mesa allows a total of 10 fowl under the city's combined fowl/rabbit rule on lots under one-half acre. Roosters may be kept only if they do not create a noise disturbance.

Common questions

How many chickens can I keep in Mesa?

Mesa says a total of 10 fowl under its combined rule are allowed on lots under one-half acre.

Are roosters allowed in Mesa?

Roosters may be kept only if they do not create a noise disturbance.

Does Mesa have enclosure distance rules?

Yes. The city directs residents to Mesa City Code 8-6-21 G & H for containment-area distance restrictions.
