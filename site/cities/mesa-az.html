What this means for a backyard coop

Mesa §8-6-21 generally allows up to 10 combined fowl/rabbits on the first half-acre. Roosters are subject to city noise rules. Applicable coop setbacks must be confirmed against Arizona state preemption.

Verify before acting. Zoning, lot size, accessory-structure rules, neighboring structures and HOA/CC&R restrictions can change the answer for a specific property.
Important Arizona preemption conflict: Mesa's municipal code describes a 40-ft open enclosure and 75-ft coop-building separation from another person's existing permanent residence—not from the property line. Arizona Revised Statutes §9-462.12 preempts conflicting municipal regulation for qualifying single-family detached homes of half an acre or less. Do not assume the old Mesa distances apply to every property. Ask city planning to confirm the applicable interpretation. We do not provide an automated Mesa compliance verdict.

Common questions

How many chickens can I keep in Mesa?

Mesa says a total of 10 fowl under its combined rule are allowed on lots under one-half acre.

Are roosters allowed in Mesa?

Roosters may be kept only if they do not create a noise disturbance.

Does Mesa have enclosure distance rules?

Mesa City Code §8-6-21(G),(H) describes 40-ft enclosures and 75-ft buildings from other existing residences, but Arizona §9-462.12 may preempt these requirements for eligible parcels.
