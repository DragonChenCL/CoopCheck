The neighbor's house and the lot line are two different tests

Garland's cited city rule allows up to four qualifying female domestic fowl older than one month and excludes roosters. For a proposed four-hen backyard coop, first mark the nearest neighboring residence and the boundary of any adjoining residentially zoned property, as those are different reference points.

The reviewed provisions use a 30-foot separation from another residence and a separate five-foot buffer for certain pens, runs or cages beside residentially zoned land. Passing five feet from a fence does not prove compliance with the 30-foot house distance. Other building-permit and zoning conditions remain unconfirmed by the poultry section.
