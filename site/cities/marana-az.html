Flock size

Primary clause recorded

Marana 2026 code §17-4-3: GR eligible lots up to SIX hens; qualifying detached houses in MR/RR/NC/VC up to six; ER/NR ONE chicken PER 1,000 sq ft; AG/RA rooster/other-fowl category maximum 40 per acre. No universal six-bird cap across all zones. 2025.002 effective Feb 7, 2025.

Marana §17-4-3 Table 3 Chickens AG/RA, ER/NR, GR, MR/RR, NC/VC; Ord.2025.002 effective 2025-02-07 · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Ask which zoning, nuisance and site constraints limit this proposal. A missing universal number is not permission for an unlimited flock.

Roosters

Primary clause recorded

Marana CURRENT use matrix §17-4-2: ROOSTERS / OTHER FOWL are conditionally allowed in AG and RA but marked X (prohibited) in ER, NR, GR, MR, RR, NC and VC; AG/RA up to 40 animals per acre subject to separation. This does NOT prohibit hens in qualifying other districts.

Marana §17-4-2 use matrix AG/RA roosters and other fowl U; ER/NR/GR/MR/RR/NC/VC X; §17-4-3 AG/RA 40/acre · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Confirm the rule for the proposed birds and zone. A hen allowance does not independently authorize roosters.

Permits and approvals

Primary clause recorded

Marana §17-4-2 distinguishes U (use allowed if Table 3 conditions met) from C (conditional-use permit) and A (accessory). Chickens are listed U across many residential zones, including GR; MR/RR detached residential development may itself require CUP. This establishes zoning CONDITIONALITY, not proof of no chicken keeping or coop-construction permit.

Marana §17-4-2 Table 2 legend U/C/A, permitted chickens conditioned by §17-4-3, amended 2025.002 · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Provide the coop footprint, height and utilities. Ask how the city classifies this structure and which conditions remain even if a building permit is exempt.

Coop placement

Primary clause recorded

Marana current §17-4-3: GR and qualifying detached MR/RR/NC/VC chicken enclosures in side/rear yard ≥20 ft from NEIGHBORING PROPERTY line, ≤200 sq ft, shorter than fence and ≤8 ft. ER/NR follow a DIFFERENT rule: ≥20 ft from OFFSITE DWELLING; ≤16sqft/≤6ft shelters exempt from side/rear lot-line setbacks, larger shelters accessory setbacks. No single citywide offset.

Marana §17-4-3 Table 3 Chickens GR vs ER/NR; §17-6-7(D), Ord.2025.002 · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Ask for a chicken-specific or independently applicable structure rule. Do not borrow a livestock distance or use an unconfirmed buffer as a clearance.
