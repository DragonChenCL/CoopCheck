Roosters

Primary clause recorded

Adult male chickens (roosters) prohibited.

Permits and approvals

Primary clause recorded

No standard chicken license or coop building permit, except if the coop has electric, mechanical or plumbing fixtures.

§6-02-030(B)(1)-(2) · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Request the keeping/licensing procedure and renewal conditions, then ask separately about the proposed structure, utilities and building approvals.

Coop placement

Primary clause recorded

Coop/hutch setback at least 1 ft from property or easement line (whichever is farther from adjacent structures), plus housing and sanitation rules.

§6-02-030(B)(3)-(D) · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Mark the legal boundary and the regulated part of the enclosure. Confirm zoning setbacks, easements and any additional structure requirements separately.

Utility upgrades can change coop permitting

Prescott Valley's reviewed code allows up to ten chickens per eligible residential lot and prohibits adult male chickens. The cited minimum separation for coops and hutches is one foot from the applicable property or easement line. If an easement is inside the apparent fence boundary, measuring from the fence alone may be inadequate.

The local poultry rules describe a routine no-permit path with an important qualification: a coop containing electrical, mechanical or plumbing installations can trigger a building permit. Before turning a simple shelter into a wired, heated or plumbed structure, verify the current permit classification and land survey rather than assuming the smallest numeric setback approves it.
