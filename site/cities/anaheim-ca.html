Backyard chicken laws in Anaheim, CA

Combined density of poultry/birds/rabbits/rodents one per 1800 square feet of lot area with half-up rounding, NOT a separate chicken allowance.

Flock size

Primary clause recorded

Residential noncommercial animal keeping under Anaheim Municipal Code §18.38.030: combined poultry, birds, rabbits and rodents are limited to one animal per 1,800 sq ft of lot area. This is a combined-category density, not a separate hen allowance.

§18.38.030(.050) · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

List every animal category counted by the cited rule, not just hens. Ask how the combined total and parcel eligibility apply to your household.

Roosters

Primary clause recorded

Roosters and peacocks prohibited in residential zones unless specifically permitted by underlying zone.

§18.38.030(.050) · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Confirm the rule for the proposed birds and zone. A hen allowance does not independently authorize roosters.

Permits and approvals

Primary clause recorded

Anaheim BUILDING DIVISION confirms proposed new construction is reviewed under 2025 California Building Standards Code and local ordinances; required building permits depend on specific accessory coop dimensions, usage, site, and plans. Existing Animal Code §8.24 and zoning still apply to the keeping of domestic poultry. City guidance does NOT establish a general hen licence waiver or universal coop permit trigger.

Anaheim City Building Division Permit Processing Information, current 2025 California Building Standards Code cycle · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Request the keeping/licensing procedure and renewal conditions, then ask separately about the proposed structure, utilities and building approvals.

Coop placement

Primary clause recorded

Birds may not be confined within 50 ft of a building used for human habitation other than the owner's, or within 20 ft of any property line, except inside the dwelling. Coops/corrals must satisfy planning confinement methods.

§18.38.030(.0201)-(.0202) · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Label both boundary and building distances separately. Ask which rule applies to the coop, the run or the whole enclosure and how each distance is measured.
