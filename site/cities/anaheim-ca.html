Questions to check before building

How many chickens can I keep here?

Residential noncommercial animal keeping under Anaheim Municipal Code §18.38.030: combined poultry, birds, rabbits and rodents are limited to one animal per 1,800 sq ft of lot area. This is a combined-category density, not a separate hen allowance.

Are roosters allowed?

Roosters and peacocks prohibited in residential zones unless specifically permitted by underlying zone.

Do I need a permit?

Standard domestic noncommercial keeping is permitted subject to zoning and Chapter 8.24; separate building/animal approvals may be needed for certain circumstances.

Where can the coop be placed?

Birds may not be confined within 50 ft of a building used for human habitation other than the owner's, or within 20 ft of any property line, except inside the dwelling. Coops/corrals must satisfy planning confinement methods.

Before buying birds or constructing a coop

Confirm that your address is inside the correct city jurisdiction; look up your parcel zoning; confirm flock-size and rooster restrictions, any poultry permit, neighboring-dwelling or property-line separation, accessory-structure/building permits, and HOA or deed restrictions.
