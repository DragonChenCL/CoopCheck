Can you keep chickens in Eugene?

Yes. Eugene allows chickens in many residential and mixed-use zones, with lot-size and enclosure standards. The exact answer for a specific home can still depend on zoning, lot size, accessory-structure rules, neighboring buildings and private HOA or CC&R restrictions.

Eugene chicken coop rules at a glance

Maximum / quantity: On lots under 20,000 sq ft: up to 6 adult + 6 under 6 months.

Roosters: Prohibited.

Permit note: No license; building permit generally not required for enclosure ≤200 sq ft and ≤10 ft high.

Placement: Animal shelters and covered runs at least 10 ft from all property lines unless adjacent owner authorizes closer placement.
