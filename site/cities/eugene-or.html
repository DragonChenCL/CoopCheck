Permits and approvals

Primary clause recorded

No license; building permit generally not required for enclosure ≤200 sq ft and ≤10 ft high

City Urban Animal Keeping p.2 (2021 guidance) · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Coop placement

Primary clause recorded

Animal shelters and covered runs at least 10 ft from all property lines unless adjacent owner authorizes closer placement

City Urban Animal Keeping p.2; EC §9.5250(7) · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Mark the legal boundary and the regulated part of the enclosure. Confirm zoning setbacks, easements and any additional structure requirements separately.

Can you keep chickens in Eugene?

Yes. Eugene allows chickens in many residential and mixed-use zones, with lot-size and enclosure standards. The exact answer for a specific home can still depend on zoning, lot size, accessory-structure rules, neighboring buildings and private HOA or CC&R restrictions.
