Backyard chicken laws in Westminster, CO

Single-family detached lots: <=1/4 acre 6 hens; >1/4-1/2 12; >1/2-3/4 18; >3/4 to <2 24; >=2 acres 30. Current government webpage newer than archival permit-form tiers; reconfirm if applying.

A quarter-acre flock tier also changes the coop area

Westminster's official animal-management rules allow up to six hens on detached-home lots of one-quarter acre or less, with higher flock tiers available for larger qualifying parcels. Its coop rule requires at least six square feet of indoor floor area per hen. A six-hen layout therefore needs at least 36 square feet; twelve hens require at least 72 under the same rule, before checking the permitted flock tier.

The city requires an approved backyard-livestock permit, currently listed at $25. Coop placement is limited to a screened rear yard at least ten feet from side and rear property lines, and other size and height limits apply. A large lot alone does not waive the permit or site-layout checks.
