Backyard chicken laws in Santa Rosa, CA

Residential hens in addition to total 5 other allowable domestic/exotic animals: <=5000 sq ft 3 hens; 5001-10000 6; 10001-43560 6 per 10k up to 12; >1 acre 6 per 10k up to 30; site zone applies.

Flock size

Primary clause recorded

Residential hen limit under Table 4-1: up to 3 on lots <=5,000 sq ft, 6 on 5,001–10,000, up to 12 on 10,001–43,560 (6 per 10,000 sq ft), and up to 30 on lots >1 acre (6 per 10,000 sq ft). Different rules apply to qualifying livestock-farming uses.

Santa Rosa Zoning Code §20-42.040(B),(C) Table 4-1 · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Bring the measured parcel area and zoning designation. Ask which row or threshold applies; do not substitute the postal city name for jurisdiction.

Roosters

Primary clause recorded

Roosters prohibited

Santa Rosa Zoning Code §20-42.040(C)(1) · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Confirm the rule for the proposed birds and zone. A hen allowance does not independently authorize roosters.

Permits and approvals

Primary clause recorded

Hen keeping is accessory zoning use conditional on lot-size bird tiers and rear-yard coop 5/10ft PROPERTY buffers plus 20ft neighbor HABITABLE structure. Not proof of unconditional exemption from coop construction permit.

Santa Rosa §20-42.040(B)-(C), Table4-1 · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Provide the coop footprint, height and utilities. Ask how the city classifies this structure and which conditions remain even if a building permit is exempt.

Coop placement

Primary clause recorded

Rear-yard securely fenced coop+pen. <=6 hens: >=5 ft from side/rear property lines; >6 hens: >=10 ft. In all cases >=20 ft from habitable structures on adjacent parcels (§20-42.040(C)).

Santa Rosa Zoning Code §20-42.040(C)(2)-(3) · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Label both boundary and building distances separately. Ask which rule applies to the coop, the run or the whole enclosure and how each distance is measured.
