Backyard chicken laws in Modesto, CA

Residential R-1,R-2,R-3 allowed TOTAL of 12 domestic fowl (hens ONLY), rabbits/hares or similar pets per DWELLING combined. This is not 12 hens plus 12 rabbits.

Flock size

Primary clause recorded

Modesto §10-3.202(a)(3) authorizes MAX TWELVE combined domestic fowl (HENS ONLY), rabbits, hares and customary household pets per dwelling unit in R-1/R-2/R-3 zones. Not a universal 12-chicken permission in other zones.

Modesto 2026 zoning §10-3.202(a)(3); Table 3.1-1 · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

List every animal category counted by the cited rule, not just hens. Ask how the combined total and parcel eligibility apply to your household.

Roosters

Primary clause recorded

Modesto's ordinary §10-3.202 residential domestic-fowl allowance explicitly specifies HENS ONLY, so roosters are NOT included in the R-1/R-2/R-3 dwelling entitlement. Agricultural or other zoning rights are not decided by this section.

Modesto Code §10-3.202(a)(3), residential fowl HENS ONLY · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Confirm the rule for the proposed birds and zone. A hen allowance does not independently authorize roosters.

Permits and approvals

Primary clause recorded

Modesto §10-3.202(b)(2) requires a CONDITIONAL USE PERMIT for ADDITIONAL NUMBERS beyond the standard combined twelve animals in residential zones. §10-3.202(e) preserves other licensing and housing regulations; routine coop building permit is NOT waived.

Modesto Code §10-3.202(b)(2), (e); Table 3.1-1 · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Request the keeping/licensing procedure and renewal conditions, then ask separately about the proposed structure, utilities and building approvals.

Coop placement

Primary clause recorded

Modesto §10-3.202(c) requires chicken COOP ≥40 ft from a WINDOW OR DOOR of any HABITABLE BUILDING. §10-3.202(d) also requires coop-to-PROPERTY LINE distance at least the applicable zoning SIDE-YARD SETBACK. These are different measurements; animal containment and zoning still apply.

Modesto Code §10-3.202(c)-(d), duplicated §10-7.407(a)-(b) · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Label both boundary and building distances separately. Ask which rule applies to the coop, the run or the whole enclosure and how each distance is measured.
