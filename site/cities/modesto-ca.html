Flock size

This field has conditions that need a closer look.

Read the full rule and exceptions

Modesto §10-3.202(a)(3) authorizes MAX TWELVE combined domestic fowl (HENS ONLY), rabbits, hares and customary household pets per dwelling unit in R-1/R-2/R-3 zones. Not a universal 12-chicken permission in other zones.

Roosters

This field has conditions that need a closer look.

Read the full rule and exceptions

Modesto's ordinary §10-3.202 residential domestic-fowl allowance explicitly specifies HENS ONLY, so roosters are NOT included in the R-1/R-2/R-3 dwelling entitlement. Agricultural or other zoning rights are not decided by this section.

Permits and approvals

This field has conditions that need a closer look.

Read the full rule and exceptions

Modesto §10-3.202(b)(2) requires a CONDITIONAL USE PERMIT for ADDITIONAL NUMBERS beyond the standard combined twelve animals in residential zones. §10-3.202(e) preserves other licensing and housing regulations; routine coop building permit is NOT waived.

Coop placement

Modesto §10-3.202(c) requires chicken COOP ≥40 ft from a WINDOW OR DOOR of any HABITABLE BUILDING.

Read the full rule and exceptions

Modesto §10-3.202(c) requires chicken COOP ≥40 ft from a WINDOW OR DOOR of any HABITABLE BUILDING. §10-3.202(d) also requires coop-to-PROPERTY LINE distance at least the applicable zoning SIDE-YARD SETBACK. These are different measurements; animal containment and zoning still apply.
