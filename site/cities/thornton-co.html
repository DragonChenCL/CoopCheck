Thornton's indoor six-square-foot rule differs from generic sizing

Thornton's official FAQ allows up to six hens at eligible detached single-family homes and requires at least six square feet of indoor coop floor for each hen. Six birds therefore need at least 36 square feet of usable coop floor. CoopCheck's generic planning rule of four square feet per bird would give 24 and must not be used as proof of local compliance.

A Thornton plan also needs a fenced backyard, one coop, a maximum coop area of 120 square feet and height of seven feet, and five-foot separation from side and rear property lines. The city says no standard chicken-keeping permit is required, but an attached townhouse does not meet the detached-home condition and other construction or HOA restrictions remain separate.
