Questions to check before building

How many chickens can I keep here?

The reviewed Corvallis Municipal Code §5.03.050.020.06(7) concerns registration for livestock and exempts domesticated fowl kept in pens/cages; it does not establish a numerical chicken allowance.

Are roosters allowed?

No general rooster rule established by this livestock-registration provision.

Do I need a permit?

Corvallis §5.03.050.020.06(7)(a) EXPRESSLY EXEMPTS domesticated fowl kept in pens/cages from the specific LIVESTOCK REGISTRATION obligation imposed on other livestock. This does NOT establish exemption from zoning review, animal nuisance rules or a construction/building permit for an actual coop.

Where can the coop be placed?

No chicken-coop setback established by the cited animal registration section.
