Read the full rule and exceptions

Corvallis §5.03.050.020.06(7)(a) EXPRESSLY EXEMPTS domesticated fowl kept in pens/cages from the specific LIVESTOCK REGISTRATION obligation imposed on other livestock. This does NOT establish exemption from zoning review, animal nuisance rules or a construction/building permit for an actual coop.
