Questions to check before building

How many chickens can I keep here?

No universal personal-use farm-animal limit specified on Scottsdale's city guidance for single- and two-family properties

Are roosters allowed?

Animals causing habitual crowing or other neighborhood noise disturbance are prohibited; no categorical citywide rooster ban established by this source

Do I need a permit?

Scottsdale City 2026 guidance: a qualifying NONLIVABLE detached accessory structure under 200 sq ft generally needs NO building permit, BUT setback conformity and Planning Services One Stop Shop PLAN APPROVAL remain mandatory. Whether a specific coop is such a structure, and other animal licences, require City review.

Where can the coop be placed?

Scottsdale's current City accessory-building guidance imposes DISTRICT-SPECIFIC setbacks on detached shelters; Foothills Overlay setbacks depend on LOT AREA, zone and main-house floor area. The City's farm guidance gives no universal poultry-coop offset. Thus no numeric citywide chicken-coop setback is proven; determine each parcel and structure type.

Before buying birds or constructing a coop

Confirm that your address is inside the correct city jurisdiction; look up your parcel zoning; confirm flock-size and rooster restrictions, any poultry permit, neighboring-dwelling or property-line separation, accessory-structure/building permits, and HOA or deed restrictions.
