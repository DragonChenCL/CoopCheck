Backyard chicken laws in Scottsdale, AZ

City guidance generally does not limit number of personally kept farm animals on approved single/two-family residential properties, but other Maricopa County/HOA and property-use rules still apply; not a universal hen allowance.

Permits and approvals

Primary clause recorded

Scottsdale City 2026 guidance: a qualifying NONLIVABLE detached accessory structure under 200 sq ft generally needs NO building permit, BUT setback conformity and Planning Services One Stop Shop PLAN APPROVAL remain mandatory. Whether a specific coop is such a structure, and other animal licences, require City review.

Scottsdale City Government Sheds/Garages/Pergolas Permitting Requirements, last updated 2026-05-21 · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Request the keeping/licensing procedure and renewal conditions, then ask separately about the proposed structure, utilities and building approvals.

Coop placement

Primary clause recorded

Scottsdale's current City accessory-building guidance imposes DISTRICT-SPECIFIC setbacks on detached shelters; Foothills Overlay setbacks depend on LOT AREA, zone and main-house floor area. The City's farm guidance gives no universal poultry-coop offset. Thus no numeric citywide chicken-coop setback is proven; determine each parcel and structure type.

Scottsdale City Government Sheds/Garages/Pergolas: accessory structure setbacks and Foothills Overlay, updated 2026-05-21 · recorded 2026-10-13
