Backyard chicken laws in Elk Grove, CA

In RD zone standard six chickens housed in clean coop/cage >=5 ft from internal side/rear lines; up to twelve if 40-ft buffer between chicken area and ANY neighboring property line, with barrier to maintain buffer.

Flock size

Primary clause recorded

RD-zone residential lots allow up to 6 chickens with a clean coop/cage; up to 12 only when a 40-ft buffer from the chicken area to every neighboring property line is maintained (§23.27, residential note 3).

EGMC Chapter 23.27 Table 23.27-1 residential note 3(a)(i)-(ii) · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Confirm the ordinary allowance separately from any consent-based exception; ask whose written consent is needed and retain it with the proposal.

Roosters

Primary clause recorded

Roosters prohibited in RD residential districts under EGMC Chapter 23.27 residential note 3(a)(iii); other zones must be checked.

EGMC Chapter 23.27 residential note 3(a)(iii) · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Confirm the rule for the proposed birds and zone. A hen allowance does not independently authorize roosters.

Permits and approvals

Primary clause recorded

RD zoning permits keeping <=6 hens as accessory use at 5ft from lot lines, up to12 only with 40ft lot-line BUFFER; roosters banned. Zoning-by-right status NOT a waiver of coop building permit.

Elk Grove §23.27 Residential Note3(a)(i)-(iii) · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Provide the coop footprint, height and utilities. Ask how the city classifies this structure and which conditions remain even if a building permit is exempt.

Coop placement

Primary clause recorded

For standard RD-zoned 6-chicken use, clean coop/cage >=5 ft from interior side and rear lines. For up to 12 chickens a barrier must maintain >=40 ft from the chicken area to every neighboring property line; specific site/zoning also apply.

EGMC Chapter 23.27 residential note 3(a)(i)-(ii) · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Mark the legal boundary and the regulated part of the enclosure. Confirm zoning setbacks, easements and any additional structure requirements separately.
