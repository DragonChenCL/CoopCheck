Questions to check before building

How many chickens can I keep here?

Bakersfield CURRENT §17.10.020 Table 17.10-1 permits animal raising/breeding in R-S (and R-H) but NOT ordinary R-1 through R-6. §17.10.040(C)(1)(a) authorizes domestic poultry in R-S without giving a NUMERIC chicken cap in that subsection; NOT an unlimited-flock authorization or general agricultural cap.

Are roosters allowed?

Bakersfield §6.08.010 covers chickens and other FOWL irrespective of sex, requiring secured confinement on agricultural or R-S parcels; §17.10.040(C)(1)(a) gives no rooster-specific ban or allowance. This is a NARROW NEGATIVE finding only: it does NOT prove every property may keep a crowing rooster.

Do I need a permit?

Bakersfield §17.10.020 Table 17.10-1 marks animal raising P (by RIGHT, subject to ordinance) in R-S/R-H but N in R-1–R-6. §17.10.040(C)(3) authorizes TEMPORARY six-month FFA/4-H educational-project animal increase only after planning-director approval; not a routine residential coop-construction permit waiver.

Where can the coop be placed?

Bakersfield R-S zone §17.10.040(C)(2): poultry coop ≥50 ft from ANY HUMAN HABITATION DWELLING, ≥100 ft from own FRONT LOT LINE, ≥10 ft from STREET SIDE of a CORNER LOT, and ≥100 ft from park/school/hospital. §6.08.010 requires securely confined birds. Agricultural zoning has separate setbacks.
