Flock size

The current Tigard Livestock Article VII, §§6.02.610–630, specifies NO universal numerical chicken/flock ceiling.

Read the full rule and exceptions

The current Tigard Livestock Article VII, §§6.02.610–630, specifies NO universal numerical chicken/flock ceiling. City Code Compliance says ownership of backyard livestock/poultry is not itself regulated as a numerical limit; rooster age, coop setbacks, nighttime enclosure, sanitation/nuisance and zoning standards still apply. This is NOT an unlimited-flock approval.

Roosters

Roosters older than 3 months prohibited

Permits and approvals

This field has conditions that need a closer look.

Read the full rule and exceptions

Tigard states a detached single-story NONHABITABLE standard accessory structure ≤200 SQ FT and ≤15ft roof-height does NOT need a building permit, subject to structural/use conditions. Larger or taller structures may require one. Front setbacks and maximum accessory area apply EVEN IF PERMIT EXEMPT; a specific chicken coop and any poultry-keeping license require city confirmation.

Coop placement

Coops at least 5 ft from all property lines (fenced runs exempt); animals must be enclosed at night

What was reviewed — and what is not confirmed?

These field notes reflect the linked sources, but not every rule or exception has been confirmed against the currently effective code. See the evidence audit.

Current codified Tigard Chapter 6.02 Article VII (Ord. 22-09) and City Code Compliance describe no universal numeric hen limit in the livestock-keeping article, while prohibiting roosters over 3 months old and requiring >=5-ft shelter lot-line/public-access-easement offsets, shelters at night, and nuisance controls. Check zone, lot coverage and separate construction approvals; no all-clear on unusually large flocks. 2026-10-13: Official city rule mapping for permit.
