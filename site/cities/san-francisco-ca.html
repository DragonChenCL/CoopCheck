Backyard chicken laws in San Francisco, CA

Residential district total is at most FOUR animals across enumerated species, including adult dogs, cats, and chickens; not a separate four-hen allotment.

Flock size

Primary clause recorded

Residential premises may keep no more than FOUR animals IN TOTAL across the species listed in San Francisco Health Code §37(a); chickens share that combined cap with named other birds/mammals, so this is not a separate four-hen allowance.

SF Health Code §37(a) · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

List every animal category counted by the cited rule, not just hens. Ask how the combined total and parcel eligibility apply to your household.

Permits and approvals

Primary clause recorded

Health Code §37(b) requires approved coops/enclosures for the covered animals. Separate zoning/building/health approvals may apply.

SF Health Code §37(b) · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Separate permission to keep birds from building, zoning and utility approvals. Obtain the applicable procedure for this particular proposal.

Coop placement

Primary clause recorded

An outdoor coop or enclosure must be at least 20 ft from any door or window of a building used for human habitation.

SF Health Code §37(b), second sentence · recorded 2026-10-13

What to confirm for this proposal

Mark the legal boundary and the regulated part of the enclosure. Confirm zoning setbacks, easements and any additional structure requirements separately.
