Questions to check before building

How many chickens can I keep here?

Residential premises may keep no more than FOUR animals IN TOTAL across the species listed in San Francisco Health Code §37(a); chickens share that combined cap with named other birds/mammals, so this is not a separate four-hen allowance.

Are roosters allowed?

The reviewed Health Code §37 does not establish a categorical rooster-specific ban; verify noise and other animal/zoning rules.

Do I need a permit?

Health Code §37(b) requires approved coops/enclosures for the covered animals. Separate zoning/building/health approvals may apply.

Where can the coop be placed?

An outdoor coop or enclosure must be at least 20 ft from any door or window of a building used for human habitation.

Before buying birds or constructing a coop

Confirm that your address is inside the correct city jurisdiction; look up your parcel zoning; confirm flock-size and rooster restrictions, any poultry permit, neighboring-dwelling or property-line separation, accessory-structure/building permits, and HOA or deed restrictions. City rules can change.
