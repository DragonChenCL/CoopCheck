Six hens or eight depends on actual parcel size

Aurora's reviewed program normally allows up to six residential hens; a qualifying single-family residential lot of at least 20,000 square feet may keep up to eight. A 19,000-square-foot parcel cannot simply select eight hens because the coop is large. The city requires its chicken or duck permit before acquisition and prohibits residential roosters.

Housing must generally remain in the rear yard with at least fifteen feet of separation from abutting property boundaries unless the specific affected owners provide the written consent contemplated by the rule. That possibility does not waive other zoning restrictions. Draw the true lot lines and consent requirements before arranging the coop footprint.
