Chicken rules at a glance

Short reading summaries only. Expand longer statements for the complete reviewed wording.

Official sources ↘
Flock size

Up to 6 hens on most residential properties; up to 8 hens on single-family lots of 20,000 sq ft or more

Roosters

Roosters prohibited on residential property

Permits and approvals

City chicken/duck permit required before acquiring birds; city's published fee is a one-time $40

Coop placement

Rear-yard chicken housing; structure at least 15 ft from abutting property lines unless affected owners consent in writing
