Backyard chicken laws in Beaverton, OR

At most four hens per eligible property. Young chicks <=12 weeks may be kept indoors as pets; §5.09.020 alone does not establish lot-size or dwelling-type eligibility.

Lot size and tenant consent are separate requirements

Beaverton's cited law permits up to four hens on qualifying properties of at least 5,000 square feet containing a detached single-family home or duplex. For a renter on a qualifying 6,000-square-foot parcel, written permission from the property owner is still necessary; the sufficient lot size does not automatically replace that consent.

A coop cannot occupy the prohibited front-of-house area and has to satisfy the city's published distance from neighboring dwellings. That twenty-foot separation should not be confused with a universal twenty-foot setback from every fence. Confirm accessory-structure permits, the actual neighbor-building location and private HOA conditions separately.
