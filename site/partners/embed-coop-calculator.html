What this tool does and does not check

  • Uses the Oregon State University Extension planning guidance for a single indoor-area comparison, with separate scenarios for routine outdoor access.
  • Accepts clear length and width in feet or inches and a flock of 1–24 adult hens.
  • Does not confirm legal flock size, building permission, ventilation, predator protection, run suitability, structural safety or total welfare.
  • Does not load the CoopCheck GA4 analytics script inside the embedded iframe; no signup, payments, cookies or user measurements are sent by the widget.
  • Uses CoopCheck's same measurement and benchmark functions as the standalone independent buyer checker; editorial judgment remains with each publisher.

Editorial use: You may embed this free widget with its visible CoopCheck source label intact; do not remove its disclaimers or represent its result as a certification. Please link to the original university source when writing about numerical recommendations. For high-traffic or custom placements, contact us first.
