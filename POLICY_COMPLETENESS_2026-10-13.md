## Rules for reviewers

- Check the currently effective government code or regulation section, not just the URL or a search snippet.
- Record the exact claim, section, inspection date, jurisdiction and conditions before changing a field to `primary_clause_checked`.
- Cross-check recent uncodified amendments, Arizona state preemption, lot size, zoning and written-neighbor-consent exceptions.
- Draft/markup PDFs do not prove enacted law.
- Pending municipalities remain `noindex` and outside sitemap and planner policy choices.

## 2026-10-13 Mesa municipal code vs. Arizona preemption
